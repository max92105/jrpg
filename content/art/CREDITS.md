# Art credits

Every file under `content/art/` names its source in `art-sources.json` (ADR 0038); this file is written by hand, one
section per source, and `ArtSourcesTests` checks that every source is here. A bought pack's license text stays outside
the repository, at the source's page.

## Lato

[Lato](https://www.latofonts.com/) by tyPoland Lukasz Dziedzic, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`).

## Cormorant Garamond

[Cormorant Garamond](https://github.com/CatharsisFonts/Cormorant) by the Cormorant Project Authors (Christian Thalmann),
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/titles/OFL.txt`).

## Mana Seed

[Mana Seed](https://seliel-the-shaper.itch.io/) by Seliel the Shaper, bought under the
[Mana Seed User License](https://selieltheshaper.weebly.com/user-license.html): one product per purchase, no
redistribution, credit not required. The Forest Autumn sheets (`tilesheets/autumn ...`) and Half-Timber Home's exteriors
(`tilesheets/home exteriors, half-timber v1.png`), and the bonus shadows, bonus wooden deck and collision & alpha
sheets (`tilesheets/bonus ...`, `tilesheets/collision & alpha.png`), used unmodified.
