# B7b1. Victory layout

Part of [the battle plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md). Split from B7b by the developer (2026-10-21): the layout here, the entrance timing in [B7b2](B7b2.md).

- **B7b1. Victory layout** (Game, about 300 lines)
  - **Goal:** the legacy `VictoryScreen` layout, leaning toward `Victory_Menu.jpg`, drawn in its final state:
    - a left-dark shade over the battle (dimmed, no blur; decision 52), with the HUD hidden;
    - "Victory!" with a hairline;
    - one pointed reward bar per reward row (only "EXP 120" now; gold and drops add rows later);
    - two columns of member cards with pointed name tabs, "Lv", "EXP n / m" and orange bars, filling as in B7a;
    - "Level Up!" on a card once its bar has filled a level.
  - **New types (Game):**
    - `Battle/VictoryScreenView`: draws the screen; its reward rows are a list built from `VictoryRewards`.
    - `Battle/PointedBarShape` (pure masks: legacy `Shape.RewardBar`, `Shape.CardHeader`).
  - **Tests:** `PointedBarShape_RewardBar_PointedEnd_IsClearAtCorners`, `PointedBarShape_CardHeader_BothEndsPointed`, `VictoryScreenView_RewardRows_ExperienceOnly_IsOneRow`.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Compare with `Victory_Menu.jpg`: layout, shade, cards.
- **Done, with deviations:** about 370 lines added and 100 removed (B7a's plain draw moved out of `VictoryScreen`, now pure). `PointedBarShape` is in `Ui/`, not `Battle/`, so `UiTextures` can bake it without `Ui` depending on `Battle` (as `CommandHeaderShape`). The shade is the menu's left-dark scrim (`UiTheme.MenuScrim`). Reward rows are `VictoryRewardRow`s; "EXP" reuses `status_exp`. The cards show name, "Lv." and level, "EXP n/m" and a flat gold bar (legacy `Brush.Bar.EXP`); legacy's "Next" row under the bar is left out, as the slice doesn't list it. A card's numbers are formatted when its bar moves, so once per frame while bars fill. The footer bar is drawn without its hint (B7b2). The reward bar uses a new fill-only `OutlinedShape.Filled` (review). The `{amount} EXP` message from B7a is replaced by `battle_level_label` ("Lv.").
