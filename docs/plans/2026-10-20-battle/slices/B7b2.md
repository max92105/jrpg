# B7b2. Victory timing

Part of [the battle plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md). Split from B7b by the developer (2026-10-21): the layout is [B7b1](B7b1.md).

- **B7b2. Victory timing** (Game, about 180 lines)
  - **Goal:** the victory screen comes in as legacy `VictoryScreen` did (design tunables, "victory"):
    - "Victory!" slides 60 px in over 0.45 s;
    - the reward bar comes after 0.25 s, the first card after 0.45 s, the next ones 0.1 s apart, then a 0.5 s pause before the bars fill;
    - "Level Up!" pops to 1.5x over 0.35 s;
    - the footer's "Next" hint shows once everything is done.
    Confirm still skips to the end.
  - **New types (Game):** `Battle/VictoryEntrance` (pure): each element's opacity and offset over time, `Skip()`, `IsComplete`.
  - **Tests:** `VictoryEntrance_Update_BeforeFirstCard_CardHidden`, `VictoryEntrance_Update_PastPause_BarsStartFilling`, `VictoryEntrance_Skip_ShowsEverything`, `VictoryScreen_Update_Done_ShowsNextHint`.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Compare with `Victory_Menu.jpg`: order, timing, Level Up pop.
- **Done, with deviations:** about 245 lines. `VictoryEntrance` follows legacy: title fade and slide over 0.45 s (cubic ease-out), reward bar fading in over 0.3 s after 0.25 s, cards fading and sliding 30 px over 0.3 s from 0.45 s, 0.1 s apart. `VictoryScreen` gives the bars only the time past the entrance. "Level Up!" pops from 1.5x down to its size over 0.35 s (`LevelUpPop`, cubic ease-out, not legacy's BackEase overshoot), timed by the bar's new `SinceLastLevelUp`; a skip shows it without the pop. The level number doesn't pop (legacy's `LevelPop`), as the slice doesn't list it. "Next" appears in the footer once done, without legacy's key badge or fade. Extra tests: `VictoryEntrance_Update_HalfwayThroughSecondCardFade_IsHalfVisible`, `LevelUpPop` (2), `ExperienceBarAnimation` pop timing (2), `VictoryScreen_Update_DuringEntrance_BarsWait`.
