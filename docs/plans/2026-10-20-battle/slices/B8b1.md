# B8b1. Defeat choices

Part of [the battle plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md). Split from B8b by the developer (2026-10-21): the choices here with a plain draw, the look in [B8b2](B8b2.md).

- **B8b1. Defeat choices** (Game, about 300 lines; decision 65)
  - **Goal:** after the defeat's beats, or at once for a battle lost at the start, a plain defeat screen shows "Defeat", "The party has fallen.", and three choices:
    - **Load last save** (selected first): loads `SaveSlots.MostRecent` at once.
    - **Load:** opens the save list over the screen; Cancel closes it.
    - **Main menu:** ends the session.
    With no readable save, both load entries are greyed and the cursor starts on Main menu.
  - **New types (Game):**
    - `Battle/DefeatEntry`: LoadLastSave, Load, MainMenu.
    - `Battle/DefeatMenu` (pure): the entries and cursor.
    - `Battle/DefeatScreen`: hosts `Saving/SaveFilesPanel` and draws a plain layout.
      - It returns `LoadRequested(save)` or `TitleRequested`, once.
      - `BattleScene` takes `SaveSlots` (fourth parameter).
  - **Tests:**
    - Menu: `DefeatMenu_Start_SelectsLoadLastSave`, `DefeatMenu_NoReadableSave_BothLoadsGreyedAndMainMenuSelected`.
    - Screen: `DefeatScreen_Update_ConfirmLoadLastSave_RequestsMostRecent`, `DefeatScreen_Update_ChooseFileFromLoad_RequestsLoadingIt`, `DefeatScreen_Update_ConfirmMainMenu_RequestsTitle`.
    - Scene: `BattleScene_Update_BattleLostAtStart_ShowsDefeat`.
    - Flow: `GameFlow_Update_DefeatThenMainMenu_EndsSession`.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Lose with a near-dead leader: Load last save restores the latest save.
    - [ ] Load picks another file.
    - [ ] Main menu ends the game.
    - [ ] With no saves, both loads are greyed.
    - [ ] Leave only fallen members active on the Party page, walk into a battle: Defeat.
- **Done, with deviations:** about 420 lines, about half of them tests, past the 300 planned. `BattleScene` would have taken a fifth parameter, so the catalog's items and the save files are grouped in `BattleSceneSources`. Every outcome now stays on the battle screen (`Finish` returns nothing), and a defeat shows `DefeatScreen`, drawn plainly over the battle (scrim, red "Defeat", the subtitle, the choices as text). `BattleTurnSequence` starts at `Over` for a battle lost at the start, so no enemy acts in it (C4's interim crash). "Load" reuses the title's `title_load` text and heading. Test names drop the class prefix; `DefeatMenu_Start_SelectsLoadLastSave` became `Start_ReadableSave_SelectsLoadLastSave`, and extra tests cover Cancel on the save list and leaving once. The planned scene test is `Update_BattleLostAtStart_FinishesBattleAndWaitsOnDefeat` (review: it checks the screen waits, not only the Confirm). `Update_PartyFalls_RequestsTitle` became `Update_PartyFallsThenMainMenu_RequestsTitle`. The flow test waits on the pending encounter, as a battle lost at the start begins and ends within one update.
