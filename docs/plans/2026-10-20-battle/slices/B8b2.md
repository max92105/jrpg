# B8b2. Defeat look

Part of [the battle plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md). Split from B8b by the developer (2026-10-21): the choices are [B8b1](B8b1.md).

- **B8b2. Defeat look** (Game, about 150 lines)
  - **Goal:** the legacy `DefeatScreen` look, in the victory screen's frame:
    - the left-dark shade over the battle, with the HUD hidden;
    - a red "Defeat" (96 px, `Brush.Danger`) fading in over 0.6 s, with the hairline; "The party has fallen." under it;
    - the choices appearing after 0.7 s, each on a pointed bar (`Shape.RewardBar`) from (88, 320), lit with the selection gradient and the pointer when selected, greyed when unavailable;
    - the footer with "Choose what to do next.".
    Input waits until the choices have appeared.
  - **New types (Game):** `Battle/DefeatScreenView`; `Battle/DefeatEntrance` (pure timing).
  - **Tests:** `DefeatEntrance_Update_BeforeChoices_ChoicesHidden`, `DefeatEntrance_Update_PastDelay_ChoicesShown`, `DefeatScreen_Update_ConfirmBeforeChoicesShow_DoesNothing`.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Compare with legacy `DefeatScreen` and `Victory_Menu.jpg`'s frame: red title, timing, lit choice.
- **Done, with deviations:** about 250 lines, over the 150 planned, as B8b1's plain drawing moved out of `DefeatScreen` into `DefeatScreenView`. `DefeatScreen.Update` now takes the delta and runs `DefeatEntrance`; the choices take input once fully faded in (1.0 s), not at 0.7 s. The selected choice is a reward bar baked in the selection gradient (`UiTextures.SelectedRewardBar`), with the code-drawn row diamond for legacy's `Pointer.png`. The title fades; the subtitle, hairline and footer show at once, as in legacy. The shade, hairline and footer repeat `VictoryScreenView`'s (second copy). `BattleSceneTests` and `GameFlowTests` wait for the choices before Confirm. New footer message `battle_defeat_hint`.
