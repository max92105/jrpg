# C1. Battles in the session

Part of [the battle plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md).

- **C1. Battles in the session** (Core, about 230 lines)
  - **Goal:** the session starts the pending encounter as a battle and finishes it:
    - experience is granted on a victory;
    - the encounter is cleared on any outcome, so the grace steps restart and saving works.
    Enemy tactics can be used by the Game.
  - **New types (Core):** `Battle/VictoryRewards`: what a won battle gave: `int Experience` (the enemies' total) and `IReadOnlyList<ExperienceGain> Gains`. Gold and drops become new members later (decision 103).
  - **Changes:**
    - `BattleState.GrantVictoryExperience` returns `VictoryRewards`. Requested change: the existing reward tests read `.Gains`, assertions unchanged.
    - `EnemyTactics` becomes `public sealed`.
    - `GameSession` keeps its catalog and `IRandom`. New members:
      - `EnemyTactics`;
      - `BattleState? ActiveBattle`;
      - `BattleState StartPendingBattle()`: throws `InvalidOperationException` with no encounter pending, or a battle already active;
      - `VictoryRewards? FinishBattle()`: throws unless the active battle is over. Experience on a victory, null otherwise; calls `Exploration.FinishEncounter()`; clears `ActiveBattle`.
  - **Tests:**
    - Starting: `GameSession_StartPendingBattle_NoEncounterPending_Throws`, `GameSession_StartPendingBattle_GoblinTrioPending_PutsActivePartyAgainstThreeGoblins` (test catalog rolling every step), `GameSession_StartPendingBattle_BattleAlreadyActive_Throws`.
    - Finishing: `GameSession_FinishBattle_BattleNotOver_Throws`, `GameSession_FinishBattle_AfterVictory_GrantsExperienceAndReturnsTotal`, `GameSession_FinishBattle_AfterEscape_ReturnsNull`.
    - After: `GameSession_Capture_AfterFinishBattle_Succeeds`, `BattleState_GrantVictoryExperience_ThreeGoblins_ExperienceIsSumOfRewards`.
