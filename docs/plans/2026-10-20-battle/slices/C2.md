Part of [the battle plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md).

  - **Goal:** Core lists who the current actor can attack, by the same rule `Execute` checks.
  - **Changes:** `BattleState.TargetChoiceForAttack()`: `TargetChoice<Combatant>` over the living enemies in formation order. A private `ChoiceFor(Targeting)` is used by it and by `ThrowIfIllegalTargets`, so the offer and the check can't drift.
  - **Tests:** `BattleState_TargetChoiceForAttack_DefeatedEnemy_IsExcluded`, `BattleState_TargetChoiceForAttack_TwoLivingEnemies_InFormationOrder`, `BattleState_TargetChoiceForAttack_IsSingleTarget`. The existing `Execute` target tests pass unchanged.
- **Done, with deviations:** as planned; the attack's targeting is one `AttackTargeting` constant shared by the offer and `Execute`. Test names drop the `BattleState_` prefix, as in the existing file.
