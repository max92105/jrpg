# C2. The leader rule and "never all fallen" in Core

Part of [the menu cleanup plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Core changes" and key decision 3.

- **C2. The leader rule and "never all fallen" in Core** (Core + content + Game, about 230 lines; decision 107)
  - **Goal:** Core owns what a member may do on the Party page, and the page asks it.
    - The leader is fixed: always first, never in the reserve, never changes place (decision 107 (a)).
    - The leader may be fallen, but the active party is never entirely fallen. Core refuses sending a member to the reserve when nobody else active is standing.
    - On the page, a refused "To reserve" is greyed. Confirm on it shows the refusal notice.
  - **Changes (Core, `Party/PartyState`):**
    - `public bool CanMoveToReserve(PartyMember member)`: the member is active, isn't the leader, and at least one other active member `Vitals.IsAlive`.
      - `TryMoveToReserve` returns `CanMoveToReserve(member) && _activeMembers.Remove(member)`.
      - The XML doc names both rules and says why swapping places doesn't need the fallen check: reordering never changes who is active.
    - `public bool CanChangePlace(PartyMember member)`: active and not the leader. Two members can swap when both can.
      - `Reorder` throws when a member who can't change place isn't at their current index. The message stays "The leader must stay first."
    - Both queries throw `ArgumentException` for a member who isn't recruited, through `ThrowIfNotRecruited`.
    - The constructor is unchanged. It still accepts an all-fallen active party, so older saves load (design, risks).
  - **Changes (content + Game):**
    - `content/text/ui.json` gains `"party_needs_someone_standing": "{name} is the only one standing and can't leave the party."` (placeholder wording, README question).
    - `Ui/MessageIds.PartyNeedsSomeoneStanding`.
  - **Changes (Game, `Menus/PartyPage`):**
    - `ConfirmSelected`, on an active member:
      - if both queries are false, show `PartyLeaderStays`. Compare with `Leader` only to name them in the message;
      - otherwise open the choice with `new ListCursor(_actions.Length, _ => true)`. Greyed actions stay selectable, as battle decision 105 does.
    - On Confirm in the choice:
      - "To reserve" with `!CanMoveToReserve(Selected)` closes the choice and shows `party_needs_someone_standing` with the member's name;
      - otherwise `TryMoveToReserve` as today.
    - `DrawActionChoice` draws "To reserve" in `UiTheme.TextDisabled` when `!CanMoveToReserve(Selected)`. The flag is worked out once when the choice opens, not per frame.
    - `MoveToNextPlaceToSwap` skips rows whose member `!_party.CanChangePlace(...)`, instead of `_row == 0`. The loop ends because the moving member can change place.
  - **Tests (Core, `PartyStateTests`):**
    - `CanMoveToReserve_Leader_ReturnsFalse`, `CanMoveToReserve_ActiveMemberWithAnotherStanding_ReturnsTrue`, `CanMoveToReserve_ReserveMember_ReturnsFalse`;
    - `CanMoveToReserve_LastStandingWithFallenLeader_ReturnsFalse`;
    - `CanMoveToReserve_FallenMemberWithLeaderStanding_ReturnsTrue`;
    - `TryMoveToReserve_LastStanding_ReturnsFalseAndKeepsThemActive`;
    - `CanChangePlace_Leader_ReturnsFalse`, `CanChangePlace_ActiveMember_ReturnsTrue`, `CanChangePlace_ReserveMember_ReturnsFalse`, `CanChangePlace_NotRecruited_Throws`.
    - Members are knocked out with `Vitals.TakeDamage(Vitals.MaxHp)`.
    - The existing `TryMoveToReserve_Leader_ReturnsFalse` and `Reorder_LeaderNotFirst_Throws` pass unchanged.
  - **Tests (Game, `PartyPageTests`):**
    - `Update_ToReserveOnLastStanding_RefusesAndSaysWhy`: leader knocked out, a second member active, To reserve chosen. The member stays active, and `Notice` is the refusal text with their name.
    - The existing tests pass unchanged, including `Update_LeaderSelected_OffersNoMove` and `Update_ChangeOrderUp_SkipsTheLeadersPlace`.
    - `MessageIdsTests` covers the new id.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Confirm on the leader still shows "{name} leads the party and always stays in it."
    - [ ] Change order still skips the leader's place, Up and Down.
    - [ ] With the leader at 0 HP (set in a save) and one other active member, "To reserve" is greyed. Confirm on it shows the refusal.
- **Done, with deviations:** the refusal notice is shown when `TryMoveToReserve` returns false rather than after a separate `CanMoveToReserve` check (same outcome, one Core call). A greyed "To reserve" draws in `TextDisabled` even when selected, as on the Inventory page.
