# U1. One section title

Part of [the menu cleanup plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "What exists" (copies), key decision 5, tunables.

- **U1. One section title** (Game, about 130 lines)
  - **Goal:** the three identical `DrawSectionTitle` copies in `PartyPage`, `EquipmentPage` and `StatusPage` become one shared piece. Nothing changes on screen.
  - **New types (Game):** `Ui/SectionTitle`, `internal sealed`. It draws one section's header: a small label over a hairline that fades out to the right (legacy `SectionHeaderStyle`).
    - `public SectionTitle(ScreenText text, UiTextures ui, string messageId)`: fetches the label and the 14 px font once.
    - `public const int LineGap = 20`: where the hairline sits below the label. Pages place their content from it.
    - `public void Draw(SpriteBatch batch, TextureLibrary textures, Rectangle section, float opacity)`. `section.Width` is the hairline's width, and only `X`, `Y` and `Width` are read.
  - **Changes:**
    - `PartyPage`: `_activeTitle` and `_reserveTitle` become `SectionTitle` fields. `CardsTop = SectionTitle.LineGap + 12`. Remove its `SectionLineGap` and `DrawSectionTitle`. `_labelFont` stays for the count.
    - `EquipmentPage`: two `SectionTitle`s (Equipment, Parameters). `EquipmentLabels` loses `Equipment` and `Parameters`. Remove `SectionLineGap` and `DrawSectionTitle`.
    - `StatusPage`: three `SectionTitle`s (Experience, Parameters, Equipment), drawn with `page.Batch`, `page.Textures` and `page.Opacity`. `StatusLabels` loses those three. Remove `SectionLineGap` and `DrawSectionTitle`.
    - The column-title copies (Inventory, `EquipmentChoiceView`, `SaveSlotListView`) stay as they are (README, out of scope).
  - **Tests:** none new. It is draw-only, and the existing page tests pass unchanged.
