# ADR 0014: Editing the shared catalogs

Part of [the editor plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions".

- **ADR 0014: Editing the shared catalogs** (before E3a)
  - Write `docs/decisions/0014-editing-shared-catalogs.md` with `/adr`, from the options in the plan's "ADRs needed" (A a catalog draft saved with Ctrl+S and undone with Ctrl+Z through a shell journal, recommended; B modal managers with their own Save; C write at once; D one undo history), and add its line to `docs/decisions/README.md`.
  - It records the developer's decisions 113 (tiles made on first paint; not undo steps of their own, since an unused tile is harmless), 115 (the cut entered as columns and rows, stored as tile size, no format change) and 117 (Ctrl+S saves both; Ctrl+Z undoes catalog changes too; an undo that would remove a record still in use is refused).
  - It records the writer rule: one `ContentWriter.WriteMapCatalogs` writes `tilesheets.json`, `tiles.json` and `objects.json` after the catalog validators, every map's layout check and the art check, all three or none.
  - Accepted by the developer before E3a starts.
- **Done, with deviations:** as planned; option A chosen by the developer (2026-10-21): [ADR 0014](../../../decisions/0014-editing-shared-catalogs.md).
