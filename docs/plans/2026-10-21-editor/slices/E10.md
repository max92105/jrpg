# E10. Above-player zones

Part of [the editor plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 7, "Legacy mapping" (`ObjectEditorWindow`). Builds on E9's shapes window.

- **E10. Above-player zones** (Editor, about 200 lines)
  - **Goal:**
    - The shapes window gets a choice of what clicks draw: **Collision (blocks the player)** or **Over the player (leaves, roofs)**, as legacy did. Collision is chosen when the window opens.
    - With "Over the player" chosen, a list shows the object's zones ("Zone 1 (6 points)"), with **New zone** and **Delete zone**. Clicks, Backspace, Ctrl+Z and Clear shape act on the chosen zone; a click with no zone chosen starts Zone 1 (legacy).
    - Every zone is drawn in the overlay's blue over the picture; the collision outline stays drawn in green whichever is chosen (dimming the inactive shapes comes in E11b).
    - A zone with 1 or 2 points disables OK and names it: "Zone 2 needs at least 3 points (has 2)." An empty zone is dropped on OK (it draws nothing, and the validator refuses it).
    - OK is one catalog step, as in E9 (Ctrl+Z in the main window restores the old zones). On OK the map redraws its canopies (parts of the object drawn over the player, `MapDrawing.DrawCanopy`) with the new zones; the game draws them over the player on the next start (its `CanopyMask` reads the same 0..1 zones).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Objects/ObjectShapesViewModel`:
      - `IsEditingZones` (observable) and `IsEditingCollision` (its inverse, for the two radio buttons);
      - `Zones` (`IReadOnlyList<IReadOnlyList<Point2D>>`, replaced on each change), `ZoneEntries` (the list's texts), `SelectedZoneIndex` (-1 when none);
      - `NewZoneCommand`, `DeleteZoneCommand` (can execute with a zone chosen; selects the zone before it, or none);
      - `ActivePoints`: the points the tools act on, the outline or the chosen zone (what the window draws on top);
      - `AddPoint` (with E9's clamping and pixel rounding), `RemoveLastPoint` and `ClearShapeCommand` act on the active shape; `Problem` also covers zones; `ToDefinition()` writes the zones, dropping empty ones.
    - `Objects/ObjectShapesWindow`: the radio buttons, the zone list and its buttons (shown while editing zones), zones drawn with `CollisionDrawing`'s zone brushes (made `internal static` like E9's outline brushes).
    - Controls help (F1): the zone lines in the "Shapes window" section.
  - **Existing tests this breaks:** none; E9's `ObjectShapesViewModelTests` run with Collision chosen, the default.
  - **Tests:**
