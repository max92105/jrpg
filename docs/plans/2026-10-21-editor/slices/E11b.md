# E11b. Rectangle, snap, zoom and dimming

Part of [the editor plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (shapes window, collision overlay); decision 126. Builds on E11a.

- **E11b. Rectangle, snap, zoom and dimming** (Editor, about 190 lines)
  - **Goal (legacy `ObjectEditorWindow`):**
    - **Shift + drag** draws a rectangle: the active shape becomes its four corners. **Whole object** makes it the full 0..1 square.
    - **Snap to 1/4 tile** checkbox (decision 126, off by default, as legacy): added, moved, inserted and rectangle points land on quarter cells of the object.
    - **Zoom:** the window opens so the object's longer side is 560 px, between 1x and 6x of map pixels; the wheel zooms 1.2x per notch between 0.5x and 12x; scroll bars when the picture is larger than the window. The picture is point-scaled; handles keep their screen size.
    - Shapes that aren't active are drawn at 35 % opacity; colors and stroke widths are the collision overlay's (E8).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Objects/ShapeEditing.SnapToQuarterCell(Point2D point, int width, int height)`: a 0..1 point moved to the nearest quarter of the object's cells.
    - `Objects/ObjectShapesViewModel`: `IsSnapping` (observable, off); `SetRectangle(Point2D corner, Point2D oppositeCorner)`, `CoverWholeObjectCommand`; the placing step snaps when `IsSnapping` (after clamping, before the pixel rounding, which a quarter cell already satisfies).
    - `Objects/ObjectShapesWindow`: a `ScrollViewer` with a `LayoutTransform` scale replaces E9's `Viewbox`; its opening zoom and wheel steps are named constants beside the tunables row; Shift + drag; the Snap box and Whole object button; inactive shapes at 35 %.
    - Controls help (F1): Shift + drag, wheel and snap.
  - **Existing tests this breaks:** none.
  - **Tests:**
    - `ShapeEditingTests`: `SnapToQuarterCell_TwoCellWideObject_RoundsToEighths` ((0.30, 0.5) on a 2 x 1 object -> (0.25, 0.5)).
    - `ObjectShapesViewModelTests`:
      - `AddPoint_Snapping_SnapsToQuarterCell`;
      - `SetRectangle_TwoCorners_MakesFourPointsClockwise`;
      - `CoverWholeObjectCommand_EditingZones_MakesTheChosenZoneTheUnitSquare`.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Open `chest` (1 x 1): it opens at 6x; `house_1`: its longer side is about 560 px. The wheel zooms; pixels stay sharp; handles stay the same size at 0.5x and 12x.
    - [ ] Snap on, Shift + drag: the rectangle's corners sit on quarter cells (count against the picture's tile grid).
    - [ ] Editing zones, the outline is faint; editing collision, the zones are faint.
- **Done, with deviations:** the scroll view is capped at 760 x 600 screen px (the window doesn't resize); Shift + drag takes priority over handles and edges and redraws the rectangle live; a Shift + drag also ends in `LostMouseCapture`, like a handle drag, and blocks keys and clicks meanwhile. Points are clamped in `Placed` now (the pixel rounding no longer clamps). Review: a test that a snapped rectangle lands on quarter cells. Left: the wheel zooms from the scroll area's top left, not from the mouse.
