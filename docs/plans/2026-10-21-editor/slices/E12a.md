# E12a. A rectangle of sheet cells

Part of [the editor plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (sheet marks: object selection). Split up front (review): the selection first, New object (its first user) in E12b, the next slice. Decision 123: no stamp brush, so painting keeps using one cell.

- **E12a. A rectangle of sheet cells** (Editor, about 140 lines)
  - **Goal:**
    - **Shift + drag** over the sheet in the Tiles panel chooses a rectangle of whole cells, outlined dashed cyan; the line under the sheet says "Cells (3, 4) to (5, 7): 3 x 4". A plain click goes back to choosing one cell.
    - Painting uses the rectangle's top-left cell (decision 123: no stamp brush); the line says so: "... (paints the top-left cell)".
    - Its use, New object from selection, is E12b.
  - **New types:**
    - `Catalogs/SheetCellRectangle` (readonly record struct `SheetId`, `Left`, `Top`, `Columns`, `Rows`): a rectangle of whole cells of one sheet.
      - `Between(SheetCell corner, SheetCell oppositeCorner)`: built from two corners in any order.
      - `Cells`: the cells row by row.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Tiles/TileBrowserViewModel`: `SelectedCells` (`SheetCellRectangle?`); `SelectCellsBetween(Point2D corner, Point2D oppositeCorner)` (image pixels; a corner outside whole cells is clamped to the nearest whole cell; sets `SelectedCell` to the top-left); `SelectCellAt` clears `SelectedCells`; `ChoiceText` covers the rectangle.
    - `Tiles/TilesheetMarks.Draw`: the dashed rectangle.
    - `Tiles/TileBrowserView`: Shift + mouse down captures, mouse up calls `SelectCellsBetween`. Controls help (F1): Shift + drag on the sheet.
  - **Existing tests this breaks:** none (a plain click behaves as before).
  - **Tests:**
    - `SheetCellRectangleTests`: `Between_CornersInAnyOrder_GivesTheSameRectangle`, `Cells_TwoByTwo_ListsRowByRow`.
    - `TileBrowserViewModelTests`: `SelectCellsBetween_TwoCells_ChoosesTheRectangleAndItsTopLeftCell`, `SelectCellsBetween_CornerPastLastWholeColumn_ClampsToIt`, `SelectCellAt_AfterRectangle_ClearsIt`.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] On `tree_01_s_01_animation`, Shift + drag over a tree's cells: the dashed rectangle and the line show; a click goes back to one cell.
    - [ ] With a rectangle chosen, paint: the top-left cell paints.
