# E13c. Dark templates: combo boxes, scroll bars, tool bar overflow

Part of [the editor plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Risks" (dark theme size); decision 121. Split from E13b, as its "Known risk" planned: E13b's theme covers every window but leaves these controls light, since their Windows 10/11 templates ignore `Background`.

- **E13c. Dark templates: combo boxes, scroll bars, tool bar overflow** (Editor, about 200 lines, XAML only)
  - **Goal:** no light patch left in the editor's windows: combo boxes (editable or not, in a panel, and the drop-down end of a tool bar's), scroll bars, the tool bars' overflow buttons, and the open menus' frame and separators are dark, in every state (hover, pressed, open, disabled), with the theme's brushes.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Shell/EditorTheme.xaml`: control templates for `ComboBox` (and its toggle and editable text box), `ScrollBar` (thumb, track, line buttons), the tool bar's overflow toggle, and the top-level `MenuItem`'s drop-down frame and `Separator` (Windows hard-codes them light); E13b's interim `ComboBox` dark-text style goes.
  - **Tests:** none new: look only.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Every window E13b lists: combo boxes and scroll bars dark in every state; the sheet box, the layer boxes and the event step editor's lists read clearly.
    - [ ] A tool bar narrowed by the window shows its overflow button and menu dark.
