# E13c. Dark templates: combo boxes, scroll bars, tool bar overflow

Part of [the editor plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Risks" (dark theme size); decision 121. Split from E13b, as its "Known risk" planned: E13b's theme covers every window but leaves these controls light, since their Windows 10/11 templates ignore `Background`.

- **E13c. Dark templates: combo boxes, scroll bars, tool bar overflow** (Editor, about 200 lines, XAML only)
  - **Goal:** no light patch left in the editor's windows: combo boxes (editable or not, in a panel, and the drop-down end of a tool bar's), scroll bars, the tool bars' overflow buttons, and the open menus' frame and separators are dark, in every state (hover, pressed, open, disabled), with the theme's brushes.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Shell/EditorTheme.xaml`: control templates for `ComboBox` (and its toggle and editable text box), `ScrollBar` (thumb, track, line buttons), the tool bar's overflow toggle, and the top-level `MenuItem`'s drop-down frame and `Separator` (Windows hard-codes them light); E13b's interim `ComboBox` dark-text style goes.
  - **Tests:** none new: look only.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Every window E13b lists: combo boxes and scroll bars dark in every state; the sheet box, the layer boxes and the event step editor's lists read clearly.
    - [ ] A tool bar narrowed by the window shows its overflow button and menu dark.
- **Done, with deviations:** the templates live in their own dictionary, `Shell/EditorTemplates.xaml`, merged after `EditorTheme.xaml` (brushes taken by `DynamicResource`, so the merge order doesn't matter for them); the theme's interim ComboBox, ToolBar and MenuItem styles went. Scroll bars are thin, with no arrow buttons (the wheel, a drag or a click on the track scroll). The menu template handles the editor's one-level menus only (bar headers and plain entries, check marks and shortcuts), not nested submenus. The tool bar template has no grip, since the tray is locked. Screenshots checked: the main window, an open menu, the sheet and layer boxes; the dialogs and the tool bar overflow need the hand checks.
- **Review follow-up:** the overflow button is 18 px so its arrow fits (the toggle template is now `DropDownToggle`, shared with combo boxes); a menu's drop-down opens against its header (`PlacementTarget`); a dragged scroll thumb keeps its highlight; tool bar separators are themed too.
- **Fix after the developer's check (2026-10-22):** the shapes window's OK button was white with light text: its local style (greyed while the shape has a problem) replaced the theme's button style. It is now `BasedOn` the theme's; no other local control style in the editor bypasses the theme (searched). Lesson for the retro: a local style on a themed control must be `BasedOn="{StaticResource {x:Type ...}}"`.
