# P1. The party sidebar: which pages, plain cards

Part of [the demo retirement plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), key decisions 4-5, risks ("The sidebar narrows pages"); tunables "sidebar placement" and "sidebar cards"; decision 139; questions 145, 146.

- **P1. The party sidebar: behavior and a plain draw** (Game, about 220 lines)
  - **Goal:**
    - A column on the right of the game menu shows a card per active member, in party order: name, level, "HP 45 / 60" and "MP 12 / 20" as plain text.
    - It shows on every page except Party, and not on the main menu (question 145 placeholder).
    - It hides while the Equipment page shows its item choice.
    - It follows changes made in the page at once: a potion used in Inventory, a member moved in Party, equipment changed.
    - The page beside it gets the narrower area.
  - **New types:**
    - `Menus/PartySidebar` (pure): the texts of the active members' cards, rebuilt only when a shown value changes.
      - `PartySidebar(PartyState party, UiMessages messages)`.
      - `Refresh()`: compares each member's level, HP, MP and the active order with the last snapshot, and rebuilds only the cards that changed.
      - `Cards` (`IReadOnlyList<SidebarCard>`).
    - `Menus/SidebarCard` (record): one card's member and its formatted texts.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/IMenuPage.ShowsPartySidebar` (`bool`, read-only):
      - `PartyPage` returns false;
      - `EquipmentPage` returns false while its choice is open;
      - every other page returns true.
    - `Menus/GameMenuScene`:
      - calls `Refresh()` after the page's update while a page is open;
      - when `ShowsPartySidebar`, gives the page the narrower area (the page area minus 460 + 24 px) and draws the cards in the column (plain text, 12 px apart).
    - Pages laid out for the full width are checked at the narrower area: `StatusPage`, `InventoryPage`, `EquipmentPage`, `OptionsPage`, `SavePage`/`LoadPage`. A page that overlaps reads its column positions from `area` instead of constants. A page that can't fit is reported to the developer, not squeezed.
    - Messages reused: `stat_value_of_max` and the HP/MP/level labels in `ui.json`. No new text.
  - **Tests:**
    - `PartySidebarTests`:
      - `Cards_ActiveMembers_InPartyOrder`;
      - `Refresh_NothingChanged_KeepsTheSameCards` (`Assert.Same`);
      - `Refresh_MemberHealed_RebuildsOnlyThatCard`;
      - `Refresh_ActiveOrderChanged_ReordersTheCards`;
      - `Cards_ReserveMember_IsNotListed`.
    - `GameMenuSceneTests`:
      - `ShowsSidebar_OnStatusPage_IsTrue`;
      - `ShowsSidebar_OnPartyPage_IsFalse`;
      - `ShowsSidebar_MainMenu_IsFalse`.
    - `EquipmentPageTests`: `ShowsPartySidebar_WhileChoiceOpen_IsFalse`.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Open Status, Inventory, Equipment, Options, Save, Load: the column on the right lists the active party. Party: no column. Main menu: no column.
    - [ ] Use a potion on a hurt member: their HP in the column changes at once.
    - [ ] Equipment, open a slot's choice: the column hides; close it: it comes back.
    - [ ] Every page's content fits left of the column, with no overlap.
