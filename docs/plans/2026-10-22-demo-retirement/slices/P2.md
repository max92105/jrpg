# P2. The party sidebar's look

Part of [the demo retirement plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Game structure added by this plan"; tunable "sidebar cards"; question 146. Look at `docs/ui_ux_exemples/` first (`.claude/rules/game.md`, "UI look").

- **P2. The party sidebar's look** (Game, about 150 lines)
  - **Goal:**
    - The plain cards become the menu's member cards: diamond portrait, name, level, thin HP and MP bars with their numbers (`Ui/MemberCardView`, as the Party page and the battle use them).
    - The cards sit on a translucent panel that fades out toward the page, in the reference style.
    - The column fades in with the page (T1, if kept).
  - **New types:**
    - `Menus/PartySidebarView`: draws the sidebar's cards from `PartySidebar.Cards` with `Ui/MemberCardView`, at an opacity.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/GameMenuScene`: draws `PartySidebarView` instead of P1's plain text.
    - `Ui/UiTheme`: the column's panel fade, if no existing value fits.
    - `Ui/MemberCardView`: only if it needs a size it doesn't have yet. Otherwise unchanged.
  - **Tests:** draw-only; P1's tests pass unchanged.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Side by side with `docs/ui_ux_exemples/` and the Party page: the cards match the game's other member cards.
    - [ ] A member at low HP: the bar shows it; after a potion it fills.
    - [ ] Four active members fit the column's height at 1080p, with room left.
