# T1. Page transition: slide and fade

Part of [the demo retirement plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Gap survey" (game screens), "Backlog items near this plan"; tunable "page transition". From the plan review; the developer can strike it (README, "Slices").

- **T1. Page transition** (Game, about 120 lines)
  - **Goal:**
    - Opening a menu page slides it 24 px in from the right while it fades in, over 220 ms with a cubic ease-out, as in the demo.
    - Switching tabs plays it again. Closing a page doesn't animate (the demo didn't).
    - It is a timing on the existing draw, so behavior and look are one slice.
  - **New types:**
    - `Ui/Easing` (static, pure): the clamped share of a duration that has passed, and the cubic ease-out.
      - `static float ShareOf(TimeSpan elapsed, TimeSpan length)`: clamped to 0..1.
      - `static float CubicOut(float share)`.
    - `Menus/PageTransition` (pure): a page's slide offset and opacity over time after it opens.
      - `Start()`, `Update(TimeSpan delta)`.
      - `Offset` (px, 24 down to 0), `Opacity` (0 to 1).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/GameMenuScene`: starts the transition when a page opens, and draws the page with its `area` shifted by `Offset` and its opacity multiplied by `Opacity`.
    - `docs/plans/backlog.md`: the "clamped time share is copied 6 times" line now says those copies can move to `Ui/Easing`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `EasingTests`:
      - `ShareOf_PastTheLength_IsOne`;
      - `ShareOf_Halfway_IsHalf`;
      - `CubicOut_Half_IsSevenEighths`.
    - `PageTransitionTests`:
      - `Update_AtStart_IsOffsetAndTransparent`;
      - `Update_PastTheDuration_IsInPlaceAndOpaque`;
      - `Start_AgainMidway_RestartsFromTheBeginning`.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Open each tab: the page glides in from the right and fades in, quick and smooth. Switch tabs: again.
    - [ ] Input works during the slide (the demo didn't block it).
