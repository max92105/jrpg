# F4. The victory card at the highest level shows a full bar and "Max"

Part of [the code health plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), backlog survey ("Victory screen at the maximum level"), "Core changes"; decision 148.

- **F4. Victory card at the highest level** (Core + Game, about 130 lines)
  - **Write first:** `VictoryScreenTests: IsAtMaxLevel_MemberAlreadyAtMaxLevel_IsTrue`. It fails to compile at first, because `IsAtMaxLevel` is a new API. That counts as the failing test; record it.
  - **Goal** (decision 148):
    - A member at the experience table's highest level shows their EXP row the way the Status page shows it: "EXP" then the max-level text (`status_max_level`, "Max") where the numbers go, and a full bar. It is never "EXP 0/0" with an empty bar, and never hidden.
    - A member who reaches the highest level in this battle sees their bar fill and "Level Up!". Once the bar is complete, the row turns to "Max" with a full bar.
    - Other members are unchanged.
  - **Changes:**
    - Core `Battle/ExperienceGain`: a new positional `bool IsAtMaxLevel`, meaning the member is at the table's highest level after this gain.
    - Core `Battle/BattleRewards.GrantExperience`:
      - sets it from `table.IsMaxLevel(member.Level)`, read after `GainExperience`;
      - the gain is computed on its own line, so the order of the two calls is visible.
    - Game `Battle/VictoryScreen`: `bool IsAtMaxLevel(int index)` is true once that member's bar is complete and their gain `IsAtMaxLevel`.
    - Game `Battle/VictoryScreenView`, both formatted once when the card's state changes (the `_shown` check gains the flag):
      - `ShowCard`: when `IsAtMaxLevel(index)`, the experience text is the max-level message and the needed text is empty;
      - the bar shows full (`Show(1, 1)`, as `StatusPage.ExperienceProgress` does at the highest level).
    - `tests/Jrpg.Game.Tests/Battle/VictoryScreenTests`: its hand-built `ExperienceGain` gets `IsAtMaxLevel: false`.
    - `docs/plans/backlog.md`: remove the "Victory screen at the maximum level" line.
  - **Tests:**
    - `BattleRewardsTests`:
      - `GrantExperience_MemberAlreadyAtMaxLevel_IsAtMaxLevel`;
      - `GrantExperience_GainReachesMaxLevel_IsAtMaxLevel`;
      - `GrantExperience_BelowMaxLevel_IsNotAtMaxLevel`.
    - `VictoryScreenTests`:
      - `IsAtMaxLevel_MemberAlreadyAtMaxLevel_IsTrue`;
      - `IsAtMaxLevel_ReachesMaxLevelWhileFilling_FalseUntilTheBarCompletes`;
      - `IsAtMaxLevel_BelowMaxLevel_IsFalse`.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] With a member at the highest level (a test save, or a content copy with a short experience table run with `--content`), win a battle: that card shows "EXP Max" and a full bar.
    - [ ] The other cards still show "EXP n/m" and their bars fill.
- **Done, with deviations:** as planned. The six tests first failed to compile (new API). `VictoryScreenView.ShowCard` now calls `ShowExperience` or `ShowMaxLevel`, which keeps it under 20 lines; "Max" and the empty needed text are measured once in the constructor. The hand checks need a member at the highest level and are left for the developer. Review: the empty `<param>` tags that format had added to `ExperienceGain` are folded into its summary.
