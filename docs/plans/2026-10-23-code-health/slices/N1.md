# N1. Data refuses more than 5 active members; the battle screens are checked with 5

Part of [the code health plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The party of five"; decision 149; questions 150 (content stays 4), 151 (enemy count). Comes after P1, which lets the sidebar hold every recruit.

- **N1. The party of five** (Core + Data + Game, about 120 lines)
  - **Write first, and see it fail:** `GameRulesValidatorTests: Validate_MoreThanFiveActiveMembers_NamesTheLimit`.
  - **Goal:**
    - The official party size is a named limit, and content can't go past it: `rules.json` with `maxActiveMembers` above 5 stops the game at startup, naming the field and the limit.
    - The battle screens are checked with 5 active members:
      - the party spots stay on screen without overlapping;
      - the five victory cards end above the footer line;
      - the party HUD's five panels fit.
    - Anything that doesn't fit and takes more than about 20 lines to fix is recorded in `backlog.md` with a screenshot, not fixed here.
    - Content stays at 4 until question 150 is answered.
  - **Changes:**
    - Core `Party/PartyRules`: `public const int MostActiveMembers = 5`, commented with decision 149: the battle screens are laid out for it.
    - Data `Content/GameRulesValidator`: refuses `party.maxActiveMembers` above `PartyRules.MostActiveMembers`, with "rules.json: party.maxActiveMembers can be at most 5 (was N)."
    - Game `Battle/VictoryScreenView`: the card position becomes `internal static Point CardTopLeft(int index)`, the same arithmetic, so a test can check it.
    - `docs/plans/backlog.md`: the "Victory cards have no row limit" line goes. Five cards fit (2 columns, 3 rows), and Data caps the party at 5.
  - **Tests:**
    - `GameRulesValidatorTests`:
      - `Validate_MoreThanFiveActiveMembers_NamesTheLimit`;
      - `Validate_FiveActiveMembers_Passes`.
    - `BattleLayoutTests`:
      - `PartySpot_FiveMembers_AllOnScreen`;
      - `PartySpot_FiveMembers_DoNotOverlap`;
      - `EnemySpot_FiveSlots_AllOnScreen`: today's layout limit, for question 151.
    - `VictoryScreenViewTests`: `CardTopLeft_FifthCard_EndsAboveTheFooterLine`.
  - **Verify by hand**, with a content copy run with `--content`: `rules.json` `maxActiveMembers` 5, and `new-game.json` with five active members.
    - [ ] Battle: five party battlers stand clear of each other. The party HUD shows five panels, and the turn order strip holds 5 + 3 combatants.
    - [ ] Win: five victory cards, the last above the footer line.
    - [ ] Menu: the Party page's columns, the sidebar and the save list's party column show five members. Record anything clipped in `backlog.md`.
