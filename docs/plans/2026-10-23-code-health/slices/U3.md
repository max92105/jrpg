# U3. `MeasuredText` wherever text is stored next to its width (not `InventoryPage`)

Part of [the code health plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), backlog survey ("Text stored next to its width by hand"). Comes after U2, which removes three of the widths.

- **U3. `MeasuredText` everywhere but `InventoryPage`** (Game, about 80 lines)
  - **Goal:**
    - A piece of text and its measured width travel together as `Ui/MeasuredText`, everywhere they are kept for right-aligning or centering, and the width fields that sat beside their text go.
    - `InventoryPage` is left alone: A1 and A2 change it in this plan, and its `ItemRow`/`InventoryLabels` widths wait with the rest of its split (backlog, "When next touched").
    - What the player sees is unchanged.
  - **Changes** (each place keeps measuring when its text changes, never per frame):
    - `Saving/SaveSlotListView`: `RowText.Party` + `PartyWidth` becomes `MeasuredText Party`.
    - `Ui/ConfirmPromptView`: `_yes`/`_yesWidth`, `_no`/`_noWidth`, the question and `_questionWidth`, and `PromptButton.Label` + `LabelWidth`.
    - `Menus/MenuHeader`: the play time with `_playTimeWidth`, and the location with `_locationWidth`.
    - `Menus/EquipmentChoiceView`: `_quantities` becomes `MeasuredText[]`.
    - `Menus/ItemTargetPickerView`: the heading and `_headingWidth`.
    - `Menus/SavePage`: `_noticeWidth`. `Notice` stays a string (`SavePageTests` reads it), with a `MeasuredText` beside it, built when it changes.
    - Afterwards, Grep `float _?\w*Width\b` in `src/Jrpg.Game`. The expected hits are:
      - `Ui/MeasuredText` itself;
      - `Menus/InventoryPage.cs` (`ItemRow`, `InventoryLabels`; left on purpose);
      - widths that aren't a text's: layout sizes such as `PanelStyle`, `VitalRowStyle`, or `BattleVitalRow`'s style width.

      List every hit in the slice's notes with its reason.
    - `docs/plans/backlog.md`: the "Text stored next to its width by hand" line keeps only `InventoryPage.ItemRow`/`InventoryLabels`, under "When next touched", with the `InventoryPage` split.
  - **Tests:** none (draw-only). `SavePageTests`, `ConfirmPromptTests` and the other presenter tests pass unchanged.
  - **Verify by hand** (before/after screenshots, the same pixels):
    - [ ] the menu header (play time and location at the right);
    - [ ] the Save page with its notice after saving;
    - [ ] a yes/no prompt (System > Quit to title);
    - [ ] the item target picker (use a potion);
    - [ ] the Equipment page with a slot's choice open.
- **Done, with deviations:** as planned, but the before/after screenshots weren't taken: the play script captures the whole screen, and with the developer working the game never came to the front (the shot caught another window, deleted at once), so the five checks stay hand checks. The width Grep finds only `Ui/MeasuredText` and `InventoryPage`'s `ItemRow`/`InventoryLabels` (left on purpose); no layout width matches the pattern. The backlog line is dropped: the `InventoryPage` split entry already carries its two widths. `MenuHeader` names its two `_shownPlayTime`/`_shownLocation`; `SavePage` keeps one nullable `MeasuredText`, `Notice` reading its text (review).
