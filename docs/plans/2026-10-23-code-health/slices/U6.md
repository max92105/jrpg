# U6. `Ui/FooterBar` and the battle outcome screens' `OutcomeFrame`

Part of [the code health plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), tunable "Outcome screen frame", backlog survey ("Footer bar with hairline drawn 3 times").

- **U6. `FooterBar` and `OutcomeFrame`** (Game, about 130 lines)
  - **Goal:**
    - The footer bar with its top hairline is drawn by one Ui part, which the menu and the two outcome screens use.
    - The victory and defeat screens share their frame: the dimmed battle, the title's place and hairline, and the hairline above the footer.
    - What the player sees is unchanged.
  - **New types:**
    - `Ui/FooterBar` (static): the bottom bar with its top hairline that screens write their hint on.
      - `static Rectangle Area(int height)`: the bar's rectangle at the bottom of the virtual screen, for placing the hint.
      - `static void Draw(SpriteBatch batch, Texture2D pixel, int height, float opacity)`.
    - `Battle/OutcomeFrame` (static): the victory and defeat screens' shared frame: dimmed battle, title hairline, footer with the hairline above it.
      - `const int TitleLeft = 104`, `const int TitleTop = 112`, `const int FooterHeight = 100`.
      - `static void DrawBackdrop(SpriteBatch batch, TextureLibrary textures, UiTextures ui)`: the scrim (decision 52) and the title hairline.
      - `static void DrawFooter(SpriteBatch batch, Texture2D pixel)`: the hairline 170 px above the bottom, then `FooterBar`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/GameMenuScene.DrawFrame`: the footer's two rectangles become `FooterBar.Draw(batch, textures.Pixel, FooterHeight, opacity)`. `DrawFooterHint` places the text in `FooterBar.Area(FooterHeight)`. The header bar stays: it has no copy.
    - `Battle/VictoryScreenView`, `Battle/DefeatScreenView`: the scrim, the title hairline and the footer rectangles become `OutcomeFrame` calls. Their copies of `TitleLeft`, `TitleTop`, `TitleLineLeft`, `TitleLineTop`, `FooterHeight` and `FooterLineAbove` go.
    - `docs/map.md`: Ui row lists `FooterBar`.
    - `docs/plans/backlog.md`: remove the "footer bar with its hairline is drawn 3 times" line.
  - **Tests:** none (draw-only). `VictoryScreenTests`, `DefeatScreenTests` and `GameMenuSceneTests` pass unchanged.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - [ ] Before/after screenshots of the menu (Esc), the same pixels.
    - [ ] Win a battle: the title, its hairline, the footer and "Next" sit where they did.
    - [ ] Lose a battle (or start one with a weak party): the same for "Defeat" and its choices.
