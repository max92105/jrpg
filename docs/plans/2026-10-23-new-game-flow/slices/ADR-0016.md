# ADR 0016: Intro cinematics

Part of [the new-game flow plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), key decisions 1, 5, 6.

- **ADR 0016: Intro cinematics** (checkbox step before K1; write with `/adr`)
  - **Question:** is the character intro the cutscene system the roadmap expects, or something simpler?
  - **Options** (README, "ADRs"):
    - A. a slideshow content type played by its own scene (recommended);
    - B. map cutscenes on `EventRun`;
    - C. video files.
  - **Record with the decision:**
    - The intro stacks over the map like a battle (ADR 0013), and the session starts at the choice.
    - Timing comes from content seconds on the update delta; voice drift is accepted.
    - Captions are optional per shot.
    - Map cutscenes stay a separate plan; a future event step can play a cinematic through `CinematicScene`.
  - **Done when:** `docs/decisions/0016-intro-cinematics.md` is accepted by the developer and indexed in `docs/decisions/README.md`.
