# ADR 0017: Stats come from the class

Part of [the new-game flow plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), key decision 4.

- **ADR 0017: Stats come from the class** (checkbox step before C1a; write with `/adr`)
  - **Question:** where do a party member's max HP and MP, attack and defense come from, now that a class is "like D&D has class" (decision 164) and everyone starts at level 1 (decision 168)?
  - **Options** (README, "ADRs"):
    - A. the class at the member's level, base plus linear growth (recommended);
    - B. per member, as today, plus a per-member growth;
    - C. class base plus a per-member modifier.
  - **Record with the decision:**
    - The formula `stat(level) = base + growth × (level − 1)` lives in one place (`Party/ClassGrowth`), with its reason, and is unit-tested.
    - A level-up adds the max HP and MP gained to current HP and MP (C1c).
    - It supersedes ADR 0007's "max HP/MP and base stats come from the member's definition". The save keeps level, XP, HP, MP and equipment, so the format doesn't change, and old saves get their class's stats at their saved level.
    - Class skills, subclasses and per-member modifiers are later extensions of the same class record.
  - **Done when:** `docs/decisions/0017-stats-come-from-the-class.md` is accepted by the developer and indexed. ADR 0007 gets a "partly superseded by 0017" note.
