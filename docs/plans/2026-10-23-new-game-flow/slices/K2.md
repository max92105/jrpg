# K2. The intro plays between the choice and the map

Part of [the new-game flow plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The flow", key decisions 6 and 7.

- **K2. The intro plays between the choice and the map, stills fading, Confirm or Cancel skipping** (Game, about 300 lines; decisions 159, 171, 172, 174)
  - **Goal:**
    - After the choice, the map starts with the leader's intro on top.
    - Each still covers the screen for its seconds, fading in and out over `ShotFade` (at most half the shot), with its caption as plain text at the bottom.
    - After the last still, or on a Confirm or Cancel press, the flow fades and the intro is removed, uncovering the map.
    - The lead-in, lead-out, caption band and skip hint come in K3.
  - **New types (Game):**
    - `Cinematics/CinematicPlayback` (pure): where a cinematic is in time.
      - `CinematicPlayback(CinematicDefinition cinematic)`;
      - `Update(TimeSpan delta)`; `Skip()`;
      - `CurrentShot` (null once over);
      - `ShotOpacity` (0 to 1);
      - `IsOver`.
    - `Cinematics/CinematicScene` (`IScene`): plays a cinematic over whatever is beneath.
      - `CinematicScene(CinematicDefinition cinematic, ScreenText text)`.
      - Confirm or Cancel skips.
      - Returns `CinematicFinished` once.
      - Draws black, then the still covering the screen (`CoverFit.DrawOverScreen`, smooth sampling) tinted by `ShotOpacity`, then the caption. The caption is measured when the shot changes.
    - `Scenes/CinematicFinished`: the cinematic on top is over; remove it.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Flow/SceneFactory.CreateIntro(CinematicDefinition cinematic)`.
    - `Flow/SessionTransitions.StartNewGame(LeaderChoice choice)` pushes the intro after the map.
    - `Flow/GameFlow`:
      - `CinematicFinished` pops the top scene;
      - split `ApplyRequestOnceBlack` here if S1 didn't.
    - `GameFlowTests.StartedFlow` presses Cancel once the intro shows, then waits out the fade.
  - **Tests:**
    - `CinematicPlaybackTests`:
      - `CurrentShot_New_IsFirstShot`
      - `Update_ExactlyFirstShotsSeconds_ShowsSecondShot` (the boundary belongs to the next shot)
      - `Update_OneDeltaLongerThanSeveralShots_LandsOnTheRightShot`
      - `Update_PastAllShots_IsOver`
      - `Skip_MidShot_IsOver`
      - `ShotOpacity_AtShotStart_IsZero`
      - `ShotOpacity_AfterShotFade_IsOne`
      - `ShotOpacity_ShotShorterThanTwoFades_PeaksAtHalfway`
    - `CinematicSceneTests`:
      - `Update_Confirm_RequestsFinishOnce`
      - `Update_Cancel_RequestsFinish`
      - `Update_PastAllShots_RequestsFinishOnce`
      - `Update_BeforeEnd_RequestsNothing`
    - `GameFlowTests`:
      - `Update_LeaderConfirmed_ShowsTheirIntroOverTheMap`: the map doesn't move under the intro while Right is held.
      - `Update_IntroSkipped_MapTakesInputAfterFade`
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - Choosing Zanyr shows the three stills in turn, each fading in and out, with captions; then the map.
    - Confirm during the intro skips it.
    - This is the first full run: title, choice, "Begin as X?", intro, map.
