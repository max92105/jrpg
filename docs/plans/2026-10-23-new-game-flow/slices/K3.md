# K3. Intro look: lead-in and lead-out, caption band, skip hint

Part of [the new-game flow plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables and placeholders" (screens: `LeadIn`, `LeadOut`, captions).

Look at `docs/ui_ux_exemples/` first: dark scrims, pale text, no boxes.

- **K3. Intro look: lead-in and lead-out, caption band, skip hint** (Game, about 170 lines; decisions 159, 171, 172)
  - **Goal:**
    - The intro opens on `LeadIn` of black. After the last still comes `LeadOut` of black, then the flow's fade to the map.
    - Subtitles sit centered in a dark band that fades out upward at the bottom: white with an outline, fading with their still.
    - A small skip hint ("Skip", with the device's Confirm button name from `ButtonLabels`) shows at the bottom right.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Cinematics/CinematicPlayback`:
      - lead-in and lead-out around the shots;
      - `CurrentShot` is null during both;
      - `IsOver` only after the lead-out.
      - The tunables are named constants with their reason.
    - `Cinematics/CinematicScene.Draw`:
      - the caption band, a vertical `UiTextures.Gradient`;
      - the outlined caption (`OutlinedFont`);
      - the skip hint, measured once.
    - `content/text/ui.json` and `Ui/MessageIds`: `cinematic_skip` ("{button} Skip").
  - **Tests (`CinematicPlaybackTests`):**
    - `CurrentShot_DuringLeadIn_IsNull`
    - `IsOver_DuringLeadOut_IsFalse`
    - `IsOver_AfterLeadOut_IsTrue`
    - K2's timing tests shift by `LeadIn`: update their expected times and keep each assertion.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - There's a short black before the first still and after the last.
    - Subtitles are readable on bright and dark stills.
    - The skip hint names the keyboard key, or the pad button when a pad is used.
    - Voice and stills stay roughly together.
