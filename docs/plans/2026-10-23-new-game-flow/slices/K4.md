# K4. Intro look: lead-in and lead-out, caption band, skip hint

Part of [the new-game flow plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables and placeholders" (screens: `LeadIn`, `LeadOut`, captions).

Look at `docs/ui_ux_exemples/` first: dark scrims, pale text, no boxes.

- **K4. Intro look: lead-in and lead-out, caption band, skip hint** (Game, about 170 lines; decisions 159, 171, 172)
  - **Goal:**
    - A run of stills opens on `LeadIn` of black and closes on `LeadOut` of black before the event's next step, so the map never flashes between stills or before the first.
    - Subtitles sit centered in a dark band that fades out upward at the bottom: white with an outline, fading with their still.
    - A small skip hint ("Skip", with the device's Confirm button name from `ButtonLabels`) shows at the bottom right while stills show.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Exploration/EventPresenter` and `StillPlayback`:
      - black holds between consecutive stills;
      - the lead-in comes before the first still of a run, and the lead-out after the last, before a non-still step;
      - the tunables are named constants with their reason.
    - The still's draw:
      - the caption band, a vertical `UiTextures.Gradient`;
      - the outlined caption (`OutlinedFont`);
      - the skip hint, measured once.
    - `content/text/ui.json` and `Ui/MessageIds`: `cinematic_skip` ("{button} Skip").
  - **Tests:**
    - `StillPlaybackTests` or `EventPresenterTests`:
      - `Blackness_BetweenTwoStills_StaysBlack`
      - `Update_DuringLeadIn_ShowsNoStill`
      - `Update_AfterLastStillsLeadOut_ContinuesToTheWarp`
    - K2c's timing tests shift by `LeadIn`: update their expected times and keep each assertion.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - There's a short black before the first still and after the last, and no map flash between stills.
    - Subtitles are readable on bright and dark stills.
    - The skip hint names the keyboard key, or the pad button when a pad is used.
    - Voice and stills stay roughly together.
