# ADR 0023: The battle formation

Part of [the class mechanics plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key design decisions" 4. Decision 201; question 211.

- **ADR 0023: The battle formation** (checkbox step before FM1; write with `/adr`)
  - **Question:** the paladin's aura reaches the allies within N places of him (decision 201), and the party must be able to move around in battle. What is a position, and how does it change?
  - **Options:**
    - A. the active party order is a line. `BattleState` keeps it as a `Formation` that starts from the active order, and a Move command swaps the actor with a neighbor (recommended). Distance is the difference in places; the party page's order already sets the starting line.
    - B. front and back rows, Octopath-like. A second concept that ranges in a line don't need, and the developer described a line ("2 left, 2 right").
    - C. free positions on a grid. Speculative.
  - **Record with the decision:**
    - The formation is battle state. `Combatants` stays the fixed roster, so events and the HUD keep their order, and positions come from `Formation`.
    - A fallen ally keeps their place and can be swapped with, so a body never blocks.
    - Placeholders (question 211): Move costs the mover's turn; the line doesn't change the party order after the battle.
    - `PlacesSwapped(Combatant Mover, Combatant Other)` is the event; the battle screen places battlers by formation position.
    - Enemies have no formation (question 224).
