# M3. The battle panel shows each member's resources, plainly

Part of [the class mechanics plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Structure" (Game), "Tunables and placeholders" (Screens). The look comes in LK1. Comes after MP2, which freed the MP row's place.

- **M3. The battle panel shows each member's resources where MP was, plainly** (Game, about 170 lines)
  - **Goal:**
    - Under a member's HP row, one row per class resource, in the old MP row's style: its name as the label, the amount large, "/max" small, and a thin bar.
    - Amounts are read live when the panel refreshes (ADR 0013).
    - A member with no resource shows no extra row. A class with two (the druid, later) gets two rows, and the panels stack by their own height.
  - **New types:**
    - Game `Battle/ResourceRowsText` (internal sealed): one member's resource amounts as text, re-formatted only when an amount changes, like `VitalsText`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `PartyStatusPanel` gains `ResourceRows` (one `BattleVitalRow` per resource) and refreshes them.
    - `PartyStatusView`:
      - draws them under HP;
      - stacks panels by a running top instead of one `_panelHeight`;
      - takes the rows' style from the old MP row's constants.
    - `UiTheme.BattleResourceBar`: a placeholder gradient (muted gold) until LK1's colors.
  - **Tests:**
    - `ResourceRowsTextTests`:
      - `Refresh_AmountChanged_Reformats`
      - `Refresh_AmountUnchanged_KeepsSameString` (starting from a state where the old value differs)
      - `Refresh_TwoResources_FormatsBoth`
    - The panel stacking is draw code, checked by hand.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - As Omer, MANA 3/10 under HP, 5/10 after his turn, lower after a spell.
    - As Zanyr, FAITH 5/5.
    - Ragnir (no resource yet) has no extra row.
    - Five members don't overlap the turn-order strip.
