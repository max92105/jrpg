# Z1. Run-through: every class's mechanic

Part of [the class mechanics plan](../README.md).

- **Z1. Run-through: every class's mechanic** (manual, the developer)
  - **Goal:** play each class once and decide what to keep, change or drop before rest (Phase 7) and the look (Phase 8). Answers to questions 208-227 go into the README's Decisions. A rule change becomes a slice added before Phase 7.
  - **Setup:**
    - A new game per leader, or one game recruiting each character from the start map's recruit points into a party of 5.
    - For long fights, add PT's `training_dummy` to the forest's encounters in the editor.
    - **Afterwards, revert any temporary encounter or content edit** (`git status` shows `content/` clean) before committing anything.
  - **Checklist** (each line: works / feels wrong / broken):
    - No MP anywhere: battle, menus, leader choice; an old save loads.
    - Mage: mana 3, +2 a turn, Meteor after saving; back to 3 next battle.
    - Cleric: faith 5; an ally's fall greys Heal All; heals never cost anything.
    - Fighter: stamina; Shield Bash stuns and the enemy loses its turn.
    - Barbarian: ferocity from hits; Taunt pulls every attack for two of his turns; Rampage.
    - Paladin: conviction per fallen enemy; Move through the line; Aura covers range 1, Wide Aura range 2.
    - Rogue: three Readies at speed 30; the extra turn shows in the strip and comes after the chosen combatant; Expose then hits land 1.5 times as hard.
    - Warlock: instability builds and drains; spells unlock; backfire at high instability.
    - Druid: Rot and Rejuvenate tick; dominance sticks on a tie; Equilibrium.
    - Monk: chakra per hit; Flurry's four numbers.
    - Nothing throws; panels, tags and strip stay readable with 5 members and 3 enemies.
  - **Record:** the date, the results and the developer's answers in this file's "Done" note; open bugs go to `docs/plans/backlog.md` or a fix slice.
