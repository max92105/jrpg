- **C2. Footer hints on `FooterBar.TextTop`, time shares on `Easing`** (Game, about 120 lines)
  - **Goal:** one way to center a footer hint and one way to compute an animation's share of its length, before the motion slices add more.
  - **Changes (Game):**
    - `Menus/GameMenuScene.DrawFooterHint`, `Battle/VictoryScreenView.Draw`, `Battle/DefeatScreenView.DrawFooter`: the hint's top comes from `FooterBar.TextTop(height, font.LineHeight)`. The menu rounded to whole pixels and `TextTop` doesn't: the menu hint may move by up to a pixel (the backlog's note); if it then looks blurred, round in `TextTop` for everyone.
    - `Ui/Easing.ShareOf` replaces the clamped shares in `Battle/ActionPlayback`, `Battle/FloatingNumber` (twice), `Battle/LevelUpPop`, `Battle/VictoryEntrance`, `Battle/DefeatEntrance`, `Battle/ResourceGaugeView.FlashShare` and `Battle/StatusMarksView.Share`; the private helpers go.
    - `Ui/Easing.CubicOut` replaces the cubic ease-outs written out in `LevelUpPop` and `VictoryEntrance`.
    - Remove the two footer lines and the easing line from `docs/plans/backlog.md`.
  - **Tests:** the existing ones pass unchanged (`FooterBarTests`, `EasingTests`, the victory, defeat, floating number and status mark tests). No new test: the helpers are already tested, and the callers' behavior doesn't change.
  - **Verify by hand:** before/after screenshots of the menu footer, the victory footer and the defeat footer; a battle's numbers, level-up pop and victory entrance animate as before.
- **Done, with deviations:** as planned. The three footer hints take their top from `FooterBar.TextTop`, unrounded, so the menu hint may sit half a pixel lower; the screenshot check decides whether `TextTop` should round. The nine clamped shares and the two cubic ease-outs go through `Ui/Easing`, and the private `ShareOf`, `EaseOut` and `Share` helpers are gone. The three backlog lines are removed; the review added one for the row-text centering written about 10 times. No new test; 2194 in the solution.
