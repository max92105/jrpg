# W2. The dialogue box in the frame language

Part of [the UI second pass plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables and placeholders" (dialogue box). After W1a. Look at the index's "dialogue box" section, `docs/ui-reference/dialog-confirm.webp` (the frame) and S0's dialogue captures first.

Decision 256: the new frame.

- **W2. The dialogue box in the frame language** (Game, about 130 lines)
  - **Goal:** the map's message box is the framed window: square corners, a darker translucent body, the outer hairline and the inner one with open corners, instead of the rounded panel with its 4 px white border. The speaker's strip, the diamond portrait, the typing and the blinking marker stay.
  - **Changes (Game):**
    - `Dialogue/DialogueBox`: its area becomes a static untitled `FramedWindowLayout` (1200 x 240, 40 px above the bottom, as today), drawn by a `FramedWindowView`; `TextInset` drops the old 4 px border from its sum (the comment says why). The portrait's white frame becomes the window's hairline color, so the box has one line weight.
    - `Ui/UiTheme` (dialogue part): `MessageBody` (`#B3000000`) replaces `MessagePanel`. `PanelView` stays for the interaction prompt and the key cap.
    - Rewrap the class summary (it describes the rounded panel).
  - **Tests:** none new (draw-only). `PanelViewTests`, `SpeechPlaybackTests` and `EventPresenterTests` pass unchanged.
  - **Verify by hand:** talk to the villager on the start map and read a found-item message: the box has the frame, text and portrait sit where they did, the speaker strip still sits above the top-left corner, the marker blinks at the bottom right; a cutscene's subtitle band (a different view) is unchanged.
