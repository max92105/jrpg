# ADR 0026: The stat model

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Before P1. Extends ADR 0017 (stats come from the class) and ADR 0019 (numbers in content). Decisions 267-273, 276, 278.

- [ ] **Write ADR 0026** with `/adr`, from the README's "ADRs" entry: A (primaries, percent-per-point conversions, rates in `rules.json`, limits in code; decided), B (derived stats straight on the class), C (ratings divided by level), D (conversion rates as code constants).
  - **Records (A):**
    - Five primaries (Power, Agility, Intelligence, Constitution, Will) are a class's base + growth × (level − 1) (ADR 0017's one formula) plus worn gear. No free points (decision 269).
    - Derived stats and their sources: the table in design.md, "The stat sheet". Speed and accuracy are their own stats from class and gear (decisions 271, 273).
    - Every conversion is a percent per point with no level divisor, applied to bases that grow (design.md, "Percent conversions"); rates and bases are `rules.json` `stats`, loaded into `Stats/StatRules`.
    - The battle's limits (hit floor and ceiling, dodge cap, resistance cap and floor, minimum damage, base crit damage 150 %) are named constants beside their formulas: members and enemies share them, and the battle takes no rules. Moving one to content later is a field and a parameter.
    - The scaling dimensions (decision 272): Power scales every raw value of a skill or attack, healing included, and crit damage; Intelligence a granted status's power, its turns (in breakpoints) and Stun's chance; Agility crit chance and dodge; Constitution max HP and the physical resistances; Will the class resource and the magical resistances. Consumables land their own numbers (decision 297).
    - Enemies have derived stats only (decision 275), crowd control resistance included (decision 300).
    - **Tenths of a percent**: content gives whole percents; Core holds every derived percent in permille (`CritChancePermille` 90 is 9 %) and screens show it rounded down to whole percents, so a small bonus (+5 Agility, +1.25 % crit) moves a stored number instead of vanishing in rounding.
    - Intelligence never adds turns to Stun (decision 293: `TurnFlow` skips a turn per Stun turn left, so extra turns would stun-lock).
    - Every class has mid-to-high Power (decision 294).
    - **Rolls draw only when the outcome is uncertain** (a chance of 0 or 100 calls no random), so certain fixtures keep seeded sequences.
    - A member is built with the rules (`PartyMember.Recruit(…, StatRules)`), since max HP depends on Constitution; the sheet is rebuilt on level-up and on equipment changes and is never saved.
  - **Consequences:** a new stat is a `StatKind` member, a sheet field, a formula and its rate; the Status page lists it. The loot plan's affixes are `StatBonuses` with rolled numbers.
