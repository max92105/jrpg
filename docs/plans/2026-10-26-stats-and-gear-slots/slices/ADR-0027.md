# ADR 0027: Damage types and resistances

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Before P3b. Decisions 292, 296, 299, 323, 331. Supersedes the defense part of the placeholder `DamageFormula` (plan 2026-10-17).

- [x] **Write ADR 0027** with `/adr`, from the README's "ADRs" entry: A (eight types in Core, one per hit, percent resistances replacing defense; decided), B (types as content), C (defense plus weakness tiers), D (defense and resistances together), E (diminishing returns, `armor / (armor + K)`).
  - **Records (A):**
    - `Stats/DamageType`: Blunt, Piercing, Slashing (physical), Fire, Ice, Lightning, Light, Dark (magical). A closed list: Constitution and Will's groups are rules in code.
    - Every hit has exactly one type, from content: a skill's or item's damage effect names it; a basic attack takes its weapon's, the unarmed type from `rules.json`, or the enemy's `damageType` (decision 292). Poison ticks and backfire are not hits and meet no resistance.
    - `Stats/Resistances`: one percent per type, kept within −100..75 % (a weakness at most doubles a hit; nothing is immune). The 75 % cap matches Path of Exile's default maximum resistance. A member's come from Constitution, Will and gear; an enemy's from content, absent meaning 0; a weakness is a resistance below 0 (decision 299).
    - Damage: `max(1, power × crit × (1000 − resistance) × (1000 + exposed) ÷ 1000³)`, percents in permille (ADR 0026).
    - **One rounding rule:** the product is computed in a `long` and rounded down once, at the end; no intermediate step rounds. A test stacks power 999, a crit, Exposed and a −100 % weakness to prove it doesn't overflow (H2).
    - Feedback (decision 323): a hit a negative resistance raised reads "Weak"; one a resistance at or above 25 % (`DamageFormula.ResistedThreshold`) lowered reads "Resisted" (D3).
    - Defense and attack leave the game (M2b); Guarded raises all eight resistances instead (decision 331).
  - **Why not E:** a rating isn't a percent the player can read on the Status page or compare on gear, and a weakness (below 0) has no natural place in `armor / (armor + K)`.
  - **Consequences:** a ninth type is an enum member, a resistance field, a `StatKind`, a name in `ui.json` and a choice of group.
  - Add its line to `docs/decisions/README.md`.
- **Done:** as planned (written from the README's ADRs entry and this slice's record, without `/adr`, since every option was already decided).
