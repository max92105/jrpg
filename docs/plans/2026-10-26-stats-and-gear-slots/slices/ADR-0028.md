# ADR 0028: Equipment slots

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Before G1a-1. Decisions 298, 322, 329. Extends ADR 0003 (equipping takes the copy out of the inventory).

- [x] **Write ADR 0028** with `/adr`, from the README's "ADRs" entry: A (nine slots as a Core enum, an item kind fitting one or two slots; recommended), B (slots in content), C (kind only; a member holds up to two rings as a list).
  - **Records (A):**
    - `Items/EquipmentSlot` is where a member wears something: Weapon, OffHand, Head, Chest, Hands, Feet, Amulet, FirstRing, SecondRing.
    - `Items/EquipmentKind` is what an item is worn as: Weapon, OffHand, Head, Chest, Hands, Feet, Amulet, Ring. `Items/EquipmentFit` says which slots a kind fits: a ring both ring slots, every other kind its own.
    - Equipping names the slot (`PartyEquipment.TryEquip(member, item, slot)`); an item that doesn't fit the slot is refused. Anyone wears anything (decision 165); taking from another member works per slot (decision 137).
    - Only the weapon gives the basic attack; the off-hand holds stats-only items, no second weapon (decision 322). The same ring may be worn twice (decision 329).
    - Saves key worn items by slot (`"worn": { "firstRing": … }`), format 4, older formats refused (ADR 0022). Today's armor is Chest.
  - **Consequences:** a tenth slot is an enum member, a fit, a label and a row on the Equipment page; two-handed weapons would be a fit rule that empties the off-hand.
  - Add its line to `docs/decisions/README.md`.
- **Done, with deviations:** written straight from the README's entry and this step, in ADR 0027's shape, without the `/adr` conversation: the developer chose A in the planning session, so there was nothing left to ask.
