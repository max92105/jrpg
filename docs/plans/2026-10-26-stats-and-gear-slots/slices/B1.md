# B1. Goblin trio simulation with hits and crits counted

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). ADR 0017 (a lone level 1 leader wins the start area's fights, question 184). Design: "Risks" (balance swings). After E2. `.claude/rules/tests.md`: simulations assert on aggregate outcomes and print a short summary.

- **B1. Goblin trio simulation with hits and crits counted; numbers recorded per class** (Simulations, about 120 lines)
  - **Goal:** `GoblinTrioSimulation`'s summary also prints, per class, the share of the leader's attacks that missed and that crit, and the average damage of a landed hit; the slice records the final table for all nine classes beside the tables P2, D2, M1, M2b, H1, H2 and I1 recorded, so the developer sees how the stat model moved the start area.
  - **Changes:** `tests/Jrpg.Simulations/Battle/GoblinTrioSimulation`: counts `HitMissed` and critical `EffectApplied` events from the leader (the events `Execute` returns), prints them; the assertion stays (every seed won). A second theory, `GoblinTrio_LoneLevelOneLeaderWithStartingGear_WinsEveryBattle`: the leader wears the new game's starting gear that fits (G2), to see gear matter.
  - **Tests:** the two theories above (nine classes each). The assertion can fail: a class that loses a seed fails it with the seed named.
  - **Record:** a table in this file's Done note: class, win rate, average turns, HP left, miss %, crit %, average hit, bare and geared. Any class near losing is a note for the developer, not a retune here (balance numbers are the developer's: propose, don't change).
  - **Verify by hand:** none beyond the run's output.
- **Done, with deviations:**
  - **The tallies are the leader's** (a hit on an enemy, a miss on an enemy), not both sides' as H1 and H2 printed; `HitTally` also sums the damage landed. "Average hit" is HP really lost, so a goblin's last hit counts only what it had left (two hits of about 20 on a 30 HP goblin average 15), which is why gear barely moves it.
  - **Loadout** is a two-value enum (`Bare`, `StartingGear`), not a flag; the geared leader wears the first starting item that fits each slot, in the bag's order: bronze sword, leather armor, shield, cap, gloves, boots, amulet, the power ring and the sage ring.
  - **Tests:** the second theory, nine classes. Count: 2,576 → 2,585. Every class wins every seed, bare and geared; none is near losing (the mage, the closest, keeps 91 of 150 HP bare and 107 geared on average).
