# D1. Every hit has a damage type

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decision 292, ADR 0027. Design: "Content shapes", "Tunables" (damage types), "Core types" (`AttackEffect`). After E1.

- **D1. Every hit has a damage type; `CombatStats` becomes a sealed record** (Core + Data + content, about 245 lines)
  - **Goal:** content declares a type on every damage effect, on every weapon and on every enemy; content without one is refused at start, naming the file and field. In battle, a basic attack carries its weapon's type, the unarmed type from `rules.json`, or the enemy's. Nothing reads the type yet (D2 does); this slice is the content and its validation, and the attack's effect moving out of `BattleState`.
  - **New types:**
    - Core `Battle/AttackEffect` (internal static): a combatant's basic attack as an effect: its attack power and type against a single enemy (`Of(Combatant attacker)`), out of `BattleState.Attack`.
    - Core `Battle/MemberBattleStats` (internal static): a member's battle stat line from what they are and wear (today's `TotalStats` plus the attack type from the worn weapon or the rules); M1, M2b and H1-I1 fill the rest from the sheet. In `Battle/`, not `Party/`, so `PartyMember` doesn't grow battle rules.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Effects/Effect`: gains `DamageType? DamageType = null`, the last parameter (no churn at the 54 test sites that build effects). A damage effect without one meets no resistance (D2): content always sets it, tests needn't.
    - `Data/Effects/EffectValidator`: a damage effect needs `damageType`; any other kind must not have one.
    - `Items/EquipmentStats`: gains `DamageType?` (init); `ItemCatalogValidator`: a weapon needs one, nothing else may have one.
    - `Battle/EnemyDefinition`: gains required `DamageType`; `EnemyCatalogValidator`; `EnemyDefinitionBuilder` default Blunt.
    - `Stats/StatRules.UnarmedDamageType`; `rules.json`; `GameRulesValidator` (required).
    - `Stats/CombatStats`: becomes a `public sealed record` (from a positional `readonly record struct`): every later slice adds init properties with neutral defaults (accuracy 100 %, crit damage 150 %), and a struct's `default` would silently give 0 accuracy. Its positional `Attack` and `Defense` stay until M2b, so the 31 `new CombatStats(` sites compile unchanged; only struct-only uses change (none expected: say so in the Done note). Gains `AttackType` (init, default Blunt); `Combatant.FromPartyMember` through `MemberBattleStats.Of(member)`: the worn weapon's type, else the rules'; `FromEnemy`: the definition's.
    - `content/skills.json`, `items.json` (bombs, three weapons), `enemies.json`: the design's types.
  - **Tests:**
    - `AttackEffectTests: Of_MemberWithSword_IsSlashing`
    - `AttackEffectTests: Of_UnarmedMember_IsRulesType`
    - `AttackEffectTests: Of_Enemy_IsItsDamageType`
    - `EffectValidatorTests: FindProblem_DamageWithoutType_NamesDamageType`
    - `EffectValidatorTests: FindProblem_HealWithType_NamesDamageType`
    - `ItemCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_WeaponWithoutDamageType_NamesTheField`
    - `ItemCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_ArmorWithDamageType_NamesTheField`
    - `EnemyCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_MissingDamageType_NamesTheField`  - **Call sites:** `Effect(` 54 unchanged; `BattleState.Attack` 1; enemies built outside `EnemyDefinitionBuilder` about 7 (`EnemyCatalogValidatorTests` 4, `BattleSceneTests` 2, `GameSessionBattleTests` 1); Data test JSON fixtures that declare damage effects or weapons (grep `tests/Jrpg.Data.Tests/TestContent` for `"damage"` and `"weapon"`; count in the Done note).
  - **Size:** about 80 Core (the two new types 45, the record 10, the rest), 40 Data and content, 8 tests (about 105), fixture JSON about 15: about 240. (H1 tests the record's neutral accuracy, the first default that matters.)
  - **Verify by hand:** the game starts; remove a skill's `damageType` and the start stops naming `skills.json` and the skill.
- **Done, with deviations:** the missing enemy type is refused while `enemies.json` is read (`required`, like every
  enemy field), so its test is `ContentFileReaderTests.Read_EnemyWithoutDamageType_ThrowsNamingTheFileAndField` with a
  new fixture, and `EnemyCatalogValidator` is unchanged; the same holds for `stats.unarmedDamageType`, a required
  constructor parameter, so `GameRulesValidator` is unchanged. `PartyMember`'s private rules field became an internal
  `Rules` property, which `MemberBattleStats` reads. No struct-only use of `CombatStats` existed. No Data fixture
  declared a weapon or damage effect that a validator reads; four validator tests about other problems gave their damage
  effects a type.
