# D2. Resistances reduce damage of their type

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decisions 292, 299, ADR 0027. Design: "Formulas" (`DamageFormula`), "Tunables" (enemies). After D1.

- **D2. Resistances reduce damage of their type; enemy weaknesses in content** (Core + Data + content, about 200 lines)
  - **Goal:** a hit meets the target's resistance to its type: 25 % Fire resistance takes a quarter off a fire hit, −25 % adds a quarter. A member's resistances come from their sheet (Constitution, Will, gear); an enemy's from `enemies.json` (absent is 0). Defense still subtracts first until M2b.
  - **New type:** Core `Effects/Mitigation` (public sealed record): what stands between a hit and the target's HP: `Defense` (until M2b), `ResistancePermille`, `TakenPermille` (Exposed); `None` (a record class, like `CombatStats`, so no zero `default`).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Effects/DamageFormula.Calculate(int power, Mitigation mitigation)`: `max(1, (power − defense) × (1000 − resistance) × (1000 + exposed) ÷ 1000²)`, percents in permille (ADR 0026), the product in a `long` rounded down once (ADR 0027), the resistance clamped to −100..75 % (`Resistances.Floor`, `Cap`); the comment gives the order's reason (a weakness multiplies what gets through, like Exposed).
    - `Effects/EffectResolver.Apply(Effect effect, Vitals target, Mitigation mitigation)`: replaces the defense and percent parameters; `Party/FieldItemUse` passes the member's defense in a `Mitigation` (no field item damages today; M2b leaves it `Mitigation.None`).
    - `Battle/EffectLanding.LandHits`: the target's `CurrentStats.Resistances.Of(type)` for a typed hit, 0 for an untyped one.
    - `Stats/CombatStats`: gains `Resistances` (init, default `None`); a member's from the sheet, an enemy's from `EnemyDefinition.Resistances` (init, default `None`); `EnemyCatalogValidator`: each within −100..75, named.
    - `content/enemies.json`: goblin Fire −25; training dummy Blunt and Piercing 25, Fire −50.
  - **Tests:**
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_FiftyPercentResistance_HalvesWhatGetsThrough`
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_NegativeResistance_RaisesDamage`
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_ResistanceAboveCap_UsesCap`
    - `BattleResistanceTests: Execute_FireSkillOnFireWeakEnemy_DealsMore`
    - `BattleResistanceTests: Execute_AttackOnSlashingResistantEnemy_DealsLess`
    - `BattleResistanceTests: Execute_MemberWithWillResistsFireHit`
    - `EnemyCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_ResistanceAboveCap_NamesTheType`
    - `EnemyCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_ResistanceBelowFloor_NamesTheType`
  - **Call sites:** `DamageFormula.Calculate` 7 (all in `DamageFormulaTests`), `EffectResolver.Apply` 3 (`EffectLanding`, `FieldItemUse`, `EffectResolverTests`).
  - **Size:** about 70 Core, 20 Data and content, 8 tests (about 105), 10 call-site lines: about 205.
  - **Verify by hand:** Fire Blast on a goblin hits a quarter harder than before (compare the number with the slice before); the training dummy takes less from a sword. The goblin trio simulation: record the table.
