# D3. "Weak" and "Resisted" cues

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decision 299 (answers 291), ADR 0027. Design: "Formulas" (`HitAffinity`), "Tunables" (`ResistedThreshold`). After D2.

- **D3. "Weak" and "Resisted" cues by the floating number** (Core + Game, about 150 lines)
  - **Goal:** when a resistance below 0 raised a hit, a small "Weak" cue shows by its floating number; when a resistance at or above 25 % lowered it, "Resisted". A hit between the two shows the number alone. Core decides which (a rule); the screen only shows it.
  - **New type:** Core `Effects/HitAffinity` (public enum): `Neutral`, `Weak`, `Resisted`: what the target's resistance did to a hit; `DamageFormula.AffinityOf(resistancePermille)` decides it with `ResistedThreshold` (25 %, a named constant beside the formula, ADR 0026's limits rule).
  - **Changes:**
    - Core `Battle/EffectApplied`: gains `Affinity` (`HitAffinity`, last parameter, default `Neutral`, so the test sites building it don't change); `EffectLanding` sets it for each damage hit.
    - Game `Battle/ActionPlayback.AddLanding`: a Weak or Resisted landing adds a cue beside its number (`battle_weak` "Weak", `battle_resisted` "Resisted" in `ui.json` and `MessageIds`); `Battle/FloatingNumbersView` or the cue placement puts it just above the number, in the number's stack.
  - **Tests:**
    - `DamageFormulaTests: AffinityOf_NegativeResistance_IsWeak`
    - `DamageFormulaTests: AffinityOf_JustBelowThreshold_IsNeutral`
    - `DamageFormulaTests: AffinityOf_AtThreshold_IsResisted`
    - `BattleResistanceTests: Execute_FireSkillOnFireWeakEnemy_ReportsWeak`
    - `ActionPlaybackTests: Read_WeakHit_ShowsWeakCueByTheNumber`
  - **Call sites:** `EffectApplied(` about 30 (unchanged: the parameter defaults).
  - **Size:** Core raw about 35 (non-view); Game raw about 35 (playback 15, placement 20), at 1.6x about 55; messages 6; 5 tests about 65: about 160.
  - **Watch:** `ActionPlayback.cs` (234).
  - **Verify by hand:** Fire Blast on a goblin (Fire −25) shows "Weak"; the magic staff (Blunt) on the training dummy (Blunt 25) shows "Resisted"; a plain hit shows only the number. F12.
