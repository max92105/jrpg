# G1a-1. Items have a kind; armor becomes Chest

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decision 298, ADR 0028. Design: "Content shapes", "Replacement mapping". After ADR 0028 and W1b. The review split the old G1a: this half is mechanical, G1a-2 adds the slots.

- **G1a-1. Items have a kind; armor becomes Chest; `"slot"` becomes `"kind"`** (Core + Data + content + Game, mechanical, about 150 lines)
  - **Goal:** an item says what it is worn as (`kind`); today's two slots are Weapon and Chest; nothing a player sees changes except the slot's name ("Chest" for "Armor"). Every kind exists in the enum, but no content uses the new ones until G2, and members still have two slots until G1a-2.
  - **New types:**
    - Core `Items/EquipmentKind` (public enum): what an item is worn as: Weapon, OffHand, Head, Chest, Hands, Feet, Amulet, Ring.
    - Core `Items/EquipmentFit` (public static): which slots a kind fits (`SlotsFor(kind)`, `Fits(kind, slot)`): here Weapon → Weapon and Chest → Chest; the other kinds fit nothing until G1a-2 (an item of them can't be equipped, and the Data validator refuses them in content until then).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Items/EquipmentSlot`: `Armor` is renamed `Chest`.
    - `Items/EquipmentStats`: `Slot` → `Kind`; `content/items.json` `"slot"` → `"kind"` (armor → chest); `ItemCatalogValidator`: a kind is required; a weapon's damage only on the weapon kind.
    - `Party/PartyEquipment.OwnedFor(slot)`: items whose kind fits the slot (through `EquipmentFit`).
    - `Session/GameSession.RestoreMember`, `Data/Saving/SaveGameRepair`, `Party/PartyMember`: `Armor` reads as `Chest` (`CaptureProgress` still writes `ArmorId` until G1b).
    - Game: `Menus/EquipmentSheet.LabelIdOf`: `slot_chest` ("Chest") replaces `slot_armor`; `Menus/InventoryFilter`: the Weapons tab is the weapon kind, the Armor tab every other kind.
    - Test support: `ItemDefinitionBuilder.AsArmor` → `AsChest`; `AsWorn(EquipmentKind kind)`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `EquipmentFitTests: SlotsFor_Chest_IsChest`
    - `EquipmentFitTests: Fits_WeaponInChest_IsFalse`
    - `ItemCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_MissingKind_NamesTheField`
    - `InventoryFilterTests: Matches_ArmorTab_ListsAChestPiece` (replaces the slot-based one)
  - **Call sites:** `EquipmentSlot.Armor` and `AsArmor` 18 in 6 test files; `EquipmentStats.Slot` (validator, `InventoryFilter`, `PartyEquipment`, repair) about 8; `"slot"` in Data test JSON fixtures (grep `TestContent`; count).
  - **Size:** about 40 Core, 25 Data and content, 10 Game, 4 tests about 50, call sites about 30: about 155. Record the test count before and after (four more).
  - **Verify by hand:** the Equipment page equips a sword and chain mail (now "Chest"); the inventory's Armor tab lists the three chest pieces.
- **Done, with deviations:**
  - **`PartyMember.Armor` is `Chest`** (the property, `Worn.Chest`, every slot switch); `CaptureProgress` still writes `ArmorId` until G1b. `SlotOf(item)` and `PartyEquipment.TryEquip` take the first slot the kind fits (a list pattern), so a kind that fits nothing yet is refused with false, and `SheetWith` throws for it.
  - **Missing kind:** the JSON reader, not the validator, names a missing `kind` (the record's required parameter), as it did the slot: `ContentFileReaderTests.Read_MissingRequiredField_ThrowsNamingTheFileAndField` on the renamed `missing-kind.json` fixture. The validator's new rule is a kind that fits no slot yet (`Validate_KindWithoutSlotYet_NamesTheKind`).
  - **Labels:** `slot_armor` became `slot_chest` ("Chest"); the inventory's `inventory_armor` tab label stays "Armor" (it lists every non-weapon piece).
  - **Call sites:** `EquipmentSlot.Armor` and `AsArmor` 18 in 9 test files (sed), the three `member.Armor` asserts, 8 `.Slot` reads in source; no test JSON fixture carried a slot.
  - **Tests:** `EquipmentFitTests` 3 (the two planned plus a ring fitting nothing yet), `ItemCatalogValidatorTests` 1, `PartyEquipmentTests` 1 (the review: a ring, fitting nothing yet, is refused and nothing moves); the two `InventoryFilterTests` renamed to say kind. Count: 2,549 → 2,554.
  - **Hand check (yours):** the Equipment page equips a sword and chain mail (now "Chest"); the inventory's Armor tab lists the three chest pieces.
