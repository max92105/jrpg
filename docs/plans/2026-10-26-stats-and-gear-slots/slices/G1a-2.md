# G1a-2. Members wear nine slots

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decisions 298, 322 (off-hand: stats only), 329 (the same ring twice), 137, 165, ADR 0028. Design: "Replacement mapping". After G1a-1.

- **G1a-2. Members wear nine slots, a ring kind in two; `TryEquip` names the slot** (Core + Game, about 240 lines)
  - **Goal:** a member has nine slots and wears an item only in a slot its kind fits; a ring in either ring slot (two copies of one ring allowed: decision 329); an off-hand item gives stats only (decision 322). The Equipment page still lists Weapon and Chest (G1c lists nine); saves keep the weapon and the chest only until G1b (known gap: an item worn in another slot in a test save is lost on load until G1b; no content item of the new kinds exists before G2).
  - **New type:** Core `Party/WornEquipment` (internal sealed): a member's nine slots: what is in each, and the bonuses of everything worn; out of `PartyMember`, which keeps `EquippedIn(slot)`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Items/EquipmentSlot`: the nine slots (Weapon, OffHand, Head, Chest, Hands, Feet, Amulet, FirstRing, SecondRing).
    - `Items/EquipmentFit`: every kind fits its slot; Ring fits FirstRing and SecondRing. `ItemCatalogValidator` stops refusing the new kinds.
    - `Party/PartyMember`: `Weapon`, `Armor`, `FillSlot` and the two-slot switches go for `WornEquipment`; the sheet sums its bonuses; `Battle/MemberBattleStats` reads the weapon from `EquippedIn(EquipmentSlot.Weapon)`.
    - `Party/PartyEquipment`: `TryEquip(member, item, slot)` (refuses an item that doesn't fit the slot); `TakeableFor` and `TryTakeFrom` unchanged per slot.
    - Game `Menus/EquipmentPage.Apply`: passes the slot.
  - **Tests** (`EquipmentFitTests`, `PartyEquipmentSlotTests`):
    - `EquipmentFitTests: SlotsFor_Ring_IsBothRingSlots`
    - `EquipmentFitTests: Fits_HelmInChest_IsFalse`
    - `PartyEquipmentSlotTests: TryEquip_RingInSecondRing_WearsIt`
    - `PartyEquipmentSlotTests: TryEquip_SameRingTwice_WearsBoth`
    - `PartyEquipmentSlotTests: TryEquip_HelmInChestSlot_RefusesAndChangesNothing`
    - `PartyEquipmentSlotTests: TakeableFor_SecondRing_ListsOtherMembersRings`
    - `PartyMemberGearTests: Wear_ShieldInOffHand_AddsItsResistancesNotAnAttack`
  - **Call sites:** `TryEquip(` 27 in 9 test files (the slot argument added); `EquipmentSlot` 62 occurrences in 17 files (most unchanged).
  - **Size:** about 85 Core (`WornEquipment` 50, `PartyMember` net −15, the rest), 5 Game, 7 tests about 90, call sites about 30 plus fit changes: about 240.
  - **Watch:** `PartyMember.cs` shrinks here (the slot switches leave); `PartyEquipmentTests` (203) and `PartyEquipmentTakeTests` (173) take only call-site edits.
  - **Watch (P4b review):** worn bonuses are summed in two places, `PartyMember.WornBonuses` and `SaveGameRepair.RepairMember`; the nine slots must reach both, or one shared sum.
  - **Verify by hand:** the Equipment page still equips a sword and chain mail; a test-built ring equips in either ring slot (Core tests; by hand from G2).
- **Done, with deviations:**
  - **`TryEquip` keeps a two-argument form** beside `TryEquip(member, item, slot)`: it equips in the first slot the kind fits (a ring in the first ring slot), which is what every test site and a future auto-equip want; the Equipment page names the slot. So the 40 test call sites didn't change. `PartyMember.SheetWith(item)` previews the same first slot; the page's ring rows get a slot-named preview in G1c.
  - **`PartyMember.Weapon` stays** (a read of the weapon slot: the basic attack's weapon, read by `MemberBattleStats` and about twenty tests); `Chest` went, its five test reads now `EquippedIn(EquipmentSlot.Chest)`. `PartyMember.cs` 265 → 241 lines.
  - **`WornEquipment`** holds the nine slots in a dictionary and sums the bonuses once; `With(slot, item)` copies it for a preview. `SaveGameRepair` still sums the two saved slots itself until G1b, which gives it the nine (the P4b watch).
  - **G1a-1's transitional pieces went:** the validator's "fits no slot yet" rule and its test, `EquipmentFitTests`' empty-ring test, `PartyEquipmentTests`' refused-ring test.
  - **Tests:** `EquipmentFitTests` 2 new, `PartyEquipmentSlotTests` 5 (the four planned plus the two-argument form taking the first ring slot; the review made the same-ring test prove its +5 Power counts twice), `PartyMemberGearTests` 1. Count: 2,554 → 2,559.
  - **Hand check (yours):** the Equipment page still equips a sword and chain mail; a ring equips in either ring slot by hand only from G2.
