# H1. Accuracy against dodge

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decisions 295, 297, 325 (only damaging hits roll), 326 (a miss is not a hit), ADR 0026 (rolls draw only when uncertain; permille). Design: "Formulas" (`HitFormula`, rolls), "Risks" (random draw order). After M2b.

- **H1. Accuracy against dodge: hits can miss, and the battle screen says so** (Core + Data + content + Game, about 240 lines)
  - **Goal:** every damaging hit rolls `clamp(accuracy − dodge, 20 %, 100 %)`; a miss deals nothing, sets off no hit trigger, and a damage effect that missed every hit grants no status. The battle screen shows "Miss" over the target. Enemies get `accuracy` and `dodge` in content.
  - **New types:**
    - Core `Effects/HitFormula` (internal static): hit chance in permille from accuracy and dodge, with `FloorPercent` 20 and `CeilingPercent` 100 and the reasoning (decision 297: accuracy already holds the base).
    - Core `Battle/ChanceRolls` (internal sealed): the battle's percent rolls over its `IRandom`; `Succeeds(int permille)` draws only when 0 < permille < 1000 (ADR 0026), so certain fixtures keep their seeded sequences.
    - Core `Battle/HitMissed` (public sealed record, a `BattleEvent`): a hit that missed its target.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/CombatStats`: gains `AccuracyPermille` (init, default 1000) and `DodgePermille` (init, default 0); a member's from the sheet; an enemy's from `EnemyDefinition.Accuracy` and `Dodge` (whole percents in content, required; validator: accuracy 0..200, dodge 0..`DodgeCap`; `enemies.json` goblin 85 / 5, dummy 80 / 0; `EnemyDefinitionBuilder` 100 / 0).
    - `Battle/EffectLanding`: built with `ChanceRolls`; each hit of a damage effect rolls first; `HitMissed` on a miss; the status of an all-missed damage effect isn't granted. `BattleState` builds the rolls from its random (constructor unchanged).
    - Game `Battle/ActionPlayback.Read`: `HitMissed` adds the impact beat once and a cue "Miss" on the target (`battle_miss` in `ui.json`, `MessageIds`).
    - Game tests' `BattleScenarios` (review edit): the member is recruited under a Game-tests copy of `TestStatRules.Neutral` (every rate 0, base accuracy 100, base crit 0) instead of the repository's rules, and the goblin is a scenario copy (`with { Accuracy = 100, Dodge = 0 }`), so every roll in the 65 scenario uses is certain and their seeded sequences hold. Scenario tests that read a member's numbers from the repository's rules (HP, say) are listed and re-pinned.

  - **Size:** Core raw about 85, Data and content 20 (non-view); Game raw: playback 12, scenarios 15 (non-view: tests), so the 1.6x applies to about 12 lines (→ 20); 10 tests about 130: about 255. If it passes 260, `ChanceRolls` with its three tests goes first.

- **Done, with deviations:**
  - **The goblin hits at 75 and never dodges** (not 85 / 5): under 85 / 5 the mage lost 5 of 1,000 seeds and the warlock 2, and under 75 / 5 the mage still lost 3; 75 / 0 wins every seed for every class. Members miss it 10 % of the time (base accuracy 90), it misses them about 25 %. The dummy stays 80 / 0.
  - **`BattleScenarios.CertainRules`** is the repository's rules with certain hits (`BaseAccuracy` 100, `BaseDodge` 0, `DodgePer100Agility` 0) and `BattleScenarios.Goblin` the repository's goblin at 100 / 0, not a neutral copy: HP, damage and every other number stay the repository's, so no scenario test had to be re-pinned.
  - **`ActionPlayback`**: the live-state filter moved from `Read` into `Start`'s loop, since the `HitMissed` case put `Read` at complexity 11.
  - **Small additions:** `RangeProblems.Within` (Data, for the enemy's accuracy and dodge ranges), `EnemyDefinitionBuilder.WithDodge` (accuracy 100, dodge 0 by default), `EffectLanding` built with `ChanceRolls`.
  - **Tests:** `HitFormulaTests` 3 (the difference, the floor, the ceiling), `ChanceRollsTests` 4 (1000 and 0 draw nothing; 500 succeeds on 499 and fails on 500), `CombatStatsTests` 1, `BattleHitTests` 6 (the review added a low-accuracy miss on each side and one landing hit of two still granting its status, with `EnemyDefinitionBuilder.WithAccuracy`), validator rows 2, `ActionPlaybackTests.Start_HitMissed_AddsMissCueOnTheImpact`.
