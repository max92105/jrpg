# H2. Critical hits

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decisions 295, 296, 300, 325 (only damaging hits crit), ADR 0027 (one rounding rule). Design: "Formulas" (units and rounding, `DamageFormula`), "Tunables". After H1.

- **H2. Critical hits; a critical number stands out; the overflow test** (Core + Data + content + Game, about 215 lines)
  - **Goal:** every damaging hit that lands rolls its user's crit chance; a crit multiplies the hit's power by the crit damage (150 % plus Power's share for a member, 150 % for an enemy) before resistance, in the one `long` product rounded down once. The floating number of a crit is bigger and in the critical color. Heals don't crit (decision 325).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/CombatStats`: gains `CritChancePermille` (init, default 0) and `CritDamagePermille` (init, default 1500, `StatFormula.BaseCritDamagePercent` × 10); a member's from the sheet, an enemy's chance from `EnemyDefinition.CritChance` (whole percent, required; validator 0..100; `enemies.json` goblin 5, dummy 0; builder 0).
    - `Effects/DamageFormula.Calculate(int power, Mitigation mitigation, int critPermille)`: the crit joins the one product (`power × crit × (1000 − resistance) × (1000 + exposed) ÷ 1000³` in a `long`); three parameters.
    - `Battle/EffectLanding`: after a landed hit, `ChanceRolls.Succeeds(critChance)`; `Battle/EffectApplied` gains `IsCritical` (default false, so the test sites building it don't change).
    - Game `Battle/FloatingNumberTone`: `Critical`; `ActionPlayback.ToneOf` picks it for a critical `EffectApplied`; `FloatingNumbersView`: a critical number at 1.4x the size, in a new `UiTheme.Battle` critical color (placeholder warm gold).
    - Game tests' `BattleScenarios`: already neutral (H1: base crit 0, the scenario goblin's `CritChance` 0).
  - **Tests:**
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_Crit150_RaisesHalf`
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_CritThenResistance_RoundsDownOnce` (a case where rounding each step would lose a point)
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_Power999CritExposedWeakness_DoesNotOverflow` (power 999, crit damage 1500 + a large Power share, Exposed 100 %, resistance −100 %: the exact expected value, review edit)
    - `BattleCritTests: Execute_CritRollSucceeds_ReportsCritical` (fixed random)
    - `BattleCritTests: Execute_Heal_NeverCrits`
    - `BattleCritTests: Execute_EnemyCrit_UsesBaseCritDamage`
    - `ActionPlaybackTests: Read_CriticalHit_UsesCriticalTone`
  - **Call sites:** `EffectApplied(` about 30 (unchanged: the new parameter defaults); `DamageFormula.Calculate` 10 (its tests, `EffectResolver`); enemies outside the builder about 7.
  - **Size:** Core raw about 45, Data and content 10 (non-view); Game raw about 30 (tone 5, view 20, theme 5), at 1.6x about 50; 7 tests about 90; call sites about 15: about 210.
  - **Watch:** `ActionPlayback.cs`.
  - **Verify by hand:** fight goblins with the rogue (crit 9 %): now and then a bigger gold number; a crit on a fire-weak goblin is bigger still, with its "Weak" cue. The goblin trio simulation: every class still wins every seed; record the table with the crit share.
- **Done, with deviations:**
  - **`EffectResolver.Apply` takes the crit** (`effect, target, critPermille, mitigation`), so `DamageFormula.Calculate(power, critPermille, mitigation)` keeps its three parameters and the hit's own factors come before the target's; `DamageFormula.NormalHit` (1000) names a hit that didn't crit, which `FieldItemUse` passes. The product starts from `(long)power`, so the four factors never meet as an `int`.
  - **`ActionPlayback.ToneOf` reads the whole `EffectApplied`** (kind and `IsCritical`), not the kind alone.
  - **`FloatingNumber.SizeOf(tone)`** holds the 1.4x (a pure query the view multiplies into its scale), tested beside `ColorOf`.
  - **`BattleScenarios.CertainRules`** also zeroes `BaseCritChance` and `CritChancePer100Agility`, and the scenario goblin's `CritChance` is 0: no scenario test shifted.
  - **Simulation:** a `HitTally` (attacks, misses, crits) replaces the two counters; the table gains "Hits critical".
  - **Tests:** `DamageFormulaTests` 3 new (crit 150 raises half; crit then resistance rounds down once: 41 × 1.5 × 0.75 = 46, not 45; power 999, crit 6495, Exposed 100 %, weakness −100 % gives 25,954), `BattleCritTests` 5 (the planned three, a failed crit roll hitting normally, and the review's Power-raised crit damage: 20 Power at 100 per 100 crits the unarmed 10 for 17), `CombatStatsTests` 1, `FloatingNumberTests` 3, `ActionPlaybackTests.Start_CriticalHit_UsesCriticalTone`, validator row 1.
