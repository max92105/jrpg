# I1. Intelligence shapes statuses; Stun against crowd control resistance

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decisions 296 (Intelligence is shape), 302, 317 (Stun never gains turns), 324 (enemies get crowd control resistance), ADR 0021. Design: "Formulas" (`GrantScaling`, Stun), "Tunables" (statuses, enemies), "Contradictions". After H2.

- **I1. Intelligence shapes statuses (never Stun's turns); Stun's chance against crowd control resistance; enemies get it** (Core + Data + content + Game, about 245 lines)
  - **Goal:** a status a member's skill grants has its power raised by their status percent (Poison and Regen ticks, Exposed's %, Guarded's points, Stun's chance) and lasts their extra status turns longer (one per 25 Intelligence), **except Stun, whose turns never change** (decision 317: `TurnFlow` skips a turn per remaining Stun turn, so extra turns would stun-lock). Stun's power is now its chance: it lands with `scaled power − target's crowd control resistance` (0..100 %), else the screen says "Resisted". Enemies get a crowd control resistance in content (decision 324); the statuses that roll against it in this plan are **Stun** (the fighter's Shield Bash here, the monk's Palm Strike from I2) and, from I2, an enemy ignoring a member's **Taunt**. Enemies grant nothing yet, so their status percent is 100 and their extra turns 0.
  - **New types:**
    - Core `Statuses/GrantScaling` (public static): a grant as its user's Intelligence shapes it (`Scaled(grant, statusPermille, extraTurns)`: power always, turns except on Stun), and Stun's landing chance (`StunChance(scaledPower, crowdControlResistancePermille)`), with the reasoning (decision 296: durations in whole turns, so Intelligence works in breakpoints; decision 317).
    - Core `Battle/StatusResisted` (public sealed record, a `BattleEvent`): a status that didn't land on a target.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/CombatStats`: gains `StatusPermille` (default 1000), `ExtraStatusTurns` (0), `CrowdControlResistancePermille` (0); a member's from the sheet; an enemy's resistance from `EnemyDefinition.CrowdControlResistance` (whole percent, required; validator 0..100; `enemies.json` goblin 10, dummy 50; `EnemyDefinitionBuilder` 0).
    - `Battle/EffectScaling.ScaledFor`: also the grant, through `GrantScaling`.
    - `Battle/EffectLanding`: a Stun grant rolls `ChanceRolls.Succeeds(StunChance(…))`; on a failure `StatusResisted`, no `StatusApplied`.
    - `Statuses/StatusKind.Stun`'s summary and `StatusGrant.Power`'s: Stun's power is its chance in percent. `Data/Statuses/StatusGrantValidator`: a Stun's power 1..100. `content/skills.json`: shield_bash's stun power 60 (placeholder).
    - Game `Battle/ActionPlayback.Read`: `StatusResisted` adds a cue "Resisted" (`battle_resisted`, shared with D3).
    - Game tests: `BattleScenarios`' goblin copy gets crowd control resistance 0; a test that plays shield_bash on repository content now rolls 60 %: grep `shield_bash` in `tests/Jrpg.Game.Tests` and pin it with a fixed random (count in the Done note).
