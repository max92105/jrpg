# L1. Stat names; the leader choice shows the class's shape

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Design: "The pages" (leader choice). After P4. Look at `docs/ui-reference/menu-status.png` (spaced-caps labels beside large numbers) first.

- **L1. Stat names and number formats; the leader choice shows HP, speed and the five primaries** (Game + text, about 200 lines)
  - **Goal:** every stat has a player-facing name and one way of writing its value (a percent sign on percent stats, a sign on a bonus), shared by the leader choice, the Status page and the Equipment page. The leader choice shows LV, HP, speed and the five primaries at level 1, in two columns, instead of attack and defense.
  - **New types:**
    - Game `Ui/StatNames` (internal static): the message id of each `StatKind` (an exhaustive switch, like `Battle/StatusNames`).
    - Game `Ui/StatText` (internal static): a stat's value as text (`ValueOf(kind, value)`: a permille shown rounded down to a whole percent, "12 %", for percent kinds; "250" otherwise; ADR 0026) and a bonus as text (`BonusOf(kind, value)`: "+5", "−5 %").
  - **Changes:**
    - `content/text/ui.json` and `Ui/MessageIds`: twenty stat names (`stat_power` … `stat_dark_resistance`; `stat_hp` and `stat_speed` exist), `stat_percent` ("{value} %"); `stat_attack` and `stat_defense` stay until E1 and S1 stop using them, then go (M2b checks).
    - `NewGame/LeaderProfile`: the class's sheet at level 1 (`StatSheet.Of(ClassGrowth.StatsAt(class, 1), StatBonuses.None, rules)`; the catalog's rules); lines LV, HP, SPEED, then the five primaries.
    - `NewGame/LeaderProfileView`: two columns of four lines (`StatsTop` 560, `StatPitch` 56: four lines end at 784).
  - **Tests:**
    - `StatNamesTests: IdOf_EveryKind_IsInTheMessageTable`
    - `StatTextTests: ValueOf_FireResistance_EndsWithPercent`
    - `StatTextTests: ValueOf_NinetySevenPermille_ShowsNinePercent` (rounded down, never up)
    - `StatTextTests: BonusOf_NegativeDodge_HasMinusSign`
    - `LeaderProfileTests: Of_Character_ListsLevelHpSpeedAndPrimaries` (replaces `Of_Character_ListsLevelHpAttackDefense`)
  - **Call sites:** `LeaderProfile` 1; `LeaderProfileTests` 1 replaced.
  - **Size:** raw about 125 (`StatNames` 30, `StatText` 25, messages and ids 45 non-view; `LeaderProfile` 10 and the view's two columns 15), the view part at 1.6x (25 → 40), and 5 tests (about 65): about 205.
  - **Verify by hand:** the leader choice for the fighter, the mage and the rogue: eight lines in two columns, the numbers differing by class; nothing overlaps the portrait. F12.
