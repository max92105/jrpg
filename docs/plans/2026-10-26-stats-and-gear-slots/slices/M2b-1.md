# M2b-1. Hits ignore defense

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). ADR 0027 (resistances replace defense), decisions 299, 331 (Guarded raises all eight). Design: "Replacement mapping", "Tunables" (statuses, enemies). After M2a. Split from M2b on 2026-10-27: defense leaving members, items and the pages is M2b-2.

- **M2b-1. Hits ignore defense; Guarded raises resistances; enemies deal damage, the goblin retuned** (Core + Data + content + tests, about 250 lines)
  - **Goal:** a hit is its scaled power through resistance and Exposed; defense no longer takes anything off. Guarded raises every resistance by its power in points (still within the cap). An enemy's `attack` becomes `damage` and its `defense` goes. A member's defense still exists on the sheet and the pages, unread by the battle, until M2b-2.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Effects/Mitigation` and `DamageFormula`: `Defense` goes: `max(1, power × (1000 − resistance) × (1000 + exposed) ÷ 1000²)`, in a `long`, rounded down once; the class summary's "placeholder ported from the demo" becomes the ADR 0027 formula with its reasoning.
    - `Battle/EnemyDefinition`: `Attack` → `Damage`, `Defense` goes (validator, `enemies.json`, `EnemyDefinitionBuilder.WithStats(maxHp, damage)`; its `WithDefense` and its users go). **The goblin is retuned** (review edit): its `damage` becomes 9, so the fighter takes about what it takes today (attack 16 − defense 8 = 8; now 9 less the fighter's 3.5 % physical resistance, rounded down once, is 8). The training dummy's `attack` 20 becomes `damage` 13 by the same rule (20 − 8 = 12 today; 13 less 3.5 % is 12). The Done note gives each class's goblin hit before and after.
    - `Statuses/StatusModifiers.Apply`: Guarded raises `Resistances` by its power (`Resistances.Raised`); `Raised(int points)` and its test `ResistancesTests: Raised_TenPoints_RaisesEveryType` land here (P3b left them to their first caller); `StatusKind.Guarded`'s summary says so. `content/skills.json`: aura 20, wide aura 12 (placeholders).
    - `Party/FieldItemUse`: `Mitigation.None`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `StatusModifiersTests: Apply_Guarded_RaisesEveryResistance` (replaces the defense one)
    - `StatusModifiersTests: Apply_GuardedPastCap_StopsAtCap`
    - `BattleStatusTests: Execute_AttackOnGuardedAlly_DealsLess` (rewritten from defense to resistance; in a new `BattleGuardedTests` if `BattleStatusTests` passes 280)
    - `EnemyCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_NegativeDamage_NamesTheField`
    - The battle tests M2a kept on `WithDefense` (the Guarded ones, the poison one) rewritten for resistances; `DamageFormulaTests`' defense cases go.
  - **Verify by hand:** a fight against goblins: a goblin hits the fighter for about 8, as before; the paladin's aura lowers what allies take. The goblin trio simulation: every class wins every seed (if one doesn't, say so and propose a number; balance is the developer's); record the table.
- **Done, with deviations:**
  - **`FieldItemUse`:** its `Land` drops the target it no longer reads.
  - **Combat stats:** an enemy's `CombatStats` carries Defense 0 until M2b-2 removes it.
  - **New tests:**
    - The Guarded tests moved into a new `BattleGuardedTests` (`BattleStatusTests` was at 280): `Execute_AttackOnGuardedTarget_DealsLess` and `Execute_GuardedEnds_ResistancesBackToBase`, with Guard +25 so the goblin's 20 lands as 15.
    - `ResistancesTests.Raised_TenPoints_RaisesEveryType` and the two `StatusModifiersTests`.
    - `Validate_NegativeDamage` is a row of the existing invalid-field theory.
  - **Changed tests:**
    - The poison test is now `Execute_PoisonedActorEndsTurn_LosesPowerIgnoringResistancesAndExposed`: a Guarded hero still loses 7.
    - `DamageFormulaTests` loses its defense cases. It gains `Calculate_NothingInTheWay_LandsThePower`, `Calculate_OnePowerAtCap_ReturnsMinimumDamage` and `Calculate_Exposed50_RaisesByHalf`, and `Calculate_FiftyPercentResistance_Halves` loses its defense.
    - The count goes 2,478 → 2,477.
  - **The goblin's hit, before (attack 16 − class defense) → after (9 less Constitution's physical resistance):**

    | Class | Before | After |
    |---|---|---|

    The frail classes take less than before, because the class defense gap is gone and Constitution's 1.5 to 4 % barely separates them. That is a balance note for the developer.
