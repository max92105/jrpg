# P4. Worn gear adds to the sheet

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decisions 293, 295, 328. Design: "The stat sheet" (gear adds to primaries first), "Content shapes", "Tunables" (gear bonuses). After P3b.

- **P4. Worn gear adds to the sheet; max HP and speed follow it; `StatSheet.ValueOf`** (Core + Data + content, about 245 lines)
  - **Goal:** equipment carries `bonuses` by stat; what a member wears adds to their primaries (and so to everything those convert into) and to derived stats directly. Putting on Constitution raises max HP, taking it off lowers it (P2's `Vitals.ResizeMax`); speed gear raises the rogue's readiness charges. Any of the twenty stats reads through one `StatSheet.ValueOf(kind)`, so E1's comparison and S1's columns don't switch over them. Today's two slots still apply (nine come in G1a-2).
  - **New types:**
    - Core `Stats/StatKind` (public enum): the twenty stats a sheet shows and gear can add: the five primaries, MaxHp, Speed, Accuracy, CritChance, CritDamage, Dodge, CrowdControlResistance, and one resistance per damage type; `StatKinds.ResistanceTo(DamageType)` maps a type to its kind.
    - Core `Stats/StatBonuses` (public sealed record): what an item adds, by stat kind (a read-only dictionary from content, whole percents for percent kinds); `Of(kind)`, `Plus(StatBonuses)`, `None`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Items/EquipmentStats`: gains `Bonuses` (`StatBonuses`, init, default `None`); `AttackBonus` and `DefenseBonus` stay until M1 and M2b.
    - `Stats/StatSheet.Of(ClassStats atLevel, StatBonuses worn, StatRules rules)`: gear primaries add before the conversions, gear on a derived kind after (in permille: a whole-percent bonus × 10; dodge and resistances still capped). `StatSheet.ValueOf(StatKind)`: the value a screen shows for a kind (whole points, or permille for percent kinds; the one switch over the twenty).
    - `Party/PartyMember`: the sheet is rebuilt on `Wear` and `TakeOff`; max HP follows it through `Vitals.ResizeMax` (decision 328).
    - `Session/GameSession.RestoreMember`: wears the saved items before `RestoreProgress` sets HP, so a member saved with Constitution gear keeps their HP.
    - `Data/Saving/SaveGameRepair`: clamps HP against the max with the repaired worn items' bonuses.
    - `Data/Items/ItemCatalogValidator`: each bonus nonzero and within −100..100, the stat named; the JSON reads stat kinds in camelCase (`fireResistance`).
    - `content/items.json`: the design's bonuses on the three armors and the magic staff.
    - `ItemDefinitionBuilder.WithBonuses(StatBonuses)` (the old `WithBonuses(attack, defense)` becomes `WithAttackAndDefense` until M2b deletes it: 3 sites).
  - **Tests** (new ones in `StatSheetTests`, `PartyMemberGearTests`, `GameSessionGearTests`):
    - `StatSheetTests: Of_PowerBonus_RaisesPowerAndDamagePercent`
    - `StatSheetTests: Of_FlatFireResistance_AddsAfterWill`
    - `StatSheetTests: Of_DodgeBonusPastCap_StopsAtCap`
    - `StatSheetTests: ValueOf_EveryKind_ReadsItsField` (a theory over the twenty kinds against a sheet with distinct values)
    - `PartyMemberGearTests: Wear_ConstitutionArmor_RaisesMaxHpKeepsHp`
    - `PartyMemberGearTests: TakeOff_ConstitutionArmor_ClampsHpToNewMax`
    - `PartyMemberGearTests: Wear_SpeedBonus_RaisesSpeed`
    - `GameSessionGearTests: Load_HpAboveClassMaxWithConstitutionArmor_KeepsHp`
    - `ItemCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_ZeroBonus_NamesTheStat`
    - `SaveGameRepairGearTests: Repair_HpWithinMaxWithGear_KeepsHp` (a new file: `SaveGameRepairTests` is at 266)
  - **Call sites:** `ItemDefinitionBuilder.WithBonuses` 3; `EquipmentStats(` constructions 6 (unchanged: `Bonuses` is init).
  - **Size:** about 105 Core (`StatKind` 30, `StatBonuses` 25, `ValueOf` 25, the rest), 25 Data and content, 10 tests (about 130 with the theory): about 245.
