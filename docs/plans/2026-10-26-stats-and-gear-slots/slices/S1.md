# S1. The Status page shows the sheet

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decisions 260, 296 (results, never raw primaries alone), 316 (the layout), 327 (the worn list leaves). Design: "The pages" (Status page). After L1; S2 follows at once. Look at `docs/ui-reference/menu-status.webp` and a capture of today's Status page first.

- **S1. The Status page shows the sheet: attributes beside a smaller portrait, combat numbers, resistances** (Game, about 240 lines)
  - **Goal:** the profile band holds the name, class, LV and experience at the left, the **Attributes** column beside them (each primary with a caption of what it gives, from `StatFormula`: "+15 % damage, +7 % crit damage") and a 160 px diamond portrait at the right. Below, **Combat** (HP with its bar, speed, accuracy, crit chance, crit damage, dodge, crowd control) and **Resistances** (the eight in `DamageType` order) side by side. Resources and Skills follow. The worn list leaves the page. Plain rows (label, value, hairline: today's `DrawRow`); S2 does the look right after.
  - Until D2, H1 and H2 land, the battle doesn't use accuracy, crits, dodge or resistances yet; the page shows them already.
  - **The arithmetic, proven before drawing** (today's `StatusSheetView`: margin 28 × 20, portrait 240, section gap 18, section title 36, rows 46, skill rows 60; the page area beside the member column is 1366 × 848):
    - Rows in the stat columns become 36 px (values in the 26 px body font, labels 18 px); skill rows stay 60 px; the portrait becomes 160 px.
    - **Profile band**, from y = 20: the left block as today ends at 196 (name 0, class 42, experience title 84, labels 120, values 146, bar 190 + 6); Attributes = title 36 + 5 × 36 = 216; portrait 160. The band is 216 tall: ends at **236**.
    - Gap 18 → 254. **Combat** = 36 + 7 × 36 = 288 (ends 542) beside **Resistances** = 36 + 8 × 36 = 324: ends at **578**.
    - Gap 18 → 596. **Skills** = 36 + 3 × 60 = 216 (every class has three skills at most; the mage, cleric, paladin, warlock and druid have three) beside **Resources** = 36 + 2 × 36 = 108 (two at most: the mage, the druid): ends at **812**.
    - 812 ≤ 848: 36 px to spare. A fourth skill would need 60 more: past 848, so S1's Done note says skills keep "as many as fit" until a scrolling list comes.
    - Widths: 1366 − 2 × 28 = 1310 = profile 420 + gap 40 + Attributes 650 + gap 40 + portrait 160. Below: two columns of (1310 − 40) ÷ 2 = 635; the bottom band Resources 420, gap 40, Skills 850. An attribute row: label 160, caption about 300 at 18 px, value right-aligned: fits 650.
    - These become `StatusSheetView`'s and `StatusParametersView`'s named constants; a test checks the sum (below), so a later size change that overflows fails.
