# A1. Lifesteal

Part of [the affix stats plan](../README.md). Decisions 294, 346. After the loot plan.

- **A1. Lifesteal** (Core + content + Game, about 230 lines)
  - **Goal:** a member with lifesteal heals by its share of the HP each landed hit of their attacks and damaging skills took, rounded down, within max HP; never from an item (decision 321), never while fallen. The battle shows a heal number on them. Gear gets it only as the `life_steal` affix.
  - **New type:**
    - `Battle/LifeStolen(Combatant Actor, int Amount)`: the event; only when the amount is above 0.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/StatKind.LifeSteal`, appended after the resistances so the twenty keep their order; `StatSheet.LifeStealPermille` and `CombatStats.LifeStealPermille` (init, 0); `Party/MemberSheet` from the worn bonus (whole percents to permille); `Battle/MemberBattleStats` copies it.
    - `Effects/DamageFormula.LifeStolen(int hpTaken, int permille)`: `hpTaken × permille ÷ 1000`, rounded down, with the reasoning comment (a share of what the hit really took, so overkill steals nothing).
    - `Battle/EffectLanding`: after a landed damaging hit, a living actor heals `LifeStolen` within max HP and reports it. A new `LandFromItem(actor, effect, targets, events)` lands an item's effect without lifesteal (decision 321); `BattleState.UseItem` calls it (one line). Both entries share the hit loop through a private `HitSource(Combatant Actor, int LifeStealPermille)` record, keeping it at four parameters and with no flag argument.
    - `Game/Battle/ActionPlayback`: `LifeStolen` adds a heal number on the actor in the same beat (241 lines now).
    - Text: `stat_life_steal` ("Lifesteal"); `Ui/StatNames`.
    - Content: `affixes.json` `life_steal` (weapon, ring; 1-3 %).
  - **Tests:**
    - `DamageFormulaTests: LifeStolen_TenPercentOfThirtySeven_IsThree`
    - `DamageFormulaTests: LifeStolen_NoLifeSteal_IsZero`
    - `BattleLifeStealTests: Execute_AttackWithLifeSteal_HealsAttacker` (a fresh test file)
    - `BattleLifeStealTests: Execute_AtFullHp_ReportsNothing`
    - `BattleLifeStealTests: Execute_MissedHit_StealsNothing`
    - `BattleLifeStealTests: Execute_BombItem_StealsNothing`
    - `CombatantTests: FromPartyMember_LifeStealWorn_CopiedToStats` (the copy test, a fixture above 0)
    - `ActionPlaybackTests: Of_LifeStolen_AddsHealNumberOnActor`
  - **Call sites:** `StatNamesTests` covers the new kind by theory; `StatComparisonTextTests` take the first kinds by order (unchanged when appended).
  - **Watch:** `BattleState.cs` 269 (+0: one call renamed); `EffectLanding.cs` 111 (+25).
  - **Verify by hand:** with a `life_steal` ring worn (win until one drops, or a Debug save): attacking a goblin shows a small green number on the attacker when hurt.
