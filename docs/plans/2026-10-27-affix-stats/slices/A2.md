# A2. Armor penetration

Part of [the affix stats plan](../README.md). Decisions 294, 347, ADR 0027 (resistances). After A1. See the README's note: no enemy reachable in play has a positive resistance.

- **A2. Armor penetration** (Core + content + text, about 170 lines, plus the reachable enemy the note settles)
  - **Goal:** a member's hits ignore their armor penetration's share of the target's resistance when that resistance is positive; a weakness is untouched. The "Resisted" cue reads the resistance after penetration (a hit that penetrates below the threshold no longer reads "Resisted"). Gear gets it only as the `armor_penetration` affix.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/StatKind.ArmorPenetration` (appended); `StatSheet.ArmorPenetrationPermille`, `CombatStats.ArmorPenetrationPermille` (init, 0); `MemberSheet`; `MemberBattleStats` copies it.
    - `Effects/DamageFormula.PenetratedResistance(int resistancePermille, int penetrationPermille)`: a positive resistance × (1000 − penetration) ÷ 1000, rounded down; a resistance at or below 0 unchanged. Comment: why weaknesses aren't deepened.
    - `Battle/EffectLanding.MitigationOf` takes the actor's penetration (A1's `HitSource`).
    - Text: `stat_armor_penetration` ("Armor penetration"); `Ui/StatNames`.
    - Content: `affixes.json` `armor_penetration` (weapon; 3-8 %); the reachable enemy with a positive resistance (README note).
  - **Tests:**
    - `DamageFormulaTests: PenetratedResistance_PositiveResistance_LosesItsShare`
    - `DamageFormulaTests: PenetratedResistance_Weakness_Unchanged`
    - `DamageFormulaTests: PenetratedResistance_NoPenetration_Unchanged`
    - `BattlePenetrationTests: Execute_PenetrationAgainstResistance_DealsMore`
    - `BattlePenetrationTests: Execute_PenetratedBelowThreshold_NotResisted`
    - `CombatantTests: FromPartyMember_PenetrationWorn_CopiedToStats`
  - **Verify by hand:** with an `armor_penetration` weapon, hits on the enemy with a positive resistance deal more than without it, and "Resisted" shows less often.
