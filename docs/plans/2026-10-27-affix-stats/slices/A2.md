# A2. Armor penetration, and the stone beetles in the forest

Part of [the affix stats plan](../README.md). Decisions 294, 323, 347, 363, 364; ADR 0027 (resistances). After A1. Builds on design.md's tunables (the beetle) and "Key decisions".

- **A2. Armor penetration, and the stone beetles in the forest** (Core + content + Data tests + Simulations, about 200 lines: about 75 of code and content, 125 of tests)
  - **Goal:** a member's hits take their armor penetration in flat percentage points off the target's resistance when it is positive, after the 75 % cap, never below 0; a weakness is untouched (decision 364). The "Resisted" cue reads the resistance after penetration, so the beetle's 25 % read with 3-8 points no longer reads "Resisted". Gear gets it only as the `armor_penetration` affix. The forest now rolls the stone beetles (decision 363), the first enemy reachable in play with a positive resistance.
  - **New types:** none (`Battle/ForestFightsSimulation` is the renamed `GoblinTrioSimulation`).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/StatKind.ArmorPenetration` (appended), `StatKinds.AffixOnly` gains it; `StatSheet.ArmorPenetrationPermille` (init, 0) and its `AffixStatOf` arm; `CombatStats.ArmorPenetrationPermille` (init, 0); `MemberSheet`; `MemberBattleStats` copies it.
    - `Effects/DamageFormula.PenetratedResistance(int resistancePermille, int penetrationPermille)`: the resistance held at the cap (`Resistances.CapPercent`), then, when positive, less the penetration, never below 0; a resistance at or below 0 unchanged. Comment: points rather than a share, after the cap (decision 364, the ARPG convention), and why a weakness isn't deepened (penetration answers armor; it isn't extra damage).
    - `Battle/EffectLanding`: `HitSource` gains `PenetrationPermille` (`Land` from `CurrentStats`, `LandFromItem` 0, decision 321); `MitigationOf(target, type, source)` passes the resistance through `PenetratedResistance`, so `AffinityOf` reads the penetrated value.
    - Text: `stat_armor_penetration` ("Armor penetration"), `MessageIds`, `Ui/StatNames`.
    - Content: `affixes.json` `armor_penetration` (`armorPenetration`, weapon, 3-8, weight 10); `enemies.json` `stone_beetle` and `encounters.json` `stone_beetle` (two beetles) per design.md's tunables; `maps/forest.json` lists `goblin_trio` and `stone_beetle` (README, "Out of scope").
    - Simulations: `Battle/GoblinTrioSimulation.cs` becomes `Battle/ForestFightsSimulation.cs` (class and summary: "the forest's fights"); `AssertEveryBattleWon`, `RunBattle` and `WriteSummary` take the encounter id (each at most four parameters); the trio's two theories keep their names.
