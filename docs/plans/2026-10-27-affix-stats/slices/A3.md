# A3. Block, with its "Blocked" cue; the shield in the goblin's drops

Part of [the affix stats plan](../README.md). Decisions 294, 348, 366; ADR 0026 (rolls draw only when uncertain), ADR 0027 (one rounding). After A2. Builds on design.md's "Key decisions" and risks (overflow).

- **A3. Block, with its "Blocked" cue; the shield in the goblin's drops** (Core + content + Game, about 180 lines: about 55 of code and content, 125 of tests)
  - **Goal:** a landed damaging hit on a member with block rolls their block chance after the crit roll; a blocked hit deals `BlockedDamagePercent` (50, placeholder) of what it would, rounded down once, at least the minimum damage (decision 348, any type, against any landed hit). The battle shows "Blocked" by the target beside the number. No enemy has block, so no seeded fixture gains a draw. Gear gets it only as the `block` affix (off-hand), and the goblins now drop the wooden shield (decision 366), so it can roll in play.
  - **New type:**
    - `Battle/HitBlocked(Combatant Target)`: the event of a hit blocked, reported before the hit's `EffectApplied`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/StatKind.BlockChance` (appended), `StatKinds.AffixOnly` gains it; `StatSheet.BlockChancePermille` (init, 0) and its `AffixStatOf` arm; `CombatStats.BlockChancePermille` (init, 0); `MemberSheet`; `MemberBattleStats` copies it.
    - `Effects/Mitigation.IsBlocked` (init, false), which `DamageFormula.Calculate` reads, so `EffectResolver.Apply` keeps four parameters.
    - `Effects/DamageFormula.BlockedDamagePercent` (const 50): a blocked hit's share joins the one `long` product (ADR 0027) rather than rounding twice; the comment names the trade-off and the overflow bound (design.md's risks). The `Calculate` summary's formula gains the factor.
    - `Battle/EffectLanding`: after the crit roll, a damaging hit rolls the target's `BlockChancePermille` through `ChanceRolls` (no draw at 0 or 100 %); a block sets `IsBlocked` on the hit's `Mitigation` and reports `HitBlocked`. The class summary's roll order gains the block.
    - `Game/Battle/ActionPlayback.AddImpactCue`: `HitBlocked` is a cue (`MessageIds.BattleBlocked`), like `HitMissed`.
    - Text: `stat_block_chance` ("Block"), `battle_blocked` ("Blocked"); `MessageIds`; `Ui/StatNames`.
    - Content: `affixes.json` `block` (`blockChance`, offHand, 3-8, weight 10); `drop-tables.json` `goblin` gains `{ "itemId": "wooden_shield", "chance": 4 }` (decision 366).
  - **Tests:**
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_Blocked_HalvesDamageRoundedDown` (power 15 blocked is 7)
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_BlockedOneDamage_StaysAtMinimum`
    - `BattleBlockTests: Execute_CertainBlock_HalvesHitAndReportsBlocked` (a fresh file; a member with 100 % block draws nothing for it)
    - `BattleBlockTests: Execute_NoBlock_DrawsNothing` (a `FixedSequenceRandom` holding only the hit's own draws: a block roll would throw)
    - `BattleBlockTests: Execute_DrawBelowBlockChance_Blocked` (hit, crit, then a block draw one below the chance)
    - `BattleBlockTests: Execute_DrawAtBlockChance_NotBlocked` (the other side)
    - `CombatantTests: FromPartyMember_BlockWorn_CopiedToStats`
    - `StatSheetTests` and `ItemCatalogValidatorTests`: the `BlockChance` cases.
    - `ActionPlaybackAffixTests: Of_HitBlocked_AddsBlockedCue`
    - `RepositoryLootTests: GoblinDrops_IncludeAnOffHandThatCanRollBlock` (the goblin's table has an off-hand item, and the pool has a block affix for off-hands: decision 366 holds while content changes)
  - **Call sites:** `LootSimulation` reads the goblin's table from content: its summary gains a line, its assertions read content (no change). No repository test asserts a seeded victory's drops (the Game tests use their own tables or none; `BattleSceneTests.Update_AttackUntilOver_FinishesBattleAndShowsVictory` asserts only experience).
  - **Watch:** `EffectLanding.cs` (about 140 after A2, +10); `ActionPlayback.cs` (247 after A1, +1); `DamageFormulaTests.cs` (about 175 after A2, +20); `CombatantTests.cs` (about 225, +13).
  - **Verify by hand:** with a `block` shield worn (or a Debug save), goblins' hits now and then read "Blocked" and take about half; without one, never.
- **Done, with deviations:** the block roll is `EffectLanding.RollsBlocked`, set on the hit's mitigation with `with { IsBlocked = … }`, so `LandHits` stays under the complexity limit. `DamageFormula` multiplies by the blocked share in percent (`BlockedDamagePercent` 50 over a whole of 100) inside the one product; its summary gives the overflow bound (1.8 × 10^16 for a power of 10,000 at a 300 % crit against a −100 % resistance with 200 % taken). `Execute_CertainBlock_HalvesHitAndReportsBlocked` also asserts `HitBlocked` comes right before the hit's `EffectApplied`; the below/at tests give the goblin a 50 % crit that draws 999 first, so the crit-then-block order is pinned. The playback test is `Start_HitBlocked_AddsBlockedCue` (the class names its tests after `Start`). The review added `Execute_HealOnCertainBlocker_NotBlocked` (the damage-only guard).
