# A3. Block

Part of [the affix stats plan](../README.md). Decisions 294, 348, ADR 0026 (rolls draw only when uncertain). After A2.

- **A3. Block, with its "Blocked" cue** (Core + content + Game, about 230 lines)
  - **Goal:** a landed damaging hit on a member with block rolls their block chance after the crit roll; a blocked hit deals `BlockedDamagePercent` (50, placeholder) of what it would, rounded down, at least the minimum damage. The battle shows "Blocked" by the target. No enemy has block, so no seeded fixture gains a draw. Gear gets it only as the `block` affix (off-hand).
  - **New type:**
    - `Battle/HitBlocked(Combatant Target)`: the event, reported before the hit's `EffectApplied`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/StatKind.BlockChance` (appended); `StatSheet.BlockChancePermille`, `CombatStats.BlockChancePermille` (init, 0); `MemberSheet`; `MemberBattleStats` copies it.
    - `Effects/DamageFormula.BlockedDamagePercent` (const 50) applied inside the one rounding (ADR 0027): the block share joins the `long` product rather than rounding twice. The comment names the trade-off.
    - `Effects/Mitigation` gains `IsBlocked` (init, false), which `DamageFormula.Calculate` reads, so `EffectResolver.Apply`'s parameters stay at four.
    - `Battle/EffectLanding`: the block roll through `ChanceRolls` (draws only when 0 < chance < 100 %).
    - `Game/Battle/ActionPlayback`: `HitBlocked` is a cue (`MessageIds.BattleBlocked`, "Blocked"), as `HitMissed`.
    - Text: `stat_block_chance` ("Block"), `battle_blocked`; `Ui/StatNames`.
    - Content: `affixes.json` `block` (offHand; 3-8 %).
  - **Tests:**
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_Blocked_HalvesDamageRoundedDown`
    - `DamageFormulaTests: Calculate_BlockedOneDamage_StaysAtMinimum`
    - `BattleBlockTests: Execute_CertainBlock_HalvesHitAndReportsBlocked`
    - `BattleBlockTests: Execute_NoBlock_DrawsNothing` (an empty `FixedSequenceRandom` after the hit's own draws)
    - `BattleBlockTests: Execute_DrawAtBlockChance_NotBlocked` (the other side)
    - `CombatantTests: FromPartyMember_BlockWorn_CopiedToStats`
    - `ActionPlaybackTests: Of_HitBlocked_AddsBlockedCue`
  - **Watch:** `ActionPlayback.cs` (241 + 1 after A1's case).
  - **Verify by hand:** with a `block` shield worn, goblins' hits now and then read "Blocked" and take about half.
