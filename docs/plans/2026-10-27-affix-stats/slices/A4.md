# A4. Drop luck

Part of [the affix stats plan](../README.md). Decisions 294, 349, 356. After A3. See the README's note: drop luck must be visible.

- **A4. Drop luck** (Core + content + text + Simulations, about 220 lines)
  - **Goal:** the active members' drop luck, fallen members included (decision 356), adds up at `FinishBattle`; it raises every drop entry's chance and the weight of every rarity above Common by its percent (decision 349). Gear gets it only as the `drop_luck` affix. The loot simulation shows its effect.
  - **New type:**
    - `Loot/LootFormula`: drop chances and rarity weights under drop luck, one place. `ChancePermille(int chancePercent, int luckPermille)`: the chance × (1000 + luck) ÷ 1000, capped at 100 %; `RarityWeight(int weight, Rarity rarity, int luckPermille)`: Common unchanged, the others × (1000 + luck) ÷ 1000, rounded down. Comment: luck makes drops and better tiers likelier without ever removing Common.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/StatKind.DropLuck` (appended); `StatSheet.DropLuckPermille` (init, 0; not on `CombatStats`: battles don't read it); `MemberSheet`.
    - `Loot/GearRoll.Roll(ItemDefinition item, int luckPermille)`; `Loot/DropRoll.Roll(tables, luckPermille)` uses `LootFormula` for each entry's chance and passes the luck to `GearRoll`.
    - `Session/GameSession.FinishBattle`: the active members' `Sheet.DropLuckPermille`, summed.
    - Simulations: the loot plan's `LootSimulation` prints a second table under +50 % luck.
    - Text: `stat_drop_luck` ("Drop luck"); `Ui/StatNames`.
    - Content: `affixes.json` `drop_luck` (amulet, ring; 5-15 %).
  - **Tests:**
    - `LootFormulaTests: ChancePermille_ThirtyPercentWithFiftyLuck_IsFortyFive`
    - `LootFormulaTests: ChancePermille_HighChanceWithLuck_CappedAtWhole`
    - `LootFormulaTests: RarityWeight_Common_Unchanged`
    - `LootFormulaTests: RarityWeight_RareWithFiftyPercentLuck_IsHalfAgainAsMuch`
    - `GearRollTests: Roll_DrawCommonWithoutLuck_IsMagicWithLuck` (one pinned draw, both sides of the boundary)
    - `DropRollTests: Roll_DrawJustAboveChance_DropsWithLuck`
    - `GameSessionDropTests: FinishBattle_FallenMemberWithLuck_StillCounts` (decision 356)
    - `MemberSheetTests: Of_DropLuckWorn_InPermille`
  - **Call sites:** `GearRoll.Roll` in `GearRollTests` (about 10) and `DropRoll.Roll` in `DropRollTests` (about 7): pass 0.
  - **Verify by hand:** the Status page shows Drop luck (ST1) and the Equipment comparison its change; the simulation's two tables show the shift.
