# E1. A map event opens the Forge

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decisions 353, 370 (no fade to black). ADR 0006 (map event flow), ADR 0008 (scenes return requests). After F1a. Rewired on 2026-10-29 after the review's blocker 2. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "What exists", "Key decisions", "Risks".

- **E1. The `openForge` event step opens the Forge and waits for it** (Core + Data + Game, about 210 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** an event step `openForge` waits, like a dialogue step, while the Forge screen is open; Cancel in the Forge closes it and the event continues with its next step. The Forge opens and closes without the flow's fade, as the menu does. No content uses the step until N1, after the editor can show it (E2; the plan lessons: content with a new step lands with or after its editor support).
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Events/OpenForgeStep`: public sealed record; the step that opens the Forge and waits until it closes (decision 353).
    - `Game/Scenes/ForgeRequested`: the request the map scene returns to open the Forge.
    - `Game/Flow/ForgeTransitions(SceneStack scenes, SceneFactory factory)`: opening the Forge over the map and closing it (ADR 0008; no fade, it fades itself in, decision 370). `Open()` pushes `factory.CreateForge()`; `Close(GameSession session)` pops it and calls `session.Exploration.ContinueEvent()` (as `BattleTransitions` calls into the session).
  - **Changes:**
    - Core: `Exploration/EventRun.ApplyAndCheckWait`: `OpenForgeStep` waits (`case DialogueStep or WarpStep or StillStep or OpenForgeStep`).
    - Data: `Content/ContentTypeDiscriminators`: `new JsonDerivedType(typeof(OpenForgeStep), "openForge")`. Not `Maps/MapEventSteps`: it only lists steps with art or audio to check.
    - `Game/Exploration/EventPresenter` (238, about +20): a case `OpenForgeStep` that sets `IsOpeningForge` (public bool, true only on the update the step first shows) through a once flag like `_isRestFadeStarted`, reset whenever the current step isn't an `OpenForgeStep` (so talking to the forge master again opens it again); the summary names the step.
    - `Game/Exploration/ExplorationScene.UpdateExploration`: right after the presenter's update and the map check, before the encounter and busy checks: `IsOpeningForge` stands the player and returns `ForgeRequested`; the `<returns>` names it.
    - `Game/Flow/GameFlow.Start` (246, about +8): `ForgeRequested` opens through `ForgeTransitions`, `ForgeClosed` closes through it (`Session ?? throw`, as `MenuRequested` does); neither goes behind the fade (the default branch would, and `ApplyRequestOnceBlack` throws on them).
    - `Game/Flow/SceneFactory.CreateForge()` (moved here from the draft's F1: its first caller).
    - Tests' `StartMapScenarios.BelowForgeChest`: the chest runs `openForge`, then a line (`WithChestSaying`).
  - **Tests:**
    - `EventRunForgeTests: Advance_OpenForgeStep_WaitsForContinue` (new file: `EventRunTests` is 269)
    - `ContentFileWriterTests: Write_EveryEventStepKind_ReadsBackAsTheSameSteps`: `new OpenForgeStep()` added to its list (fails without the discriminator)
    - `ExplorationSceneTests: Update_OpenForgeStep_RequestsForgeOnce` (145 lines; counts `ForgeRequested` over many updates, as `Update_StepStartsEncounter_RequestsBattleOnce` does)
    - `EventPresenterForgeTests: Update_SameEventRunAgain_OpensTheForgeAgain` (new file: `EventPresenterTests` is 305; the once flag resets after `ContinueEvent`)
    - `GameFlowForgeTests: Update_CancelInTheForge_ContinuesTheEvent` (new file: `GameFlowTests` is 321; `StartedFlow(catalog: BelowForgeChest)`, Confirm, then Cancel: the event's current step is the line after `openForge`; seed-independent)
  - **Call sites:** `GameFlow`'s constructor builds the `ForgeTransitions` (its own four parameters unchanged).
  - **Watch:** `ExplorationScene` must return the request only once (the flow lessons: a second one would push a second Forge); the once flag guarantees it, and `Update_OpenForgeStep_RequestsForgeOnce` checks it. The editor throws on a step it can't open: no content may use `openForge` before E2.
  - **Verify by hand:** none yet (no content uses the step until N1); the flow test plays it headless.
