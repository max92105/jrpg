# E2. The editor shows and adds the `openForge` step

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decision 353; ADR 0012 (editor architecture). After F2.

- **E2. The editor shows and adds the `openForge` step** (Editor, about 150 lines; the editor plan's sizing: tests count as much as code)
  - **Goal:** the event dialog lists an `openForge` step like the other steps (no fields), adds and removes it with undo, and a "Forge master" preset makes an Interact event of a short line and the step.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Editor/Events/EventStepViewModel` (201): the step's label and kind.
    - `Editor/Events/EventPresets` (102): the "Forge master" preset (a dialogue step, then `openForge`).
    - Text in the editor's resources: the step's and preset's names.
  - **Tests:**
    - `EventStepViewModelTests: From_OpenForgeStep_ShowsItsLabel`
    - `EventStepViewModelTests: ToStep_OpenForge_RoundTrips`
    - `EventPresetsTests: ForgeMaster_DialogueThenOpenForge`
  - **Verify by hand:** in the editor, place an NPC, add an Interact event, pick the Forge master preset; save, reopen: the step is still there.
