# F1. The Forge screen lists owned pieces, in its final look

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decision 353 (a screen a forge master opens, not a menu tab). After M3. Was F1 and F4 in the loot draft (the look folded in on 2026-10-27). Reference: `docs/ui-reference/menu-equipment.webp` (a list beside a detail column), `menu-status.png` (section bands, label and value rows).

- **F1. The Forge screen lists owned pieces, in its final look** (Game + text, raw 200, about 320 lines at 1.6x: over budget, see the size note)
  - **Goal:** a Forge screen over the map lists the owned pieces (worn ones aren't owned), one row each in rarity color, with the shared selection look; beside the list, `SectionTitle` bands for the highlighted piece's affixes and the materials owned, in `SheetRowLook` rows. Cancel closes it. Nothing opens it in play until E1 (known gap: tests and a Debug key only, if the developer wants one).
  - **New types:**
    - `Crafting/ForgeScene` (Game folder `Crafting/`, as Core's: a folder named `Forge` would clash with Core's `Crafting/Forge` type, CA1724): the Forge screen's state and input (an `IScene` over the map; returns a close request once).
    - `Crafting/ForgeRow(GearPiece Piece, string Name, string IconFile, Rarity Rarity, string DetailLine)`: one piece as the Forge lists it, formatted once (`AffixText`).
    - `Crafting/ForgeView`: draws the screen from its rows, detail and materials.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Flow/SceneFactory.CreateForge(session)`.
    - The pixel arithmetic that proves the detail column fits three affixes, two materials and (F2) three actions in the screen's height, written here before drawing.
    - Text: `forge_title`, `forge_hint`, `forge_materials`, `forge_empty`; `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests** (`ForgeSceneTests`):
    - `Open_ListsOwnedPiecesNotWorn`
    - `Open_NoPieces_ShowsEmptyAndNoCursor`
    - `Update_Down_HighlightsNextPiece`
    - `Open_ShowsMaterialsOwned`
    - `Update_Cancel_RequestsCloseOnce`
  - **Size note:** the behavior and the look in one slice is about 320 lines; the plan lessons split a screen's behavior from its look up front. Review before approval: split back (behavior with a plain draw, then the look) or accept.
  - **Verify by hand:** (after E1) the forge lists every owned piece, a worn one is absent, the highlighted piece's affixes and the Scrap and Essence counts show beside it, in the Equipment page's look.
