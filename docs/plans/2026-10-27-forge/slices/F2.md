# F2. Salvage, upgrade and reroll at the Forge

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decisions 351, 352, 354; question 357. After E1. Was F2 and F3 in the loot draft (merged on 2026-10-27). Reference: `docs/ui-reference/dialog-confirm.webp`.

- **F2. Salvage, upgrade and reroll at the Forge** (Game + text, raw 230, about 370 lines at 1.6x: over budget, see the size note)
  - **Goal:** Confirm on a piece opens its actions with what each gives or costs: Salvage ("2 Scrap, 1 Essence"), Upgrade (its cost, or "Highest rarity" at Rare), Reroll (its cost; nothing to reroll on a plain piece). An action that doesn't apply or can't be paid is greyed and Confirm on it does nothing. Salvage asks "Salvage the Rare Bronze Sword?" (`ConfirmPrompt`); Yes salvages, No or Cancel keeps it. After an upgrade or reroll the piece stays highlighted with its new tier and affix lines and a notice says what changed. Cancel wins when nested (prompt, then actions, then the screen).
  - **New type:**
    - `Crafting/ForgeActionChoice`: the actions open on one piece: which are offered, what each costs or gives, which can be done, which is highlighted.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Crafting/ForgeScene`: the actions and the prompt; carries them out through `Forge.TrySalvage`, `TryUpgrade`, `TryReroll`; keeps the cursor on the same row after an upgrade or reroll.
    - `Crafting/ForgeView`: the actions as rows with their cost right-aligned, greyed when unavailable.
    - Text: `forge_salvage`, `forge_upgrade`, `forge_reroll`, `forge_cost`, `forge_highest_rarity`, `forge_salvage_confirm`, `forge_salvaged`, `forge_upgraded`, `forge_rerolled`; `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests** (`ForgeSceneActionTests`):
