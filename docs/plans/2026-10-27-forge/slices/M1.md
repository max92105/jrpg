# M1. Materials

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decisions 339 (Material as its own tier and color), 354, 362 (a muted teal), 371. After the affix stats plan. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Core changes", "Tunables".

- **M1. Materials: an item kind, their color, a Materials tab, Scrap and Essence that goblins drop** (Core + Data + content + Game, about 210 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** an item can be a material (`"isMaterial": true`): it stacks, is never used, lists under a new Materials tab on the Inventory page (and under All), and its name is drawn in the Material color there and on the victory Items step. Scrap and Essence exist; goblins drop them now and then.
  - **New types:** none.
  - **Changes:**
    - Core: `Items/ItemDefinition.IsMaterial` (init, false; summary: a crafting material, decision 339; not a `Rarity`).
    - Data: `Items/ItemCatalogValidator`: a material has no effect, no equipment, and `isUsableFromMenu` false (each its own message naming the item).
    - Game:
      - `Ui/UiTheme.Loot.RarityMaterial` = `FromStraightArgb(0xFF5FAFA0)` (decision 362: a muted teal; summary says so).
      - `Menus/InventoryRow.IsMaterial` (init, false); `InventoryList.RowOf(InventoryEntry)` sets it from the item.
      - `Menus/InventoryListView.TextColorOf` (made `internal static` for its test): a material's color first, before the "unusable means grey" branch (a material is never usable, so the order matters).
      - `Battle/VictoryDropRow.IsMaterial` (init, false); `VictoryDropsView.RowsOf` sets it for a stack; `DrawRow` (line 103) draws a material's name and count in `RarityMaterial`.
      - `Menus/InventoryCategory.Materials`; `CategoryRail.Tabs` gains it after Armor (icon `UiArtFiles.Icons.Materials` = `"Item.png"`, placeholder); `InventoryFilter.Keeps`: `Materials => item.IsMaterial` (All keeps them already).
      - Text: `inventory_materials` ("Materials"); `MessageIds.InventoryMaterials`.
    - Content: `items.json` `scrap` ("Scrap", "Metal scraps from broken gear. A smith can work them.") and `essence` ("Essence", "A shimmer drawn out of enchanted gear."), `maxStack` 99, `isUsableFromMenu` false, `isMaterial` true, icon `Item.png`; `drop-tables.json` goblin: `scrap` 20 % 1-2, `essence` 5 % (decision 371).
