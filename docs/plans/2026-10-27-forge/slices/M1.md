# M1. Materials

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decisions 339 (Material as its own tier and color), 354. After the loot plan.

- **M1. Materials: an item kind, their color, a Materials tab, two placeholder materials that drop** (Core + Data + content + Game, about 190 lines)
  - **Goal:** an item can be a material (`"isMaterial": true`): it stacks, is never used, lists under a new Materials tab on the Inventory page, and its name is drawn in the Material tier's color (decision 339; the color from question 362). Scrap and Essence exist; goblins drop Scrap now and then.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Items/ItemDefinition.IsMaterial` (init, false).
    - `Data/Items/ItemCatalogValidator`: a material has no effect, no equipment, and `isUsableFromMenu` false.
    - Game: `Menus/InventoryCategory.Materials`; `CategoryRail` gains its tab (after Armor; icon `UiArtFiles.Icons.Materials` = `Item.png`, placeholder); `InventoryFilter` keeps materials there (and in All); `UiTheme.MaterialColor`, used by `InventoryList` for a material's row. Text: `inventory_materials`; `MessageIds`.
    - Content: `items.json` `scrap` ("Scrap") and `essence` ("Essence"), `maxStack` 99, icon `Item.png`; `drop-tables.json` goblin's `scrap` entry (20 %, 1-2).
  - **Tests:**
