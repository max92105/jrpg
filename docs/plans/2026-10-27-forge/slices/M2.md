# M2. Salvage in Core

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decisions 354, 358. After M1.

- **M2. Salvage in Core: `Crafting/Forge`, yields by rarity in content** (Core + Data + content, about 220 lines)
  - **Goal:** an owned piece (not worn) salvages into its rarity's materials; refused, with nothing changed, when it isn't owned or a material's stack is full (decision 358).
  - **New types:**
    - `Crafting/CraftingRules(IReadOnlyDictionary<Rarity, IReadOnlyList<InventoryEntry>> Salvage)`: what crafting gives and costs, by rarity (M3 adds the costs).
    - `Crafting/Forge(Inventory inventory, IReadOnlyDictionary<string, ItemDefinition> items, CraftingRules rules)`: works owned pieces with materials. `IReadOnlyList<InventoryEntry> YieldOf(GearPiece piece)`; `bool TrySalvage(GearPiece piece)`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Content/GameRules` gains `Crafting`; `ContentCatalogBuilder` passes a one-scrap yield per rarity.
    - `Session/GameSession.Forge`.
    - `Data/Crafting/CraftingRulesValidator`: Common, Magic and Rare each have a yield (Unique none until unique items exist), each item id known and a material, quantities at least 1; `GameRulesValidator` calls it.
    - Content: `rules.json` `crafting.salvage`: common 1 scrap, magic 2 scrap, rare 2 scrap and 1 essence (placeholders).
  - **Tests:**
    - `ForgeTests: TrySalvage_OwnedPiece_RemovesItAndAddsMaterials`
    - `ForgeTests: TrySalvage_NotOwned_ReturnsFalse`
    - `ForgeTests: TrySalvage_MaterialStackFull_ReturnsFalseAndKeepsPiece` (decision 358)
    - `ForgeTests: YieldOf_RarePiece_IsRaresYield`
    - `CraftingRulesValidatorTests: Validate_YieldNotAMaterial_Throws`
    - `CraftingRulesValidatorTests: Validate_MissingRarity_Throws`
    - `CraftingRulesValidatorTests: Validate_NullEntry_Throws`
  - **Call sites:** `new GameRules(` in `ContentCatalogBuilder` (1).
