# M2. Salvage in Core

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decisions 354, 358, 369. After M1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Core changes", "Key decisions".

- **M2. Salvage in Core: `Crafting/Forge`, its quotes and refusals** (Core, about 220 lines)
  - **Goal:** an owned piece (worn ones aren't owned) salvages into its rarity's materials. `Forge.QuoteSalvage` says what it would give and why it's refused: no yield for its rarity, or the whole yield wouldn't fit (decision 358). A refused or unowned salvage changes nothing.
  - **New types** (all in `Core/Crafting/`):
    - `CraftingRules`: public sealed record; what crafting gives and costs. `IReadOnlyDictionary<Rarity, IReadOnlyList<InventoryEntry>> Salvage { get; init; }`, empty by default (M3a, M3b add the costs).
    - `ForgeRefusal`: public enum; why the Forge refuses an action. `NoYield` (the piece's rarity has no salvage row: Unique), `StackFull` (a material of the yield wouldn't fit whole).
    - `ForgeQuote(IReadOnlyList<InventoryEntry> Materials, ForgeRefusal? Refusal)`: public sealed record; one action as offered: the materials it moves and its refusal, null when it can be done. `bool IsRefused => Refusal is not null`.
    - `Forge`: public sealed class; works owned pieces with materials. `internal Forge(Inventory inventory, IReadOnlyDictionary<string, ItemDefinition> items, CraftingRules rules)`; `ForgeQuote QuoteSalvage(GearPiece piece)`; `bool TrySalvage(GearPiece piece)` (false when not owned or refused; else the piece leaves and the yield is added).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Items/Inventory.HasRoomFor(ItemDefinition item, int quantity)`: room for that many more copies; gear always has room. The one-copy `HasRoomFor(item)` stays as it is (its callers are the Equipment page's).
    - `Content/GameRules.Crafting { get; init; } = new()` (the comment says why a default: the record's other members are positional, and the fixtures need none); no constructor call site changes.
    - `Session/GameSession.Forge` (public; built in the constructor from the inventory, `catalog.Items` and `catalog.Rules.Crafting`).
    - Test builder: `ItemDefinitionBuilder.AsMaterial()`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `ForgeSalvageTests: TrySalvage_OwnedPiece_RemovesItAndAddsTheYield`
    - `ForgeSalvageTests: TrySalvage_NotOwned_ReturnsFalseAndChangesNothing` (a piece equal to none in `Gear`, as a worn one is)
    - `ForgeSalvageTests: TrySalvage_WholeYieldWouldNotFit_ReturnsFalseAndKeepsAll` (98 Scrap owned, a yield of 2, max 99: decision 358)
    - `ForgeSalvageTests: TrySalvage_RarityWithoutYield_ReturnsFalse` (a Unique piece; rules without its row)
    - `ForgeSalvageTests: QuoteSalvage_RarePiece_IsRaresYield` (Rare's row differs from the others in the fixture)
    - `ForgeSalvageTests: QuoteSalvage_YieldWouldNotFit_RefusedStackFull`
    - `InventoryTests: HasRoomFor_NinetyEightOwnedTwoMore_IsFalse`
    - `InventoryTests: HasRoomFor_NinetyEightOwnedOneMore_IsTrue` (the rule's other side)
  - **Call sites:** none (`GameRules.Crafting` is an init property; `Forge` is new).
  - **Watch:** `GameSession` 237 (+8). No content yet: the repository's `Rules.Crafting` is empty until M4, so the repository session's Forge refuses every salvage with `NoYield`; nothing calls it before F2a.
