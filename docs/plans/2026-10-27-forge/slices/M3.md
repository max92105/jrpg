# M3. Upgrade and reroll in Core

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decisions 351, 352; question 357 (an exhausted pool). After M2. Was M3 and M4 in the loot draft (merged on 2026-10-27).

- **M3. Upgrade and reroll in Core** (Core + Data + content, about 300 lines: over budget, see the size note)
  - **Goal:**
    - **Upgrade:** an owned Common or Magic piece upgrades for its next tier's cost: the materials are taken, the piece becomes Magic (one affix) or Rare (one more affix), rolled from the pool for its kind; it keeps its place in the list. Rare is the top (Unique is never reached). Refused, with nothing changed, when not owned, at Rare, short of materials, or (question 357, recommended) when the pool has no affix left for its kind.
    - **Reroll:** an owned piece with affixes rerolls for the reroll cost: every affix drawn again (decision 352: same count, same rarity, never one twice), the piece keeps its place. Refused when not owned, without affixes, or short of materials.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Crafting/CraftingRules.UpgradeCosts` (by the tier reached: Magic, Rare) and `RerollCost`.
    - `Loot/GearRoll.AddAffix(GearPiece piece, Rarity rarity)` (null when the pool has nothing left for the kind) and `Reroll(GearPiece piece)`, both reusing the loot plan's affix draw.
    - `Items/Inventory.Replace(GearPiece old, GearPiece replacement)`: in place; false when `old` isn't owned.
    - `Crafting/Forge`: takes the `GearRoll` (four parameters); `UpgradeCostOf(piece)` and `RerollCostOf(piece)` (null when the action doesn't apply); `TryUpgrade(piece)`, `TryReroll(piece)`.
    - `Data/Crafting/CraftingRulesValidator`: both upgrade costs and the reroll cost present, materials known, quantities at least 1.
    - Content: `rules.json` `crafting.upgradeCosts` (magic 3 scrap; rare 5 scrap and 1 essence) and `rerollCost` (2 scrap and 1 essence).
