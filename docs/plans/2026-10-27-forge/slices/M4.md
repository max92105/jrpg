# M4. The crafting rules in content

Part of [the forge plan](../README.md). Decisions 351, 354, 358. After M3b. New on 2026-10-29: the review's blocker 1 (the rules can't be checked against the items in `GameRulesValidator`) and the size budget put the validator and the content in one slice after the Core rules. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables", "What exists".

- **M4. The crafting rules in content, checked against the items** (Data + content, about 240 lines)
  - **Goal:** `rules.json` `crafting` holds the salvage yields, the upgrade costs and the reroll cost (placeholders); a broken block stops the start with a message naming `rules.json` and the field. Every entry names a known material, at least one of it, and no material twice in one list.
  - **New type:**
    - `Data/Crafting/CraftingRulesValidator`: checks `rules.json` `crafting` against the items. `public static void Validate(string fileName, CraftingRules crafting, IReadOnlyDictionary<string, ItemDefinition> items)`: `salvage` has Common, Magic and Rare (Unique none until unique items exist); `upgradeCosts` has Magic and Rare; `rerollCost` isn't empty; in every list each entry isn't null, its item is known and a material, its quantity at least 1, and no item comes twice (a doubled material would pass `Inventory.HasRoomFor` per entry and overflow).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Data/Content/ContentCatalogLoader.Load`: calls `CraftingRulesValidator.Validate(RulesFile, rules.Crafting, itemsById)` once the items are loaded, as `DropTableCatalogValidator` gets `itemsById` (`GameRulesValidator` never sees the items: `rules.json` is read before `items.json`).
    - Content: `rules.json` `crafting`: `salvage` common 1 scrap, magic 2 scrap, rare 2 scrap and 1 essence; `upgradeCosts` magic 3 scrap, rare 5 scrap and 1 essence; `rerollCost` 2 scrap and 1 essence (design.md, "Tunables").
  - **Tests** (`CraftingRulesValidatorTests`, new file, its fixture the content's numbers; the valid case is `Load_RepositoryContent_Succeeds`'s):
    - `Validate_SalvageRarityMissing_ThrowsNamingIt`
    - `Validate_UpgradeCostMissing_ThrowsNamingIt`
    - `Validate_RerollCostEmpty_Throws`
    - `Validate_NullEntry_ThrowsNamingTheIndex` (tests rule: a list in content gets a null-entry test)
    - `Validate_UnknownItem_ThrowsNamingTheId`
    - `Validate_YieldNotAMaterial_Throws`
    - `Validate_QuantityZero_ThrowsNamingTheField`
    - `Validate_SameMaterialTwice_Throws`
    - `ContentCatalogLoaderCraftingTests: Load_SalvageYieldsAPotion_ThrowsNamingRulesJson` (new file: proves the loader calls the validator; copies the content to a temporary folder and rewrites `rules.json`, as `ContentWriterTests` copies it; `ContentCatalogLoaderTests` is 249 and only loads the repository)
    - `ContentCatalogLoaderTests: Load_RepositoryContent_RareSalvageGivesEssence` (the content's shape the Forge relies on, by id, not a total)
  - **Call sites:** none.
  - **Watch:** the size counts the temporary-folder fixture (about 25 lines). The repository's Forge works from here on (M2's watch).
