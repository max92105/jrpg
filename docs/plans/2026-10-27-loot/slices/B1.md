# B1. Loot simulation

Part of [the loot plan](../README.md). Design: "Tunables", "Risks" (balance). After P1b.

- **B1. Loot simulation: drops and rarities per goblin trio** (Simulations, about 120 lines)
  - **Goal:** the developer sees what the placeholder numbers give before tuning them: over 2,000 seeded goblin trio victories, the items dropped per victory, the share of each rarity among dropped gear, the share of Rare pieces with three affixes, and the victories per piece of gear.
  - **New type:**
    - `tests/Jrpg.Simulations/Loot/LootSimulation`: rolls the repository's goblin table through `DropRoll` and `GearRoll` with seeded randoms (no battle fought: drops don't depend on how the fight went) and prints the table.
  - **Assertions** (only what the bounds can detect): every rolled tier with a weight above 0 appears at least once over 2,000 rolls; no Unique ever rolls; the potion drop rate lands within ±3 % of its content value. The table is printed for the developer; numbers are recorded in the Done note.
  - **Watch:** asserts read the content's numbers, never copies of them, so tuning the tables doesn't break the simulation. The affix stats plan extends it with drop luck.
