# C2. `EquipmentPage`'s open choice moves to `Menus/SlotChoice`

Part of [the loot plan](../README.md). `Menus/EquipmentPage.cs` is at 281; G1a and G1b-2 change its choice handling and P1b its rows.

- **C2. `EquipmentPage`'s open choice moves to `Menus/SlotChoice`** (Game, mechanical, about 110 lines moved)
  - **Goal:** the page keeps the member, the slot rows and "Remove all"; the open choice for one slot (its list, its comparison preview, its take hint, the pick applied) moves out. No behavior changes.
  - **New type:**
    - `Menus/SlotChoice`: one slot's open choice, from opening to the applied pick. Built with the slot, the member, the session's `PartyEquipment` and party, and the page's `EquipmentComparison`. Members moved from the page: `ShowPreview`, `Apply`, `UpdateChoice` (as `Update(input)` returning whether it closed), `_previewedRow`, `_takeHint` (as `TakeHint`), `Choice`.
  - **Changes:** `Menus/EquipmentPage` holds a `SlotChoice?` instead of an `EquipmentChoice?` and `_previewedRow`; `Choice`, `IsChoosing`, `Hint`, `Notice` keep their names (about 200 lines after).
  - **Tests:** existing tests pass unchanged (`EquipmentPageTests` 270, `EquipmentPageComparisonTests`, `EquipmentPageCaptionTests`, `EquipmentChoiceTests`).
  - **Verify by hand:** the Equipment page behaves the same: open a slot, the Changes band follows the highlight, taking a sword from another member names them in the footer, Cancel closes the choice.
- **Done, with deviations:** `SlotChoice` is one long-lived instance per page, built with the session, catalog, comparison and messages (four parameters), and `Open(member, slot)` starts each choice, instead of a new `SlotChoice?` per slot (which would have needed six). The page's no-room notice comes back as `SlotChoice.Refusal`. `EquipmentPage` 281 → 228 lines, `SlotChoice` 128; test count unchanged (2587).
