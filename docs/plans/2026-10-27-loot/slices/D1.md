# D1. Defeated enemies drop items

Part of [the loot plan](../README.md). ADR 0030, decisions 343, 344. Design: "Loot", "Folder dependencies". After G1c.

- **D1. Defeated enemies drop items: `Loot/DropRoll` at `FinishBattle`, `Session/BattleSpoils`** (Core, about 250 lines)
  - **Goal:** after a victory, each defeated enemy with a drop table rolls it; the loot goes into the inventory and comes back beside the rewards. Content has no table until D2, so play doesn't change yet; tests run on a test catalog.
  - **Moved:** `Battle/ChanceRolls` → `Randomness/ChanceRolls` (namespace only; 4 source sites, `ChanceRollsTests` moves to `tests/Jrpg.Core.Tests/Randomness/`).
  - **New types:**
    - `Loot/DropEntry(string ItemId, int Chance)`: one item an enemy may drop, its chance (a whole percent, as enemies' `critChance`) and how many: `MinQuantity` and `MaxQuantity` are init properties defaulting to 1, so content leaves them out for a single copy (ADR 0002: no DTO).
    - `Loot/DropTable(string Id, IReadOnlyList<DropEntry> Entries)`: what an enemy names to drop; several enemies may name one (decision 343).
    - `Loot/DroppedStack(ItemDefinition Item, int Quantity)`: a stack of one dropped item.
    - `Loot/Drops(IReadOnlyList<DroppedStack> Stacks, IReadOnlyList<GearPiece> Gear)`: what a won battle dropped; `Drops.None`, `IsEmpty`.
    - `Loot/DropRoll`: rolls tables into drops. `static Drops Roll(IReadOnlyList<DropTable> tables, IReadOnlyDictionary<string, ItemDefinition> items, IRandom random)`: tables in enemy order, each entry its own chance (`ChanceRolls`, the chance as permille), its quantity drawn only when `MinQuantity < MaxQuantity`, stacks merged by item in first-drop order, equipment as plain pieces (rolled from R3b).
    - `Session/BattleSpoils(VictoryRewards Rewards, Drops Drops)`: what a won battle gave: the experience and the drops.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Battle/EnemyDefinition.DropTableId` (`string?`, init): null drops nothing.
    - `Content/ContentCatalog.DropTables` (`IReadOnlyDictionary<string, DropTable>`, required): `ContentCatalogBuilder` defaults to empty, `ContentCatalogLoader` sets it empty until D2.
    - `Session/GameSession.FinishBattle` returns `BattleSpoils?` (null after an escape or a defeat): on a victory, rolls the defeated enemies' tables with the session's random, adds stacks (`Inventory.Add`; extra copies past `maxStack` are lost, as a found item's are, and `Drops` lists what was added) and pieces (`AddGear`).
    - `Game/Battle/BattleScene`: passes `spoils.Rewards` to `VictoryScreen` (D3 passes the spoils).
    - `Battle/VictoryRewards`' summary loses "drops join it later" (they return beside it).
  - **Tests:**
    - `DropRollTests: Roll_CertainEntry_DropsWithoutDrawing` (an empty `FixedSequenceRandom`)
    - `DropRollTests: Roll_DrawBelowChance_Drops`
    - `DropRollTests: Roll_DrawAtChance_DropsNothing` (the other side)
    - `DropRollTests: Roll_QuantityRange_DrawsWithinIt`
    - `DropRollTests: Roll_SameItemFromTwoEnemies_MergesStack`
    - `DropRollTests: Roll_Equipment_DropsPlainPiece`
    - `GameSessionDropTests: FinishBattle_Victory_AddsDropsToInventory`
    - `GameSessionDropTests: FinishBattle_Escaped_ReturnsNullAndRollsNothing`
    - `GameSessionDropTests: FinishBattle_EnemyWithoutTable_DrawsNothing`
  - **Call sites:** `ChanceRolls` 4; `FinishBattle()` 11 in `GameSessionBattleTests` (the ones reading the result read `.Rewards`); `BattleScene` 1.
  - **Watch:** `GameSession.cs` 184 (+15). New tests in `GameSessionDropTests` (`GameSessionBattleTests` is at 242).
