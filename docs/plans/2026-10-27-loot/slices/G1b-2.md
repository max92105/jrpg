# G1b-2. Equipping moves pieces; the room rule leaves

Part of [the loot plan](../README.md). ADR 0029, decision 345. Design: "Gear pieces". After G1b-1; closes G1a's and G1b-1's interim wrapping.

- **G1b-2. Equipping moves pieces; the room rule leaves; the Equipment choice lists pieces** (Core + Game, about 220 lines)
  - **Goal:** `PartyEquipment` moves pieces between the inventory's gear list and members, never refused for room (decision 345). The Equipment page's choice lists one row per owned piece; the "no room" notice leaves.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Party/PartyEquipment`: `TryEquip(member, GearPiece piece)` and `TryEquip(member, GearPiece piece, EquipmentSlot slot)`; `OwnedFor(slot)` returns pieces (the catalog parameter goes); `TryUnequip` and `TryTakeFrom` lose their room checks; return docs updated.
    - Game: `EquipmentChoice(IReadOnlyList<GearPiece> owned, …)`, `Highlighted` a `GearPiece?`; `EquipmentPick.Piece`; `EquipmentChoiceView` drops its quantity column; `EquipmentComparison` passes the piece; `SlotChoice` drops the "no room" notice; `MessageIds.EquipmentNoRoom` and its `ui.json` line go.
    - Simulations: `GoblinTrioSimulation`'s two `TryEquip` calls.
  - **Tests:**
    - `PartyEquipmentTests: TryUnequip_ManyPiecesOwned_PutsPieceBack` (replaces the full-stack refusal)
    - `PartyEquipmentTests: TryEquip_RolledPieceOwned_WearsThatPiece`
    - `PartyEquipmentSlotTests: OwnedFor_Ring_ListsEachPieceInPickupOrder`
    - `PartyEquipmentTakeTests: TryTakeFrom_ReceiverWearsEqualPiece_ReturnsFalse`
    - `EquipmentChoiceTests: Highlighted_OwnedRow_IsThatPiece`
  - **Call sites:** `TryEquip`/`OwnedFor`/`TryTakeFrom`/`TryUnequip` about 70 test lines in 18 files (`TryEquip(_fighter, _bronzeSword)` becomes `TryEquip(_fighter, GearPiece.Plain(_bronzeSword))`). The room-refusal tests leave with the rule (decision 345): list each by name in the Done note with the test that replaces it (the `…StackFull…` tests of `PartyEquipmentTests` and `PartyEquipmentTakeTests`, and the Equipment page's notice test).
  - **Watch:** `PartyEquipmentTests` 203, `PartyEquipmentTakeTests` 173, `EquipmentChoiceTests` 125.
  - **Verify by hand:** a new game's Equipment page lists three Magic Staff rows for the weapon slot (no quantity); equip one, two stay; remove it, three again.
- **Note from G1b-1:** `OwnedFor`'s catalog parameter and `PartyEquipment`'s room checks already left in G1b-1, with the six room-refusal tests (listed there) and `TryUnequip_ManyPiecesOwned_PutsPieceBack`. Left for this slice: `SlotChoice`'s refusal, the page's notice, `MessageIds.EquipmentNoRoom` and its `ui.json` line; `OwnedFor` stops taking `Distinct` bases and returns pieces.
