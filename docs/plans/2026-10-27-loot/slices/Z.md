# Z. Walk-through

Part of [the loot plan](../README.md). The developer's hand check of the whole plan, after B1.

- [ ] **Z. Walk-through: drops, pieces, saves and pages by hand** (manual)
  - New game: the Inventory and Equipment pages list each starting piece (the All tab: stacks, then pieces); equip, take from another member, remove all.
  - Fight the goblin trio until gear drops: the victory screen's Items step lists it in its color; the Inventory page shows the piece's affix line in place of the description; the Equipment comparison shows what it changes.
  - Save, quit, load: every worn and owned piece keeps its rarity and affixes, in the same order.
  - Note every number that feels wrong for the tuning pass (B1's table beside it).
