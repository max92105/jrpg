# B2. Boss phases in Core, entered at the end of `Execute`

Part of [the enemy skills and bosses plan](../README.md). After B4. Decisions 404, 405. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Core changes", "Key decisions" 4.

- **B2. Boss phases in Core, entered at the end of `Execute`** (Core, about 210 lines)
  - **Goal:** an enemy may list phases, each with an HP threshold and its own actions (decision 404). At the end of `BattleState.Execute`, while the battle isn't over, every standing enemy whose HP has fallen below a phase's threshold enters the deepest such phase, reported as `EnemyPhaseEntered`, the call's last event; from then on its tactics choose from that phase's actions, and it never leaves the phase (decision 405). `TurnFlow` doesn't change. No content has phases until B3, so the Game never meets the event before its playback case exists.
  - **New types** (`Core/Battle/`):
    - `EnemyPhase`: the actions an enemy switches to below a share of its HP. `public sealed record`; `required int BelowHpPercent { get; init; }`, `required IReadOnlyList<EnemyAction> Tactics { get; init; }`.
    - `EnemyPhaseEntered`: the event. `public sealed record EnemyPhaseEntered(Combatant Enemy, EnemyPhase Phase) : BattleEvent`.
    - `EnemyPhases`: enters the phases standing enemies' HP has reached. `internal static`; `void EnterReached(IReadOnlyList<Combatant> combatants, List<BattleEvent> events)`: the deepest phase whose threshold the HP is below (`hp × 100 < max × percent`), only when deeper than the current one, so a hit past two thresholds enters the second at once with one event.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Battle/EnemyDefinition`: `IReadOnlyList<EnemyPhase> Phases { get; init; } = []` (descending thresholds, which Data checks in B3); `NamesSkill` also reads the phases' actions.
    - `Battle/Combatant`: `public EnemyPhase? EnemyPhase { get; internal set; }` (null before any; battle only, never saved).
    - `Battle/BattleState.Execute`: after the turn flow, `if (!IsOver) EnemyPhases.EnterReached(Combatants, events)`; the `Execute` summary names the step.
    - `Battle/EnemyTactics`: the current actions are `actor.EnemyPhase?.Tactics ?? enemy.Tactics`.
    - Test builder: `EnemyDefinitionBuilder.WithPhases(params EnemyPhase[] phases)`.
  - **Tests** (new file `EnemyPhaseTests`):
    - `Execute_HitTakesBossBelowThreshold_ReportsPhaseEnteredLast` (the last event of the call)
    - `Execute_HitLeavesBossAtTheThreshold_EntersNoPhase` (HP exactly 50 of 100: strict "below"; the rule's other side)
    - `Execute_HitPastTwoThresholds_EntersTheDeeperOnceWithOneEvent`
    - `Execute_BossAlreadyInThePhase_ReportsNothingMore` (a second hit under the threshold)
    - `Execute_BossHealedAboveThreshold_StaysInThePhase` (decision 405)
    - `Execute_PoisonTickTakesBossBelow_EntersThePhase` (the boss's own upkeep)
    - `Execute_StunnedBossTickTakesItBelow_PhaseEventComesAfterTheSkipAndTick` (the stunned loop inside the same call: `TurnSkipped`, `StatusTicked`, then `EnemyPhaseEntered`)
    - `Execute_QuickSkillHitsBossBelow_EntersThePhase` (the kept turn; a test quick skill with a test resource)
    - `Execute_BossFallsToTheHit_EntersNoPhase` (a fallen enemy enters nothing)
    - `Execute_LastMemberHitsBossBelowThenFallsToPoison_EntersNoPhase` (the boss stands below the threshold, but the member's own tick ends the battle in defeat: the `!IsOver` guard)
    - `ChooseCommand_BossInAPhase_PicksFromThePhasesActions` (phase 1 only attacks, phase 2 only a skill)
    - `Knows_SkillNamedOnlyByAPhase_IsTrue`
  - **Verify by hand:** nothing changes in play (no content has phases yet).
