# B4. A boss is drawn bigger

Part of [the enemy skills and bosses plan](../README.md). After B3. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 7, "Tunables and placeholders", "Risks" (overlap). Question Q10.

- **B4. A boss is drawn bigger** (Game, about 140 lines; no new view type, so no 1.6x)
  - **Goal:** a boss's battler is drawn at `BossSizeFactor` (2, placeholder) times a regular battler's size, standing on its spot's bottom edge as every battler does. Numbers, the target pointer and effect strips already place themselves from the drawn area or the spot, so they follow.
  - **New types:** none.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Game/Battle/BattlerArt`: `int SizeFactor` (1; `BossSizeFactor` = 2 for an enemy whose definition `IsBoss`), with the placeholder named and commented.
    - `Game/Battle/BattlerScaling.Of(Point sourceSize, int sizeFactor)`: the painting square and the halved pixel art, each times the factor (whole factors: pixel art stays point-scaled, `.claude/rules/game.md`).
    - `Game/Battle/BattlersView`: passes the art's factor at its two `BattlerScaling.Of` calls.
  - **Call sites:** `BattlerScaling.Of` (2 in `BattlersView`, its tests).
  - **Tests:**
    - `BattlerArtTests: Of_BossEnemy_IsDrawnAtTheBossFactor`
    - `BattlerArtTests: Of_RegularEnemy_IsDrawnAtOne`
    - `BattlerArtTests: Of_PartyMember_IsDrawnAtOne`
    - `BattlerScalingTests: Of_PixelArtAtFactorTwo_IsItsSourceSize` (256 px art: 256, not 128)
    - `BattlerScalingTests: Of_PaintingAtFactorTwo_IsTwiceTheSquare` (smoothed, 192)
  - **Verify by hand** (with an F12 capture for the review): the Chief stands twice the goblins' size in the middle slot; its tag, floating numbers, effect strip and target pointer sit on it; note whether it covers the goblin above it or the turn order strip (design.md, "Risks"); if it does, the developer chooses a spot rule then (not planned).
  - **Watch:** `BattlersView` 162 (+2); `EnemyTagView` places itself from the spot or the drawn top, which the hand check confirms.
