# B4. The Chief's own art, drawn bigger by its size

Part of [the enemy skills and bosses plan](../README.md). After B1. Decision 408. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 7, "Tunables and placeholders", "Risks" (the Chief's placeholder art).

- **B4. The Chief's own art, drawn bigger by its size** (content + test, about 40 lines)
  - **Goal:** the Chief looks bigger than the goblins through its art alone: a 512 px `GoblinChief.png` draws at 256 px through today's `BattlerScaling` (pixel art up to 512 px is halved), twice the goblin's 128. No Game code changes (decision 408).
  - **New types:** none.
  - **Content:**
    - `content/art/battlers/GoblinChief.png`: the goblin's battler scaled up 2x by whole pixels (nearest neighbor), 512 px on its longer side; made with PowerShell's `System.Drawing` (no Python here). A placeholder until the developer's art.
    - `content/enemies.json`: the Chief's `battleSpriteFile` becomes `GoblinChief.png` (portrait and attack strip stay the goblin's).
  - **Tests:**
    - `BattlerScalingTests: Of_RepositoryChiefArt_IsTwiceTheGoblinsDrawnSize` (both images' sizes read with `Data/Art/PngSize` from the repository's battlers: pins that the placeholder stays pixel art at 512 px, so a 513 px or a 1024 px replacement would show as a painting at the spot's size and fail here first)
  - **Verify by hand** (with an F12 capture for the review; the forest table edited locally, then reverted, as in C1): the Chief stands twice the goblins' size on the enemies' top row; its tag, floating numbers, effect strip and target pointer sit on it; nothing is drawn over it. If it reaches the turn order strip, the developer chooses a spot rule then (not planned).
