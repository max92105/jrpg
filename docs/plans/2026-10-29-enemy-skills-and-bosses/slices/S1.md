# S1. A boss fight simulation

Part of [the enemy skills and bosses plan](../README.md). After B4. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables and placeholders", "Risks" (the forest gets harder).

- **S1. A boss fight simulation** (Simulations, about 110 lines)
  - **Goal:** the developer tunes the Chief with numbers: a table of win rate, average turns and how often each phase was reached, for a party at a few levels against the repository's `goblin_chief` encounter, every enemy choosing through `EnemyTactics` with the catalog's skills.
  - **New types:**
    - `tests/Jrpg.Simulations/Battle/BossFightSimulation`: plays seeded Chief fights and prints their table. Built as `ForestFightsSimulation` is (repository content, a fixed seed per fight, the party attacking a random enemy as that simulation's policy does; read it before writing this one and reuse its party setup if it is shared, rather than copying it).
  - **Assertions** (aggregate, tests.md; only checks the bound can detect):
    - `Run_ChiefFights_EveryFightEnds` (no fight passes the turn cap: a tactics loop that never lands damage would hit it)
    - `Run_ChiefFights_ThePhaseIsReachedInSomeFights` (at least one fight reports `EnemyPhaseEntered`; a broken threshold would report none)
  - **Prints:** level, fights, win rate, average turns, phase reached (share of fights), average member HP left after a win.
  - **Verify by hand:** after a build, `tests/Jrpg.Simulations/bin/Debug/net10.0/Jrpg.Simulations.exe -showLiveOutput` shows the table; paste it in this slice's Done note for the developer's tuning (Q12).
