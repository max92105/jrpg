# S1. A Goblin Chief fight simulation

Part of [the enemy skills and bosses plan](../README.md). After T3. Decisions 399, 410. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables and placeholders".

- **S1. A Goblin Chief fight simulation** (Simulations, about 140 lines)
  - **Goal:** the developer tunes the Chief with numbers before the boss rules land: seeded fights against the repository's `goblin_chief` encounter, every enemy choosing through `EnemyTactics` with the catalog's skills, the party choosing through `AttackFirstLivingEnemyPolicy` (attack the first living enemy), as `ForestFightsSimulation` does. Two tables:
    - a lone level 1 leader of each class (ADR 0017's start-area check, printed only: no map rolls the Chief, decision 399);
    - a party of four at a few levels (placeholder: 1, 3 and 5; members recruited from the repository, as many classes as fit).
  - **New types:**
    - `tests/Jrpg.Simulations/Battle/BossFightSimulation`: plays seeded Chief fights and prints their tables. Its leader setup (`PartyMember.Recruit` of the stand-in with a class) mirrors `ForestFightsSimulation.RunBattle`; if both need the same lines, a small shared builder in the folder holds them rather than a copy.
  - **Assertions** (aggregate, tests.md; only checks the bound can detect):
    - `Run_ChiefFights_EveryFightEnds` (no fight reaches the turn cap: a pick that never lands damage would)
    - `Run_ChiefFights_TheChiefUsesEveryActionOfItsKit` (over all seeds, the Chief's `ActionStarted` events include the attack, Cleave and Field Dressing: a broken weight or condition would leave one out)
  - **Prints:** class or level, fights, win rate, average turns, average HP left after a win, and the share of the Chief's turns spent on each action.
  - **Verify by hand:** after a build, `tests/Jrpg.Simulations/bin/Debug/net10.0/Jrpg.Simulations.exe -showLiveOutput` shows the tables; paste them in this slice's Done note for the developer's tuning (decision 410).
