# T2. Conditions on actions; a heal aims at the most wounded

Part of [the enemy skills and bosses plan](../README.md). After T1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Core changes", "Extension points". Questions Q3, Q4, Q5 (placeholders until answered).

- **T2. Conditions on actions; a heal aims at the most wounded** (Core, about 210 lines)
  - **Goal:** an action may carry a condition; it is eligible only while the condition holds. With no eligible action the enemy attacks (Q5). A healing skill aimed at one ally goes to the living ally with the lowest HP share, with no draw (Q3).
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Tactics/TacticConditionKind`: the closed list of conditions. `public enum`: `OwnHpBelow`, `AllyHpBelow`, `AllyFallen`.
    - `Core/Tactics/TacticCondition`: when an action may be chosen. `public sealed record TacticCondition(TacticConditionKind Kind, int Percent = 0)`; `Percent` is a whole percent of max HP, unused by `AllyFallen`.
    - `Core/Battle/TacticConditions`: whether a condition holds for an enemy now. `internal static`; `bool Holds(TacticCondition condition, Combatant enemy, BattleState battle)`. HP below a share compares `hp × 100 < max × percent` in integers (no rounding); an ally is another combatant on the enemy's side; the default arm throws.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Tactics/EnemyAction`: `TacticCondition? When { get; init; }`.
    - `Battle/EnemyTactics`: an action is eligible when `When` is null or holds, and its skill can be used; none eligible → the attack.
    - `Battle/EnemyTargeting.ForSkill`: a `RestoreHp` skill aimed at one ally → the candidate with the lowest HP share (ties: the first in battle order), no draw.
  - **Tests** (new file `EnemyTacticConditionTests`):
    - `ChooseCommand_OwnHpBelowAndHurt_ActionIsEligible` (HP 49 of 100, below 50)
    - `ChooseCommand_OwnHpAtTheThreshold_ActionIsNotEligible` (HP 50 of 100: "below" is strict; the rule's other side)
    - `ChooseCommand_AllyHpBelowAndAnAllyHurt_ActionIsEligible`
    - `ChooseCommand_AllyHpBelowOnlyItselfHurt_ActionIsNotEligible` (the enemy isn't its own ally)
    - `ChooseCommand_AllyFallen_ActionIsEligible`
    - `ChooseCommand_NoAllyFallen_ActionIsNotEligible`
    - `ChooseCommand_NoActionEligible_Attacks` (one conditioned skill, condition false: an attack with today's draw)
    - `ChooseCommand_HealOnOneAlly_AimsAtTheLowestHpShare` (two hurt goblins, 20/30 and 40/100: the second, by share, not by HP)
    - `ChooseCommand_HealWithTiedShares_AimsAtTheFirstInBattleOrder`
    - `ChooseCommand_HealOnOneAlly_DrawsNothingForTheTarget` (the sequence holds only the pick's draw)
  - **Verify by hand:** nothing changes in play yet.
  - **Watch:** `EnemyTactics` stays under about 80 lines; if the eligibility filter grows past that, it moves into `TacticConditions`.
