# T2. Conditions on actions: own HP, a living ally's HP, an ally fallen

Part of [the enemy skills and bosses plan](../README.md). After C1. Decisions 402, 403. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Core changes", "Extension points".

- **T2. Conditions on actions: own HP, a living ally's HP, an ally fallen** (Core + Data, about 220 lines)
  - **Goal:** an action may carry a condition; it is eligible only while the condition holds (decision 402). With nothing eligible the enemy attacks (T1's fallback, decision 403). Data reads and checks `when`. No repository content uses a condition until T3.
  - **New types** (`Core/Battle/`):
    - `TacticConditionKind`: the closed list of conditions. `public enum`: `OwnHpBelow`, `AllyHpBelow`, `AllyFallen`.
    - `TacticCondition`: when an action may be chosen. `public sealed record TacticCondition(TacticConditionKind Kind, int Percent = 0)`; `Percent` is a whole percent of max HP, unused by `AllyFallen`.
    - `TacticConditions`: who meets a condition for an enemy now. `internal static`; `IReadOnlyList<Combatant> MeetingIt(TacticCondition condition, Combatant enemy, BattleState battle)` (`OwnHpBelow`: the enemy itself when below; `AllyHpBelow`: the living allies below; `AllyFallen`: the fallen allies; an ally is another combatant on the enemy's side); `bool Holds(...)` is "someone meets it". HP below a share compares `hp × 100 < max × percent` in integers (no rounding, strict). The default arm throws. T3's heal reads `MeetingIt`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Battle/EnemyAction`: `TacticCondition? When { get; init; }`.
    - `Battle/EnemyTactics`: an action is eligible when `When` is null or holds, and its skill can be used.
    - `Data/Battle/EnemyTacticsValidator`: `when` must have `percent` within 1..99 for `ownHpBelow` and `allyHpBelow`, and none (0) for `allyFallen`.
  - **Tests** (new file `EnemyTacticConditionTests`):
    - `ChooseCommand_OwnHpBelowAndHurt_ActionIsEligible` (HP 49 of 100, below 50)
    - `ChooseCommand_OwnHpAtTheThreshold_ActionIsNotEligible` (HP 50 of 100: "below" is strict; the rule's other side)
    - `ChooseCommand_AllyHpBelowAndALivingAllyHurt_ActionIsEligible`
    - `ChooseCommand_AllyHpBelowOnlyItselfHurt_ActionIsNotEligible` (the enemy isn't its own ally)
    - `ChooseCommand_AllyHpBelowOnlyAFallenAlly_ActionIsNotEligible` (a fallen ally at 0 HP counts for nothing: living allies only)
    - `ChooseCommand_AllyFallen_ActionIsEligible`
    - `ChooseCommand_NoAllyFallen_ActionIsNotEligible`
    - `EnemyTacticsValidatorTests: Validate_HpConditionPercentZero_IsRefused`
    - `EnemyTacticsValidatorTests: Validate_HpConditionPercentHundred_IsRefused` (the range's other side)
    - `EnemyTacticsValidatorTests: Validate_AllyFallenWithAPercent_IsRefused`
    - `EnemyTacticsValidatorTests: Load_ActionWithACondition_ReadsTheConditionKind` (a temp content root: the camelCase enum and `when` reach the record)
  - **Verify by hand:** nothing changes in play (no content uses a condition yet).
  - **Watch:** `EnemyTactics` about 60 lines after this slice.
