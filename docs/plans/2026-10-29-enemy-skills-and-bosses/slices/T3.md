# T3. A heal aims at the most wounded ally its condition found; the Chief's Field Dressing

Part of [the enemy skills and bosses plan](../README.md). After T2. Decision 401. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Core changes", "Tunables and placeholders".

- **T3. A heal aims at the most wounded ally its condition found; the Chief's Field Dressing** (Core + content, about 110 lines)
  - **Goal:** a `restoreHp` skill aimed at one ally goes to the lowest HP share among the legal targets that met the action's condition: itself under "own HP below", the living allies below the share under "ally HP below", every legal target without an HP condition; ties go to the first in battle order; no draw (decision 401). The condition and the target agree, so a heal picked because a goblin is hurt lands on that goblin. The Chief gets Field Dressing.
  - **New types:** none.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Battle/EnemyTargeting`: `ForSkill` takes the chosen `EnemyAction` (its condition) beside the skill; a single-ally `RestoreHp` keeps the candidates `TacticConditions.CombatantsMeeting` returns for an HP condition, then the lowest HP share, compared by cross-multiplying (`hpA × maxB < hpB × maxA`), no rounding.
    - `content/skills.json`: `field_dressing` (design.md, "Tunables").
    - `content/enemies.json`: the Chief's third action, `field_dressing` weight 3 when a living ally's HP is below 50 %.
  - **Tests** (`EnemySkillTacticsTests`, or a new `EnemyHealTargetTests` if that file passes 250):
    - `ChooseCommand_HealOnAllyBelowHalf_AimsAtTheLowestShareThatMetIt` (goblins at 45/100 and 14/30, both below half: the first, lowest by share though it has more HP)
    - `ChooseCommand_HealOnAllyBelowHalf_SkipsAWoundedAllyAboveTheThreshold` (the Chief itself at 60/240 is lower but isn't an ally that met the condition)
    - `ChooseCommand_HealWithTiedShares_AimsAtTheFirstInBattleOrder`
    - `ChooseCommand_HealOnOwnHpBelow_AimsAtItself`
    - `ChooseCommand_HealOnOneAlly_DrawsNothingForTheTarget` (the sequence holds only the pick's draw)
  - **Verify by hand** (the forest table edited locally, then reverted, as in C1): once a goblin is under half HP, the Chief sometimes casts "Field Dressing" on the most wounded goblin (a cue with the name and a heal number on that goblin).
