# B1. The Buy tab lists the stock

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 252 (one selection look), 370 (a refused action greyed). Reference: `docs/ui-reference/shop-sell.webp` (name, owned and price columns; a refused row greyed). After N1 (checked by hand at the shopkeeper). The forge plan's F1b is the model. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (quotes, refusal order).

- **B1. The Buy tab lists the stock with prices and owned counts** (Game + text, about 175 lines; extends `ShopView`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** the Shop lists its stock in content order, one row each: icon, name, how many the party owns (a stack's count, or the pieces of that base), the price. A row Core refuses (`Shop.QuoteBuy`) is greyed. Up and Down move the shared selection (gliding highlight, bobbing pointer). Nothing is bought yet (T1).
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Trade/StockRow(ItemDefinition Item, MeasuredText Name, string Owned, string Price, TradeRefusal? Refusal)`: one stock item as shown, formatted once; `bool IsAvailable`.
    - `Game/Trade/StockList`: the Buy tab's rows, their scrolling cursor and gliding highlight, rebuilt when the inventory or the gold changes. `StockList(Shop shop, Inventory inventory, SpriteFontBase font)`; `IReadOnlyList<StockRow> Rows`; `ListCursor? Cursor`; `SelectionMotion Selection`; `StockRow? SelectedRow`; `Refresh()`; `MoveUp()`, `MoveDown()`, `Advance(TimeSpan delta)`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: builds the list; Up and Down move it; `Advance` passes the delta.
    - `Trade/ShopView`: the rows (icon, name, owned, price right-aligned through `GoldText`, greyed when refused), the selection through `UiTextures.DrawGlidingSelection` and `MenuPointer`. Plain placement (the look is L2a).
    - Text: none new (`gold_amount` from G3).
  - **Tests:**
    - `StockListTests: Rows_GeneralStore_InContentOrderWithPrices`
    - `StockListTests: Rows_OwnedStack_ShowsItsCount`
    - `StockListTests: Rows_OwnedPieces_CountsThePiecesOfThatBase`
    - `StockListTests: Rows_ShortOfGold_RefusedShortOfGold` (an empty purse: every row greyed)
    - `ShopSceneTests: Update_Down_MovesTheList` (the scene's routing)
  - **Verify by hand:** at the shopkeeper: the nine items with prices, potions owned shown; Down and Up glide the highlight; spend the gold at the inn down to a few G: the dear rows greyed.
