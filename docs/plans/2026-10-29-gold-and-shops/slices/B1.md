# B1. The Buy list

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 252 (one selection look), 362 (rarity colors), 370 (a refused row greyed), 380 (prices with the coin), 385 (the stock's pieces). Reference: `docs/ui-reference/shop-sell.webp` (name, owned and price columns; a refused row greyed). After N1 (checked by hand at the shopkeeper). The forge plan's F1b and F1d are the model. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (quotes; the stock).

- **B1. The Buy list: the stock's stacks and pieces with owned counts and prices** (Game, raw 150, about 240 lines at 1.6x: adds a view type)
  - **Goal:** the Shop lists its stock in the page column, in its list's order (each gear entry's rolled piece in its place): icon, name (a piece in its rarity's color), how many the party owns (a stack's count, or the owned pieces of that base), the price with the coin. A row Core refuses (`Shop.QuoteBuy` for one) is greyed. Up and Down move the shared selection (gliding highlight, bobbing pointer); a long list scrolls. Nothing is bought yet (T1).
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Trade/StockRow(ItemDefinition Item, GearPiece? Piece, MeasuredText Name, string Owned, MeasuredText Price, TradeRefusal? Refusal)`: one stock entry as shown, formatted once; `bool IsAvailable`.
    - `Game/Trade/StockList`: the Buy list's rows, their scrolling cursor and gliding highlight, rebuilt when the inventory, the gold or the stock changes. `StockList(Shop shop, Inventory inventory, SpriteFontBase font)`; `IReadOnlyList<StockRow> Rows`; `ListCursor? Cursor`; `SelectionMotion Selection`; `StockRow? SelectedRow`; `Refresh()`; `MoveUp()`, `MoveDown()`, `Advance(TimeSpan delta)`.
    - `Game/Trade/ShopListView`: draws the list column: the rows (icon, name, owned, the price through `GoldAmountView`, greyed when refused), the selection through `UiTextures.DrawGlidingSelection` and `MenuPointer`, the empty line.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: builds the list; Up and Down move it; `Advance` passes the delta.
    - `Trade/ShopView`: places `ShopListView` in the page column.
  - **Pixel arithmetic** (the list column, x 40 to 1000, 848 tall): B2's tabs 48 + gap 12; B3's column titles 36; 13 rows x 52 (the Inventory's row) = 676. Total 772 ≤ 848, 76 px spare; more rows scroll (`ListCursor`'s window). Name from 96 (after a 40 px icon; B3's rail takes the left 56 and shifts it), owned right-aligned at 760, price right-aligned at 960 (the widest, the coin and "9999999" at the body size, measured in the slice, must fit 180 px).

    - `StockListTests: Rows_OwnedPieces_CountsThePiecesOfThatBase`
    - `StockListTests: Rows_ShortOfGold_RefusedShortOfGold` (an empty purse: every row greyed)
    - `ShopSceneTests: Update_Down_MovesTheList` (the scene's routing)
  - **Verify by hand:** at the shopkeeper: the stock with prices and the coin, the rolled sword and cap in their rarity's colors, potions owned shown; Down and Up glide the highlight. The greyed rows: with a content copy starting with 20 G (from T1 on: buy until short).
