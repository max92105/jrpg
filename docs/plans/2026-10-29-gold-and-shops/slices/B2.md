- **B2. The Buy and Sell tabs; the Sell list** (Game + text, about 230 lines; extends `ShopListView`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** the Shop opens with the cursor on two tabs above the list, Buy and Sell (`Ui/PointedBarShape`, as the victory's Items step; the shown one lit): Left and Right choose (no wrap), the list under them follows, Confirm enters the list, Cancel leaves the Shop. In the list, Cancel returns to the tabs. The Sell list shows the owned stacks in pickup order, then the owned pieces in pickup order: icon, name (a piece in its rarity's color), owned count (a piece: none), the sale price from `ItemSales.QuoteSale` with the coin; a row Core refuses (not for sale) greyed. Each tab keeps its own cursor. Nothing is sold yet (T3).
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Trade/ShopTab`: the two tabs, in the order shown: `Buy`, `Sell`.
    - `Game/Trade/SaleRow(ItemDefinition Item, GearPiece? Piece, MeasuredText Name, string Owned, MeasuredText Price, TradeRefusal? Refusal)`: one owned stack or piece as shown, formatted once; `bool IsAvailable`.
    - `Game/Trade/SaleList`: the Sell list's rows, their cursor and gliding highlight, rebuilt when the inventory changes. `SaleList(ItemSales sales, Inventory inventory, IReadOnlyDictionary<string, ItemDefinition> items, SpriteFontBase font)`; `Rows`, `Cursor`, `Selection`, `SelectedRow`, `Refresh()`, `MoveUp()`, `MoveDown()`, `Advance(TimeSpan delta)`; with nothing owned, no cursor.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: `ShopTab Tab`, `bool IsOnTabs`; the tab level's input; Up and Down move the shown tab's list.
    - `Trade/ShopListView`: the tabs above the list (the selection on the chosen tab while the cursor is there), the Sell rows, the empty line.
    - Text `shop_buy` ("Buy"), `shop_sell` ("Sell"), `shop_nothing_to_sell` ("Nothing to sell."); the footer hint names the tabs; `MessageIds`.
