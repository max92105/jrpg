# B2. The Sell tab, and the tabs

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 362 (rarity colors), 369 (owned pieces only), 370. Reference: `docs/ui-reference/shop-sell.webp` (the sell list: owned count, sale price). After B1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), README "Decisions" (Sell tab order; unpriced rows greyed).

- **B2. The Sell tab lists what can be sold; Left and Right switch tabs** (Game + text, about 210 lines; extends `ShopView`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** the Shop has two tabs, Buy and Sell; Left and Right switch them (Buy first, no wrap). The Sell tab lists the owned stacks in pickup order, then the owned pieces in pickup order: icon, name (a piece in its rarity's color), owned count (a piece: none), the sale price from `ItemSales.QuoteSale`; a refused row (not for sale, purse full) greyed. Each tab keeps its own cursor. Nothing is sold yet (T2).
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Trade/ShopTab`: the two tabs, in the order shown: `Buy`, `Sell`.
    - `Game/Trade/SaleRow(ItemDefinition Item, GearPiece? Piece, MeasuredText Name, string Owned, string Price, TradeRefusal? Refusal)`: one owned stack or piece as shown, formatted once; `bool IsAvailable`.
    - `Game/Trade/SaleList`: the Sell tab's rows, their cursor and gliding highlight, rebuilt when the inventory changes. `SaleList(ItemSales sales, Inventory inventory, IReadOnlyDictionary<string, ItemDefinition> items, SpriteFontBase font)`; `Rows`, `Cursor`, `Selection`, `SelectedRow`, `Refresh()`, `MoveUp()`, `MoveDown()`, `Advance(TimeSpan delta)`; with nothing owned, no cursor.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: `ShopTab Tab`; Left and Right switch; Up and Down move the shown tab's list.
    - `Trade/ShopView`: the tab labels over the list (the shown one bright), the Sell rows, the empty line.
    - Text `shop_buy` ("Buy"), `shop_sell` ("Sell"), `shop_nothing_to_sell` ("Nothing to sell."); `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `SaleListTests: Rows_StacksThenPieces_InPickupOrder`

    - `SaleListTests: Rows_Piece_PricedAsItsBase` (Q1's placeholder as shown)
    - `SaleListTests: Rows_NothingOwned_HasNoCursor`
    - `ShopSceneTabTests: Right_OnBuy_ShowsSell` (new file)
    - `ShopSceneTabTests: Left_OnSell_ShowsBuy`
    - `ShopSceneTabTests: Right_OnSell_StaysOnSell`
  - **Watch:** `SaleList`'s constructor is at four parameters. `ShopScene` grows toward the Forge's 233: if it passes 200 here, T1 and T2 carry out trades in a `Trade/ShopTrades` presenter instead of the scene.
  - **Verify by hand:** at the shopkeeper: Right shows the potions and the starting pieces with half their prices, Scrap greyed "not for sale" once owned; Left returns to Buy with its cursor where it was.
