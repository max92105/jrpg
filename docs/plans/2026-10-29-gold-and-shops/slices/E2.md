# E2. The editor shows and adds the `openShop` step

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decision 353; ADR 0012 (editor architecture). After E1. The forge plan's E2 is the model. The editor only picks a shop id: shops themselves are edited by hand (README, "Out of scope"). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "What exists" (editor).

- **E2. The editor shows and adds the `openShop` step; the Shopkeeper preset** (Editor, about 190 lines; tests count about as much as code)
  - **Goal:** the event dialogs list an `openShop` step with its shop, picked from the loaded shops; add, move and remove it with undo; a step with no shop chosen says so and can't be kept. A "Shopkeeper" preset makes an Interact event of an empty dialogue line (for the developer to write) and an `openShop` step with no shop yet.
  - **New types:** none.
  - **Changes** (labels are C# strings: the editor has no resources file):
    - `Editor/Events/EventStepKind.OpenShop`.
    - `Editor/Events/EventStepViewModel`: `ShopId` (`[ObservableProperty]`), `IsOpenShop`; `Label` "Open shop: {ShopId}"; `Problem` "Choose the shop."; `From`/`ToStep`.
    - `Editor/Events/StepChoices.ShopIds` (`IReadOnlyList<string>`, an `init` member defaulting to empty: the record already has four positional members, so its 3 sites stay as they are); `EventEditingViewModel.Choices` fills it from the catalog's shops, sorted.
    - `Editor/Events/EventStepEditor.xaml`: the shop combo box (as the item one).
    - `Editor/Events/EventViewModel.AddOpenShop` (`[RelayCommand]`), its "Shop" button beside "Forge" in `ObjectEventsWindow.xaml` and `CellEventWindow.xaml` (both rows already wrap, forge plan E2).
    - `Editor/Events/EventPresets.Shopkeeper(EventIdAllocation target)`; `EventListViewModel.ApplyShopkeeperPreset` with its "Shopkeeper" button.
  - **Tests:**
    - `EventStepViewModelShopTests: FromStep_OpenShopStep_ShowsItsShop` (new file: `EventStepViewModelTests` is 260)
    - `EventStepViewModelShopTests: ToStep_OpenShop_ReturnsStepWithTheShop`
    - `EventStepViewModelShopTests: Problem_OpenShopWithoutShop_AsksForIt`
    - `EventStepViewModelTests: StepsOfEveryKind` gains `new OpenShopStep("general_store")`
    - `EventPresetsTests: Shopkeeper_TalksThenOpensAShop`
    - `EventListViewModelShopTests: ApplyShopkeeperPreset_SelectsTheEmptyDialogueStep` (new file: `EventListViewModelTests` is 258)
    - `EventListViewModelShopTests: AddOpenShopCommand_NoStepChosen_AddsOpenShopLast`
    - `EventEditingViewModelTests: Choices_RepositoryCatalog_ListsTheShops`
  - **Call sites:** none (`ShopIds` is an `init` member).
  - **Verify by hand:** in the editor, place an NPC, open its events, press Shopkeeper: an Interact event with an empty line selected and an "Open shop: " step flagged until a shop is chosen; choose `general_store`, write the line, OK, save, reopen: both steps are there. Add an Open shop step to a cell event; Ctrl+Z removes it.
