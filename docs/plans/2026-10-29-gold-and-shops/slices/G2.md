# G2. The format-6 save: gold and the shops' stocks

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 374 (format 6 here, 7 in the quests plan), 385 (the stock is saved). ADR 0022 (a format change bumps `formatVersion` and refuses older saves). After G1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Risks" (save format).

- **G2. The format-6 save: gold and the shops' stocks** (Core + Data, about 175 lines)
  - **Goal:** saves keep the party's gold and, from S6 on, every opened shop's rolled stock: the whole format-6 shape lands once, before either appears in play. `Capture` writes the gold and an empty stock list (nothing rolls a stock before S5; S6 fills it and reads it back); `Restore` restores the gold. A format-5 save is refused as older.
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Trade/SavedShopStock(string ShopId, int RolledAtLevel, IReadOnlyList<SavedGear> Pieces)`: public sealed record; one shop's rolled stock as a save keeps it: its pieces and the leader's level they were rolled at.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Saving/SaveGame.Gold` (required int) and `ShopStocks` (required `IReadOnlyList<SavedShopStock>`).
    - `Session/GameSession.Capture` writes `Party.Purse.Gold` and `ShopStocks = []`; `Restore` adds `save.Gold` to the new party's purse.
    - `Data/Saving/SaveFile.CurrentFormatVersion = 6`; `FileSaveStore`: gold below 0 or above `Purse.MaxGold` is unreadable; a null stock or a null piece in a stock joins the null-entry check.
    - Test fixtures: `Gold` and `ShopStocks` in the 4 `SaveGame` initializers (`SaveGameFixtures` (2), `SaveGameRepairWornTests`, `SaveGameRepairGearTests`); `SaveGameFixtures`' stored save holds a gold and one stock.
  - **Tests:**
    - `GameSessionGoldSaveTests: Capture_PurseHoldsGold_SaveKeepsIt` (new file: `GameSessionSaveTests` is 270)
    - `GameSessionGoldSaveTests: Restore_SaveWithGold_PurseHoldsIt`
    - `FileSaveStoreFormatTests: Write_File_HoldsTheFormatSixEnvelope` (renames `…FormatFive…`)
    - `FileSaveStoreFormatTests: Read_FormatFive_ThrowsOlder` (replaces `Read_FormatFour_ThrowsOlder`)
    - `FileSaveStoreFormatTests: Read_NegativeGold_ThrowsUnreadable`
    - `FileSaveStoreFormatTests: Read_GoldAboveMax_ThrowsUnreadable`
    - `FileSaveStoreFormatTests: Read_NullShopStock_ThrowsUnreadable` (beside the existing null-entry cases)
    - Existing: the store's round trip reads back the fixture's gold and stock.
  - **Call sites:** `SaveGame` initializers (5 with `GameSession.Capture`).
  - **Watch:** `FileSaveStoreFormatTests` 191 (+40): if it passes 250, the gold and stock tests go in a `FileSaveStoreGoldTests` file. `GameSession` 245 (+3).
  - **Verify by hand:** start the game, save, load: it loads; a save from before (format 5) is refused with the "older format" message.
