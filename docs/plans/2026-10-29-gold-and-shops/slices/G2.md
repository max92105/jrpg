# G2. Enemies drop gold; a new game starts with some

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decision 103 (gold joins the victory's rewards). ADR 0030 (loot rolls after the experience, drawing only when uncertain); the plan's "ADR: gold and trade" step comes first. After G1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (gold rolls after the items; what went in), "Tunables", "Risks" (determinism).

- **G2. Enemies drop gold into the purse; a new game starts with some** (Core + Data + content, about 235 lines)
  - **Goal:** a drop table may name a gold range; after a victory each defeated enemy whose table has one rolls it, after every item draw, and the gold goes into the purse; `BattleSpoils.Gold` says what went in. A new game starts with `startingGold`. Nothing shows it yet (G3; known gap: tests only, and the menu's Save keeps it).
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Loot/GoldRange(int Min, int Max)`: public sealed record; the gold a table drops per defeated enemy, both ends included.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Loot/DropTable.Gold` (`GoldRange?` init, null drops none).
    - `Loot/DropRoll.RollGold(IReadOnlyList<DropTable> tables)`: one `RangeRoll.Within` per table with gold (no draw on a single value); its summary names the second job.
    - `Session/BattleSpoils(VictoryRewards Rewards, Drops Drops, int Gold)`: what the purse took.
    - `Session/GameSession.SpoilsOf`: `RollGold` after `Roll` and `Keep`; adds to `Party.Purse` and reports what fit. `StartNew` adds `NewGame.StartingGold`.
