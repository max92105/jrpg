# G3. Gold on the victory screen and in the menu header

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decision 103 (the victory view takes gold as an addition). References: `docs/ui-reference/battle-victory.jpg` (reward rows), `shop-sell.webp` and `menu-tabs.webp` (gold top right). After G2. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Replacement mapping".

- **G3. Gold on the victory screen and in the menu header** (Game + text, about 130 lines; adds no view type: one more reward row and one more header text)
  - **Goal:** the victory screen shows a GOLD reward row under EXP when the battle gave gold (none for the training dummy); the menu header shows the party's gold, "100 G", left of the map name, updated when it changes.
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Ui/GoldText`: an amount of gold as players read it ("1250 G"), through `NumberText` and `gold_amount`. `static string Of(int gold, UiMessages messages)`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Battle/VictoryScreenView.RewardRows(BattleSpoils spoils)`: EXP, then a gold row when `spoils.Gold > 0` (label `victory_gold`); the view passes the screen's spoils. `VictoryScreen` keeps the spoils' gold (`Gold`). About +3 lines to the view (263): the backlog's `VictoryCardsView` split waits (README, "Decisions").
    - `Menus/MenuHeader`: measures the gold text when the purse's gold changes, draws it left of the map name (`TextSecondary`, the location's gap); its summary names it.
    - Text `gold_amount` ("{amount} G"), `victory_gold` ("GOLD"); `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `VictoryScreenViewTests: RewardRows_WithGold_ListsExperienceThenGold`
    - `VictoryScreenViewTests: RewardRows_NoGold_IsExperienceOnly` (renames `RewardRows_ExperienceOnly_IsOneRow`)
    - `GoldTextTests: Of_Amount_WritesItWithTheCurrency`
    - `MenuHeaderTests: Update_GoldChanged_ShowsTheNewAmount` (an internal `ShownGold` read accessor; new file if none)
  - **Watch:** the second reward bar sits `RewardBarHeight + RewardGap` under the first: check by hand that it clears the members' cards.
  - **Verify by hand:** the menu header reads "100 G" on a new game; win against the goblins: a GOLD row under EXP with the amount, then the menu shows it added; beat the training dummy: no gold row.
