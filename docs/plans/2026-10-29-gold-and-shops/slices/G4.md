# G4. Gold with its coin icon on the victory screen and in the menu header

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 103, 380 (a number with a coin icon, no currency name). References: `docs/ui-reference/battle-victory.jpg` (reward rows), `shop-sell.webp` and `menu-tabs.webp` (a coin before the amount, top right). After G3. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (coin icon), "Replacement mapping".

- **G4. Gold with its coin icon on the victory screen and in the menu header** (Game + art + text, raw 105, about 170 lines at 1.6x: adds a view type)
  - **Goal:** the menu header shows the party's gold as the coin icon and the number, left of the map name, measured when it changes; the victory screen shows a GOLD reward row under EXP, its value with the coin, when the battle gave gold (none for the training dummy).
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Ui/GoldAmountView`: draws an amount of gold: the coin icon, then the number, right-aligned at a point. `GoldAmountView(SpriteFontBase font)` (the caller's font, which also measures the amount); `Draw(SpriteBatch batch, TextureLibrary textures, Place place, float opacity)` with `Place(MeasuredText Amount, Vector2 RightEdge, Color Color)`; the icon scaled to the font's line height (point-scaled by a whole factor, the art rule).
  - **Changes:**
    - Art: `content/art/icons/Gold.png`, a copy of `Item.png` as the placeholder; `Ui/UiArtFiles.Icons.Gold` (checked by `UiArtFilesTests`, as every UI icon).
    - `Menus/MenuHeader`: measures the gold (`NumberText`) when the purse's gold changes; draws it with `GoldAmountView` left of the map name; its summary names it.
    - `Battle/VictoryScreenView.RewardRows(BattleSpoils spoils)`: EXP, then a gold row when `spoils.Gold > 0` (label `victory_gold`, its value drawn with the coin); `VictoryScreen` keeps the spoils' gold. About +8 lines to the view (263): the backlog's `VictoryCardsView` split stays in the backlog (it waits for a change to the cards).
    - Text `victory_gold` ("GOLD"); `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `VictoryScreenViewTests: RewardRows_WithGold_ListsExperienceThenGold`
    - `VictoryScreenViewTests: RewardRows_NoGold_IsExperienceOnly` (renames `RewardRows_ExperienceOnly_IsOneRow`)
    - `MenuHeaderTests: Update_GoldChanged_ShowsTheNewAmount` (an internal `ShownGold` read accessor; new file if none)
    - Existing: `UiArtFilesTests` finds `Gold.png`.
  - **Watch:** the second reward bar sits `RewardBarHeight + RewardGap` under the first: check by hand that it clears the members' cards. No allocation in `Draw`: the amount is measured on change.
  - **Verify by hand:** the menu header reads the coin and 100 on a new game; win against the goblins: a GOLD row under EXP with the coin and the amount, then the menu shows it added; beat the training dummy: no gold row.
