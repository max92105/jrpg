# I1. A priced rest waits for payment

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Question 222 (paying for rest), decision 257 (the inn's beat). ADR 0006 (a step waits for the Game), ADR 0024 (`PartyMember.Rest`). After X1 and G1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (the inn's rule is Core's).

- **I1. A priced rest waits for payment: `InnStay`** (Core + Data, about 190 lines)
  - **Goal:** a `rest` step may carry a price. A free one (no price) rests at once, as today. A priced one waits without resting; the Game pays through `ExplorationState.CurrentInnStay.TryPay()`, which takes the price and rests every member, or refuses (short of gold, or already paid) and changes nothing; continuing without paying leaves the party as it was. No content has a price yet (I3), and the Game still shows a priced rest as a free one until I2b (known gap: tests only).
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Exploration/InnStay`: public sealed class; a priced rest waiting for its answer. `int Price`; `bool CanPay` (the purse holds the price and it isn't paid yet); `bool TryPay()` (spends, rests every member; false and nothing changed when it can't).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Events/RestStep.Price` (`int?` init; null rests free). Left out of a file while null (`AbsentWhenNullFilter`), so today's `"type": "rest"` reads unchanged.
    - `Exploration/EventRun`: a priced `RestStep` sets `CurrentInnStay` (with `_party`) instead of resting, and waits; `CurrentInnStay` clears on the next step, as `CurrentReceipt` does. Its summary names the inn.
    - `Exploration/ExplorationState.CurrentInnStay` (from the run; 3 lines after X1).
    - Data: `Maps/MapEventValidator`: a rest price is 1 to `Purse.MaxGold` ("rest price must be …").
  - **Tests:**
    - `EventRunInnTests: Advance_PricedRest_WaitsWithoutResting` (new file: `EventRunTests` is 269)
    - `EventRunInnTests: Advance_FreeRest_RestsAtOnceWithNoInnStay` (the case the rule doesn't apply to)
    - `InnStayTests: TryPay_EnoughGold_TakesPriceAndRestsParty`
    - `InnStayTests: TryPay_ShortOfGold_ReturnsFalseAndChangesNothing`
    - `InnStayTests: TryPay_Twice_PaysOnce`
    - `EventRunInnTests: Continue_WithoutPaying_LeavesThePartyUnrested`
    - `MapEventValidatorTests: Validate_RestPriceZero_Throws`
    - `ContentFileWriterTests: Write_EveryEventStepKind_ReadsBackAsTheSameSteps`: `new RestStep { Price = 20 }` added beside the free one.
  - **Call sites:** none (`RestStep` keeps its parameterless form).
  - **Watch:** `ExplorationState` stays under 300 after X1. `MapEventValidatorTests` 169 (+12).
  - **Verify by hand:** none yet (no content prices a rest until I3).
