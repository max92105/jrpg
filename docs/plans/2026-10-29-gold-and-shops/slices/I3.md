# I3. The editor's rest step takes a price; the innkeeper charges

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). ADR 0012 (editor architecture). After I2b (the game shows a priced rest), so content with a price lands once both the game and the editor handle it (the plan lessons). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (inn price), "Risks" (content written by the editor).

- **I3. The editor's rest step takes a price; the innkeeper charges** (Editor + content, about 150 lines)
  - **Goal:** the event dialogs show a rest step's price and edit it (empty for a free rest), with undo as every field; an editor save keeps it. The start map's innkeeper charges 20 (placeholder) and says so.
  - **New types:** none.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Editor/Events/EventStepViewModel`: `PriceText` (`[ObservableProperty]`, empty by default); `Label` "Rest" or "Rest for 20 gold"; `Problem` "The price must be empty or a whole number of at least 1." (and at most `Purse.MaxGold`); `From` reads `RestStep.Price`; `ToStep` writes it (null when empty); `IsRest` for the field's visibility.
    - `Editor/Events/EventStepEditor.xaml`: the price box under a rest step.
    - Content (through the editor): the innkeeper's line "A placeholder inn. Rest here for 20 gold." and its rest step's price 20.
  - **Tests:**
    - `EventStepViewModelRestTests: FromStep_PricedRest_ShowsItsPrice` (new file: `EventStepViewModelTests` is 260)
    - `EventStepViewModelRestTests: ToStep_EmptyPrice_ReturnsFreeRest`
    - `EventStepViewModelRestTests: ToStep_Price_ReturnsPricedRest`
    - `EventStepViewModelRestTests: Problem_ZeroPrice_Explains`
    - `RepositoryInnTests: StartMap_Innkeeper_ChargesForRest` (Data tests; asserts the step it needs, not a total)
    - Existing: `FromStep_RestStep_ShowsRestWithNoFields` keeps passing for a free rest.
  - **Watch:** the Game tests that talk to the innkeeper (`StartMapVillagerTests`, `EventPresenterRestTests` on the start map) now meet the question: answer it in those tests, or point them at a free rest; budget the fixes after a grep of `innkeeper` in the Game tests.
  - **Verify by hand:** in the editor, open the innkeeper's event: "Rest for 20 gold"; clear the price: "Rest"; Ctrl+Z puts it back; save and reopen. In the game, the innkeeper asks for 20 G.
