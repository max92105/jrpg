# L1. One overlay fade and one close request for the screens an event opens

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decision 370 (a screen over the map fades itself in). The forge plan's backlog line: "`ForgeMotion` is the third copy of the 'opening counted from the first drawn frame, closing from Cancel' fade, after `MenuMotion` and `TitleEntrance` (F1d): extract one frame-fade type in `Ui/`." The Shop would be the fourth. After S6. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Replacement mapping", "Key decisions" (one way back).

- **L1. One overlay fade and one close request for the screens an event opens** (Game refactor, about 120 lines)
  - **Goal:** what the Forge and the Shop (H1) share moves out of the Forge first, unchanged: its fade to `Ui/`, its close request to one request both screens return. `MenuMotion` (with the tabs' slide) and `TitleEntrance` (from black) stay: they do more than this fade.
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Ui/OverlayFade`: a screen over the map fading in from its first drawn frame and out once asked (decision 370), at the menu's placeholder times (0.15 s in, 0.12 s out). The members `Crafting/ForgeMotion` has today: `IsClosing`, `IsClosingDone`, `Opacity`, `MarkDrawn()`, `Advance(TimeSpan)`, `StartClosing()`.
    - `Game/Scenes/EventScreenClosed`: the request a screen a map event opened (the Forge, the Shop) returns once it has faded out.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Crafting/ForgeMotion` removed (moved, its summary reworded for any overlay); `ForgeScene` holds an `OverlayFade` and returns `EventScreenClosed`.
    - `Scenes/ForgeClosed` removed; `GameFlow.Start`'s case reads `EventScreenClosed` (still through `ForgeTransitions` until E1).
    - Tests: `ForgeClosed` → `EventScreenClosed` in the Forge's scene and flow tests (mechanical).
    - The backlog line is edited: `MenuMotion` and `TitleEntrance` remain separate, and why.
  - **Tests:**
    - `OverlayFadeTests: Opacity_BeforeFirstDraw_StaysClear` (new file: the type gets its second user, and has no test of its own today)
    - `OverlayFadeTests: IsClosingDone_AfterTheClosingTime_IsTrue`
    - Existing: `ForgeSceneTests: Update_Cancel_RequestsCloseOnceTheFadeHasRun` and `GameFlowForgeTests` pass with the renamed request.
  - **Verify by hand:** at the forge master: the Forge fades in and out as before, and the event goes on after it closes.
