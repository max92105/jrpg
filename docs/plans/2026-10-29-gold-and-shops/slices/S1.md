# S1. Buying in Core

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decision 344 (a plain piece from outside the drops). After G1 and the "ADR: gold and trade" step. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (quotes; `Shop` buys, `ItemSales` sells; refusal order), "Structure and folder dependencies".

- **S1. Buying in Core: prices on items, `Shop`, its quotes and refusals** (Core, about 210 lines)
  - **Goal:** an item may have a price; a shop sells the items of its stock. Each purchase is quoted first: its price, and why it's refused (the item's stack is full, or the purse is short). Buying takes the price and adds one copy: a stack grows by one, gear comes as one plain Common piece. A refused or unlisted purchase changes nothing. No content or screen yet (S3, Phase 4).
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Trade/ShopDefinition(string Id, string Name, IReadOnlyList<string> ItemIds)`: public sealed record; one shop as content describes it: its name and what it sells, in order.
    - `Core/Trade/TradeRefusal`: public enum; why a trade is refused: `StackFull`, `ShortOfGold` (S2 adds `NotForSale`, `PurseFull`).
    - `Core/Trade/TradeQuote(int Gold, TradeRefusal? Refusal)`: public sealed record; one trade as offered: the gold it costs or gives, and its refusal (null when it can be done); `bool IsRefused`.
    - `Core/Trade/Shop`: public sealed class, internal constructor; buying from one shop's stock. `Shop(ShopDefinition definition, IReadOnlyList<ItemDefinition> stock, Inventory inventory, Purse purse)`; `string Name`; `IReadOnlyList<ItemDefinition> Stock`; `TradeQuote QuoteBuy(ItemDefinition item)`; `bool TryBuy(ItemDefinition item)` (false when not in the stock or refused).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Items/ItemDefinition.Price` (`int?` init): what it costs in a shop; null is never bought or sold (S3 loads and checks it).
  - **Tests** (`ShopBuyTests`, new file; a two-item stock from `ItemDefinitionBuilder`):
    - `QuoteBuy_EnoughGold_QuotesThePriceUnrefused`
    - `QuoteBuy_ShortOfGold_RefusedShortOfGold`
    - `QuoteBuy_FullStack_RefusedStackFull`
    - `QuoteBuy_FullStackAndShortOfGold_RefusedStackFull` (the order)
    - `QuoteBuy_Gear_NeverStackFull` (gear has no cap: the case the stack rule doesn't apply to)
    - `TryBuy_Consumable_AddsOneAndTakesThePrice`
    - `TryBuy_Gear_AddsOnePlainPiece`
    - `TryBuy_Refused_ChangesNothing`
    - `TryBuy_ItemNotInStock_ReturnsFalse`
  - **Call sites:** none.
  - **Watch:** `Shop`'s constructor is at four parameters; a fifth means it does too much. Core trusts validated content for a listed item's price (`item.Price ?? throw` naming the item, a content bug).
  - **Verify by hand:** none (Core only).
