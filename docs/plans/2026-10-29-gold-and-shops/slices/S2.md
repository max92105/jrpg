# S2. Selling in Core

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 358 (nothing destroyed for less than it yields), 369 (owned pieces only). After S1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions", "Extension points" (rarity-priced sales).

- **S2. Selling in Core: the sell share, `ItemSales`** (Core, about 230 lines)
  - **Goal:** an owned priced item sells for a share of its price (`rules.json` `trade.sellPercent`, S3), rounded down: one copy of a stack, or one owned piece (worn ones aren't owned). Each sale is quoted first: not for sale (no price, or worth 0), or the purse can't take the gold. A rolled piece sells as a plain one (Q1's placeholder). Selling is the same at every shop, so it lives on the session.
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Trade/TradeRules`: public sealed record; `rules.json` `trade`: `int SellPercent { get; init; }` (0 by default: every sale refused, as empty crafting rules refuse every action).
    - `Core/Trade/PriceFormula`: the trade's numbers in one place. `static int SalePrice(int price, int sellPercent)`: `price × sellPercent / 100`, rounded down once, in a `long` (comment: the shop takes the rest, the classic JRPG half).
    - `Core/Trade/ItemSales`: public sealed class, internal constructor; selling owned items for gold. `ItemSales(Inventory inventory, Purse purse, TradeRules rules)`; `TradeQuote QuoteSale(ItemDefinition item)`, `bool TrySell(ItemDefinition item)` (one copy of a stack); `TradeQuote QuoteSale(GearPiece piece)`, `bool TrySell(GearPiece piece)` (the first equal owned piece).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/TradeRefusal`: `NotForSale`, `PurseFull`.
    - `Content/GameRules.Trade { get; init; } = new()` (init, as `Crafting`, so no constructor site changes); its summary names it.
    - `Session/GameSession.Sales` (built with `Inventory`, `Party.Purse`, `Rules.Trade`).
