# S2. What a rolled piece is worth; buying a stock piece

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 375 (affix gold values; a piece's price is its base price plus its affixes' values), 385 (a stock piece is one of a kind). ADR 0030 (affixes are a content pool). After S1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (what a thing is worth; the stock).

- **S2. What a rolled piece is worth; buying a stock piece** (Core + Data + content, about 225 lines)
  - **Goal:** every affix has a gold value in `affixes.json`; a piece's price is its base item's price plus its affixes' values (a plain piece: the base price). A stock holds rolled pieces besides its stacks; buying one is quoted (short of gold refuses; gear never fills a stack), takes the price, moves that very piece into the inventory and out of the stock.
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Trade/Pricing`: public sealed class; what items and pieces cost in a shop. `Pricing(IReadOnlyDictionary<string, int> affixValues)` (an affix id to its gold value, so `Trade` doesn't read `Loot`); `int PriceOf(ItemDefinition item)`; `int PriceOf(GearPiece piece)`.
    - `Core/Trade/PriceFormula`: the trade's numbers in one place. `static int PieceValue(int basePrice, IEnumerable<int> affixValues)` (summed in a `long`, capped at `Purse.MaxGold`; comment: a rolled piece is worth its base plus what each affix adds, decision 375).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Loot/AffixDefinition.GoldValue` (required int): what the affix adds to a piece's price. The 3 `new AffixDefinition` test sites give one.
    - `Trade/ShopStock(ShopDefinition definition, IReadOnlyList<ItemDefinition> stacks, IEnumerable<GearPiece> pieces, int rolledAtLevel)`: `Pieces`, `RolledAtLevel`, `internal bool TryTake(GearPiece piece)` (the first equal piece).
    - `Trade/Shop(ShopStock stock, Inventory inventory, Purse purse, Pricing pricing)`: `TradeQuote QuoteBuy(GearPiece piece)`, `bool TryBuy(GearPiece piece)` (false when the stock doesn't hold it or the quote is refused); stacks priced through `Pricing`.
    - Data: `Loot/AffixCatalogValidator`: `goldValue` within 0 and `Purse.MaxGold`, naming the affix.
    - Content: `affixes.json` `goldValue` 20 on each of the 23 affixes (placeholders). The JSON name is `goldValue`: saves use `value` for an affix's rolled stat.
  - **Tests:**
    - `PriceFormulaTests: PieceValue_BaseAndAffixes_AddsThem`
    - `PriceFormulaTests: PieceValue_PastMaxGold_IsMaxGold`
    - `PricingTests: PriceOf_PlainPiece_IsTheBasePrice`
    - `PricingTests: PriceOf_RolledPiece_AddsItsAffixValues`
    - `ShopPieceTests: QuoteBuy_Piece_QuotesItsPrice` (new file: `ShopBuyTests` holds the stacks)
    - `ShopPieceTests: QuoteBuy_PieceShortOfGold_Refused`
    - `ShopPieceTests: TryBuy_Piece_MovesThatPieceFromStockToInventory`
    - `ShopPieceTests: TryBuy_PieceNotInStock_ReturnsFalse`
    - `AffixCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_NegativeGoldValue_ThrowsNamingTheAffix`
    - `AffixCatalogValidatorTests: Validate_GoldValueAboveMaxGold_Throws`
  - **Call sites:** `new ShopStock(` and `new Shop(` in S1's tests (a shared helper); `new AffixDefinition` (3).
  - **Verify by hand:** none (Core only); the game still starts with the new `affixes.json`.
