# S5. The shops' stocks: rolled once, rerolled after a leader level

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decision 385 (rolled once on first opening, kept, rerolled once the leader has gained a level; flagged interpretations), 349 (luck is a drop's). ADR 0030 (one `GearRoll`, pool in id order). After S4. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (the stock), "Risks" (determinism).

- **S5. The shops' stocks: rolled on first opening, rerolled after a leader level** (Core, about 160 lines)
  - **Goal:** `GameSession.ShopFor(shopId)` opens a shop: the first time, each gear entry of its list rolls into one piece through the session's shared `GearRoll` (in list order, no luck), its stacks resolved and never rolled, and the stock is kept; later openings find the same stock, drawing nothing, until the leader's level is above the level it was rolled at, when the next opening rolls it again. A bought piece stays gone until then.
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Session/ShopStocks`: internal sealed; every opened shop's stock by id, rolled when missing or stale. `ShopStocks(ContentCatalog catalog, GearRoll gearRoll)`; `ShopStock StockFor(string shopId, int leaderLevel)` (S6 adds capture and restore).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Session/GameSession.ShopFor(string shopId)`: `new Shop(_stocks.StockFor(shopId, Party.Leader.Level), Inventory, Party.Purse, _pricing)`; throws on an unknown id, naming it. The `GearRollFor` comment names the third user.
  - **Tests** (`GameSessionShopTests`, new file; the repository catalog, a fixed seed):
    - `ShopFor_FirstOpening_RollsOnePiecePerGearEntryInOrder` (pinned rarities and affixes)
    - `ShopFor_Stacks_AreNeverRolled` (the stacks are the catalog's items; the case the roll doesn't apply to)
    - `ShopFor_OpenedAgain_SameStockAndNoDraw`
    - `ShopFor_LeaderGainedALevel_RollsAgain`
    - `ShopFor_PieceBought_StaysGoneUntilTheReroll`
    - `ShopFor_UnknownShop_ThrowsNamingIt`
  - **Call sites:** none.
  - **Watch:** `GameSession` about 268 (+6): the stocks live in `ShopStocks`, not in the session. A test that checks the random wasn't drawn reads `FixedSequenceRandom`'s position.
  - **Verify by hand:** none (Core only).
