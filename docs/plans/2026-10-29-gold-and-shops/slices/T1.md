# T1. Buying at the Shop

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decision 370 (a refused action greyed with its reason). Reference: `docs/ui-reference/shop-sell.webp`. After L2b. The forge plan's F2a and F2c are the model. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (quotes, refusal order), README "Decisions" (one per Confirm; buying goes at once).

- **T1. Buying at the Shop: Confirm buys one; a refused row greyed with its reason** (Game + text, about 190 lines; drawn by `ShopDetailView`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** on the Buy tab, the highlighted refused row's reason shows in the detail column ("Not enough gold.", "You can't carry more."). Confirm on an available row buys one copy through `Shop.TryBuy`: the gold in the header drops, the owned count rises, every row's quote is asked again (a purchase can grey others), the cursor stays, and a notice says what was bought ("Bought: Potion.") until the cursor moves or the tab changes. Confirm on a greyed row does nothing.
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Trade/TradeRefusalNames`: each `TradeRefusal`'s message id, as `Crafting/ForgeRefusalNames` (`static string IdOf(TradeRefusal refusal)`).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: Confirm on the Buy tab; `Notice` (string?), cleared on a move or a tab switch; after a purchase `StockList.Refresh()`, `SaleList.Refresh()` and the header's gold.
    - `Trade/ShopDetailView`: the reason line for a refused row, or the notice.
    - Text `shop_refused_short_of_gold`, `shop_refused_stack_full`, `shop_refused_not_for_sale`, `shop_refused_purse_full` (all four now, so the names test covers every refusal), `shop_bought` ("Bought: {name}."); `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests:**

    - `TradeRefusalNamesTests: IdOf_EveryRefusal_HasItsOwnLine`
  - **Watch:** `ShopScene` size (B2's watch): carrying out trades moves to a `Trade/ShopTrades` presenter if the scene would pass 230.
