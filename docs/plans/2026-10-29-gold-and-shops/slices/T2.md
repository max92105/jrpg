# T2. The quantity picker; buying stacks

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decision 377 (the picker: Up and Down by one, wrapping; Left and Right by ten; from 1 to Core's most), 379. Reference: `docs/ui-reference/shop-sell.webp`. After T1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (the Shop's controls).

- **T2. The quantity picker; buying stacks with it** (Game + text, about 220 lines; drawn by `ShopDetailView`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** Confirm on an available Buy stack opens a quantity picker at 1, drawn in the detail column's reason line: "Quantity ◂ 3 ▸" and the total with the coin. Up and Down change the count by one, wrapping between 1 and `Shop.MostBuyable(item)`; Left and Right by ten, stopping at the ends. Confirm buys that many through `Shop.TryBuy(item, count)`: the gold drops, the owned count rises, the lists refresh, the notice says "Bought: Potion x3."; Cancel closes the picker (Cancel wins when nested). The total is Core's quote for the count.
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Trade/QuantityPicker`: a count chosen between 1 and a most, and its total formatted when it changes. `QuantityPicker(int most, Func<int, TradeQuote> quote)`; `int Count`; `TradeQuote Quote` (Core's quote for the count, asked when it changes; the view measures its gold then); `Up()`, `Down()`, `StepUp()`, `StepDown()` (by ten). Shared with T3's sales.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: `QuantityPicker? Picker`; Confirm on a Buy stack opens it; its input while open; Confirm buys; Left and Right go to the picker while it is open (not the member switch).
    - `Trade/ShopDetailView`: the picker in the reason line's place.
    - Text `shop_quantity` ("Quantity"), `shop_bought_count` ("Bought: {name} x{count}."); `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuantityPickerTests: Up_AtTheMost_WrapsToOne` (new file)
    - `QuantityPickerTests: Down_AtOne_WrapsToTheMost`
    - `QuantityPickerTests: StepUp_NearTheMost_StopsAtTheMost`
    - `QuantityPickerTests: Up_TotalFollowsTheQuote`
    - `ShopSceneQuantityTests: Confirm_Stack_OpensThePickerAtOne` (new file)
    - `ShopSceneQuantityTests: ConfirmInPicker_BuysTheCountAndTakesTheTotal`
    - `ShopSceneQuantityTests: Cancel_InPicker_ClosesItOnly` (the list keeps the cursor; nothing bought)
    - `ShopSceneQuantityTests: Right_InPicker_StepsTheCountNotTheMember`
  - **Watch:** the picker's limit is Core's (`MostBuyable`): no count rule in the Game. `ShopScene` size (B2's watch).
  - **Verify by hand:** at the shopkeeper with 100 G: Confirm on Potion: "Quantity 1", 10 with the coin; Up to 3: 30; Down from 1 wraps to 10 (what 100 G pays); Right from 1: 10; Confirm: 70 G left, "Bought: Potion x3."; Cancel in the picker buys nothing.

- **Done, with deviations:** the buying moved out of `ShopScene` (282 lines after T1) into a new `Purchase` (the piece bought at once, the stack's picker and its input, the lists' refresh, the notice), the scene keeping the tabs, the cursor, the preview and the refusal line. The picker's arrows are two small diamonds (decision 253), not glyphs the fonts may lack; `ShopDetailView` draws the picker in the notice's place and takes the coin's textures. `ShopDetailView.Place` folds the member and the changes into a `MemberPreview` struct, staying at four parameters. Review: the trade's notice now shows over a refused row's reason, since buying the most can grey the row and hide "Bought".
