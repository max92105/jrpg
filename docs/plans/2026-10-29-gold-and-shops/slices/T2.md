# T2. Selling at the Shop

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 354 (destroying a piece asks first, the salvage precedent), 358, 369. Reference: `docs/ui-reference/dialog-confirm.webp`. After T1. The forge plan's F2b is the model. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Replacement mapping" (`PieceName`), README "Decisions" (a piece asks; a stack goes at once).

- **T2. Selling at the Shop: Confirm sells one; a piece asks first** (Game + text, about 210 lines; reuses `Ui/ConfirmPromptView`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** on the Sell tab, Confirm on an available stack sells one copy through `ItemSales.TrySell` at once; on an available piece it asks "Sell the Magic Bronze Sword for 25 G?" (`Ui/ConfirmPrompt`), and Yes sells it. The gold in the header rises, the lists refresh, the cursor stays on the same row (or the last, when the row left), and a notice says what was sold ("Sold: Potion for 5 G.") until the cursor moves. No or Cancel keeps the piece; Cancel wins when nested: the prompt, then the Shop. Confirm on a greyed row does nothing and its reason shows.
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Ui/PieceName`: a piece's name as the player reads it in a question: the base name, with its rarity first when it rolled ("Magic Bronze Sword"). `static string Of(GearPiece piece, UiMessages messages)`; moved from `Crafting/ForgeScene.NameOf` (its second user), the message `forge_rolled_piece_name` renamed `rolled_piece_name`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: Confirm on the Sell tab; the prompt state (`ConfirmPrompt? Prompt`); after a sale the refreshes and the notice, the cursor kept.
    - `Trade/ShopView`: the prompt through `ConfirmPromptView`.
    - `Crafting/ForgeScene`: calls `PieceName.Of` (its salvage question reads the same).
    - Text `shop_sell_confirm` ("Sell the {name} for {price}?"), `shop_sold` ("Sold: {name} for {price}."), `rolled_piece_name` (renamed); `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests** (`ShopSceneSellTests`, new file):
    - `Confirm_Stack_SellsOneAndAddsGold`
    - `Confirm_Piece_AsksFirst` (the piece is still owned)
    - `PromptYes_SellsThePiece`
    - `PromptNo_KeepsThePiece`
    - `Cancel_InPrompt_ClosesThePromptOnly` (the Shop stays open)
    - `Confirm_NotForSale_DoesNothing` (Scrap: no prompt, nothing changes)
    - `PromptYes_LastRow_CursorMovesToTheNewLast`
    - `PieceNameTests: Of_RolledPiece_PutsTheRarityFirst`
    - `PieceNameTests: Of_PlainPiece_IsTheBaseName` (the case the rarity doesn't apply to)
    - Existing: `ForgeSceneSalvageTests` pass unchanged (the question's text is the same).
  - **Verify by hand:** at the shopkeeper: sell a potion: the gold rises by half its price, the count drops, "Sold: …"; sell a Magic piece: the prompt names it with its rarity, starts on No; Yes: gone from the list, gold up; Scrap greyed "Not for sale", Confirm does nothing. At the forge: Salvage's question still names a rolled piece with its rarity.
