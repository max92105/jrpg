# T3. Selling at the Shop

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 377 (the picker), 378, 379 (only selling a gear piece asks first), 380 (a question's price drawn under it with the coin), 354 (destroying a piece asks first, the salvage precedent), 369. Reference: `docs/ui-reference/dialog-confirm.webp`. After T2. The forge plan's F2b is the model. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Replacement mapping" (`PieceName`).

- **T3. Selling: stacks with the picker, a piece behind a priced prompt** (Game + text, about 235 lines; reuses `Ui/ConfirmPromptView`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** on the Sell list, Confirm on an available stack opens T2's picker (1 to `ItemSales.MostSellable`, the total the sale's quote), and Confirm sells that many at once. Confirm on an available piece asks "Sell the Magic Bronze Sword?" with its sale price under the question (the coin and the number), No first; Yes sells it. The gold in the header rises, the lists refresh, the cursor stays on the row (or the last), and a notice says what was sold ("Sold: Potion x3.") until the cursor moves. No or Cancel keeps the piece; Cancel wins when nested: the prompt or picker, the list, the tabs. Confirm on a greyed row does nothing and its reason shows.
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Ui/PieceName`: a piece's name as the player reads it in a question: the base name, with its rarity first when it rolled ("Magic Bronze Sword"). `static string Of(GearPiece piece, UiMessages messages)`; moved from `Crafting/ForgeScene.NameOf` (its second user), the message `forge_rolled_piece_name` renamed `rolled_piece_name`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Ui/ConfirmPrompt.Price` (`int?` init) and `Ui/ConfirmPromptView`: a priced question draws the price under it through `GoldAmountView` (the window one line taller); I2b's inn question reuses it.
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: Confirm on the Sell list; the prompt state (`ConfirmPrompt? Prompt`); after a sale the refreshes and the notice, the cursor kept.
    - `Trade/ShopView`: the prompt through `ConfirmPromptView`.
    - `Crafting/ForgeScene`: calls `PieceName.Of` (its salvage question reads the same).
    - Text `shop_sell_confirm` ("Sell the {name}?"), `shop_sold` ("Sold: {name}."), `shop_sold_count` ("Sold: {name} x{count}."), `rolled_piece_name` (renamed); `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests** (`ShopSceneSellTests`, new file):
    - `ConfirmInPicker_Stack_SellsTheCountAndAddsGold`
    - `Confirm_Piece_AsksWithItsPrice` (the piece is still owned; the prompt's price is the quote's)
    - `PromptYes_SellsThePiece`
    - `PromptNo_KeepsThePiece`
    - `Cancel_InPrompt_ClosesThePromptOnly` (the Shop stays open)
    - `Confirm_NotForSale_DoesNothing` (Scrap: no picker, nothing changes)
    - `PromptYes_LastRow_CursorMovesToTheNewLast`
    - `PieceNameTests: Of_RolledPiece_PutsTheRarityFirst` (new file)
    - `PieceNameTests: Of_PlainPiece_IsTheBaseName` (the case the rarity doesn't apply to)
    - Existing: `ForgeSceneSalvageTests` pass unchanged (the question's text is the same); `ConfirmPromptView` callers without a price draw as before.
  - **Watch:** `ShopScene` size (B2's watch); `ConfirmPromptView` 74 (+10).
  - **Verify by hand:** at the shopkeeper: sell 3 potions with the picker: the gold rises by three half-prices, "Sold: Potion x3."; sell a Magic piece: the prompt names it with its rarity and shows its price with the coin, starts on No; Yes: gone from the list, gold up; Scrap greyed "Not for sale", Confirm does nothing. At the forge: Salvage's question still names a rolled piece with its rarity.

- **Done, with deviations:** T2's `Purchase` became `ShopCounter`, which owns both lists and every deal (buying, the picker, the sale's question), so `ShopScene` stays under 300 lines; `QuantityPicker.Update(input)` answers Yes or No as `ConfirmPrompt` does, so the counter reads both the same way. The lists' cursors wrap (`ListCursor.Step`), so the tests' "move until" helpers fail on coming back to the start rather than on a stopped cursor; the last-row test sells the rolled sword added last. `ConfirmPrompt.Price` is drawn by `ConfirmPromptView` in a 40 px line over the buttons. Review: a piece's notice names it as the question does (`PieceName`), and the open deal is one value (`OpenDeal`: the picker or the question, and what Yes does).
