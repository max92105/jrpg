# B1. The impact's sounds, each at its beat

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S2 (`BattleScene.Sounds`). Decision 423. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 5, "Risks" (cross-plan order, files near 300).

- **B1. A turn's impact sounds its hits, critical hits, misses, blocks, resists, statuses and heals, each at its beat** (Game, about 245 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** when a turn's playback shows, every impact event sounds as the impact beat starts: a critical hit's sound if any damage was critical, else the hit sound; a miss, a block, a resisted status, a status applied, a heal (restored HP, a revive, life stolen); each file once per update (decision 423). The timing every battle sound uses lands here: each sound at the start of the beat that shows its event. The other event kinds are listed silent until B2 (and `BattleEnded` stays silent: B4's outcome screens sound it); an event kind the switch doesn't know throws, as `ActionPlayback` does.
  - **New types:**
    - `Battle/TimedSound` (readonly record struct `(string File, TimeSpan At)`): one sound of a turn and when, from the playback's start, it plays.
    - `Battle/BattleSounds` (internal static): the sounds a turn's events make, each at the start of its beat. `public static IReadOnlyList<TimedSound> Of(IReadOnlyList<BattleEvent> events, Func<PlaybackBeatKind, TimeSpan> startOf)`; sorted by `At`; one switch for the impact's events and one for the rest, so neither passes the complexity limit.
    - `Battle/PlaybackSoundQueue`: asks the shown playback's sounds as its time reaches each, once. `Follow(ActionPlayback? playback, AskedSounds sounds)`: a new playback restarts from its first sound; no allocation per frame.
  - **New files:** `content/audio/effects/PlaceholderHit.wav`, `...Critical`, `...Miss`, `...Block`, `...Resisted`, `...StatusApplied`, `...Heal` (5 ms fades, pitches apart).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Ui/UiSoundFiles.Battle` (in `effects/`): `Hit`, `Critical`, `Miss`, `Block`, `Resisted`, `StatusApplied`, `Heal`; `UiSoundFilesTests` gains the group.
    - `Battle/ActionPlayback` (252, about +6): `Sounds` (`IReadOnlyList<TimedSound>`), set at the end of `Start` from `BattleSounds.Of(events, playback.StartOf)`; the summary says the sounds follow the beats.
    - `Battle/BattleScene` (about 260 after S2, +4): a `PlaybackSoundQueue` followed after `_sequence.Update`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `BattleSoundsTests` (new): `Of_DamageLanded_IsTheHitAtTheImpactsStart` (a `startOf` giving the impact 0.4 s, so the time is pinned), `Of_CriticalAmongHits_IsTheCriticalOnly`, `Of_HealLanded_IsTheHeal`, `Of_LifeStolen_IsTheHeal`, `Of_Miss_IsTheMiss`, `Of_Block_IsTheBlock`, `Of_StatusResisted_IsResisted`, `Of_StatusApplied_IsTheStatusSound`
    - `PlaybackSoundQueueTests` (new): `Follow_TimeNotYetReached_AsksNothing`, `Follow_TimeReached_AsksItOnce`
    - `ActionPlaybackSoundTests` (new: `ActionPlaybackTests` is 287): `Start_DamageLanded_SoundsTheHit` (the wiring; B2 pins later beats' times here)
  - **Verify by hand:** a forest battle: an attack's hit sounds with its number; a critical sounds different; a miss, a block and a resisted status each have their own; a heal sounds; a skill on several targets sounds its hit once.
