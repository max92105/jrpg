# B1. Impact and victory sounds

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S1. Pending Q4 (victory) and Q9 (other happenings). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 3, "Extension points".

- **B1. A turn's impact sounds its hits, critical hits, misses and statuses; victory sounds** (Game, about 180 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** when a turn's playback shows its impact, the battle scene asks `battleCritical` if any hit was critical, else `battleHit` if any damage landed; `battleMiss` if any hit missed; `statusApplied` if any status was applied; each once, together. When the victory screen opens it asks `victory`, once. Everything else is silent (README, "Decisions"; Q9). Core is unchanged: the sounds are read from the events `ActionPlayback` already reads.
  - **New types:**
    - `Battle/ImpactSounds` (internal static): the effect ids a turn's impact plays. `public static IReadOnlyList<string> Of(IReadOnlyList<BattleEvent> events)`; built once per turn, not per frame. Unknown events are silent, not errors (sound is optional; `ActionPlayback` still throws on them).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Battle/ActionPlayback` (252, +3): `ImpactSounds` (`IReadOnlyList<string>`), set in `Start` from `ImpactSounds.Of(events)`; the summary mentions it.
    - `Battle/BattleScene` (about 262 after P1 and S2, about +8): its `AskedSounds` (from S2); `ShowPlayback` asks `AskAll(playback.ImpactSounds)`; `Finish` asks `victory` when it opens the victory screen.
  - **Tests:**
    - `ImpactSoundsTests` (new): `Of_DamageLanded_IsBattleHit`, `Of_CriticalAmongHits_IsBattleCriticalOnly`, `Of_Miss_IsBattleMiss`, `Of_HitAndStatusApplied_AsksBoth`, `Of_HealOnly_IsSilent`, `Of_BlockedHit_IsSilent` (Q9's placeholder; the test is renamed if Q9 gives blocks a sound)
    - `BattleSceneSoundTests` (new; `BattleSceneTests` is 208): `Update_ImpactShown_AsksItsSoundsOnce` (the next update asks nothing), `Update_VictoryScreenOpens_AsksVictory` (a battle the party wins from `BattleScenarios`' certain-chance content, so no seed matters)
  - **Verify by hand:** a forest battle: an attack's hit sounds at the impact, with the number; a critical sounds different; a miss has its own; a status skill sounds once even on several targets; the victory screen sounds once. Effects at 0: silent.
