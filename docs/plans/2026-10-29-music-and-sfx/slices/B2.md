# B2. Cues on the battle's commands

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After B1 (`BattleScene.Sounds`). Pending Q7. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 3.

- **B2. Cues on the battle's commands, skill and item lists and target cursor** (Game, about 140 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** while a party member chooses, Up and Down in the commands, the skill and item lists and the target cursor tick `menuMove` when the selection changes; Confirm that opens a list or the target cursor, or submits a command, sounds `menuConfirm`; Cancel back a step sounds `menuCancel`. A greyed command or skill (not enough of a resource) sounds nothing unless Q7 adds a refused cue.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Battle/CommandMenu` (220, about +12): an `AskedSounds` cleared as its `Update` starts; its own cursor, `BattleSkillChoice`, `BattleItemChoice` and `BattleTargetCursor` compared around their moves; `Sounds` (internal read).
    - `Battle/BattleScene`: `AskAll(_commands.Sounds)` after `_commands.Update` (+1).
  - **Tests** (`CommandMenuSoundTests`, new: `CommandMenuTests` is 393):
    - `Update_Down_AsksMenuMove`
    - `Update_ConfirmSkills_AsksMenuConfirm`
    - `Update_CancelInTheSkillList_AsksMenuCancel`
    - `Update_ConfirmOnATarget_AsksMenuConfirm`
    - `Update_ConfirmOnAGreyedSkill_AsksNothing`
  - **Verify by hand:** a battle: moving through commands, skills, items and targets ticks; choosing sounds; backing out sounds lower; the impact's sounds (B1) follow the confirm without doubling it.
