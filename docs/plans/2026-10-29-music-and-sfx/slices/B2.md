# B2. Every other battle event kind sounds

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After B1. Decision 423. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 5, "Extension points".

- **B2. Every other battle event kind sounds at its beat: falls, ticks, backfires, stuns, gains, moves, items, Defend** (Game, about 200 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** the event kinds B1 left silent sound, each at the start of the beat that shows it (decision 423): a fall (any combatant) at the defeat beat; a Poison tick and a Regen tick at the tick beat, each its own sound; a backfire at its beat; a lost turn (stunned) at the skip beat; an item used and Defend as the action starts; a resource gain, a status ending, a move (places swapped) and an extra turn readied at the playback's start (they have no beat; they show by live state). A resource cost is silent (the skill's sound, B3, covers spending). A skill's action start stays silent until B3; `BattleEnded` stays silent (B4).
  - **New files:** `content/audio/effects/PlaceholderFall.wav`, `...PoisonTick`, `...RegenTick`, `...Backfire`, `...Stunned`, `...ResourceGain`, `...StatusEnded`, `...Move`, `...ExtraTurn`, `...ItemUse`, `...Defend` (5 ms fades).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Ui/UiSoundFiles.Battle`: the eleven constants.
    - `Battle/BattleSounds`: the second switch's cases (`CombatantDefeated`, `StatusTicked` by its status, `Backfired`, `TurnSkipped`, `ResourceChanged` with a positive change, `StatusEnded`, `PlacesSwapped`, `ExtraTurnReadied`, `ActionStarted` with an item or Defend); a status that ticks other than Poison and Regen throws (a new ticking status gets its sound with it).
  - **Tests:**
    - `BattleSoundsTests`: `Of_EnemyFell_IsTheFallAtTheDefeatBeat`, `Of_PoisonTicked_IsThePoisonTickAtTheTickBeat`, `Of_RegenTicked_IsTheRegenTick`, `Of_Backfired_IsTheBackfireAtItsBeat`, `Of_TurnSkipped_IsStunnedAtTheSkipBeat`, `Of_ResourceGained_IsTheGain`, `Of_ResourceSpent_IsSilent`, `Of_ItemUsed_IsTheItemSound`, `Of_Defended_IsTheDefendSound`, `Of_PlacesSwapped_IsTheMove`
    - `ActionPlaybackSoundTests`: `Start_HitThenFall_FallSoundsAtTheDefeatBeatsStart` (the times from the real beats)
  - **Verify by hand:** in a forest fight: a goblin's fall sounds as it fades; Fire Blast's poison ticks at the start of the burned enemy's turn; a heal over time ticks differently; a stun, a backfire, a Move, Defend and a potion each sound; a resource gain on a hit sounds, a skill's cost doesn't.
