# B4. The battle start, the outcomes and their music

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After B1 (and P3, the pause and resume). Decisions 416, 418, 421, 423. ADR 0035 (the flow's timing). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (fanfare hold), "Key decisions" 2, 3 and 6.

- **B4. The battle-start sound at the flash; victory's fanfare over silence, then the map's track; defeat and escape** (Game, about 230 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:**
    - The encounter's flash sounds the battle-start sound once, as the battle theme cuts in (decisions 416, 421); the updates while the flash plays add nothing (the scenes don't update, and the channel is cleared each update).
    - The victory screen sounds the fanfare over silence; after `FanfareHold` (3.0 s, the placeholder fanfare's length) the map's track comes back, resumed with its fade-in (decisions 417, 418).
    - The defeat screen sounds the defeat sound over silence, which lasts until the title or a load.
    - The escaped notice sounds the escape sound, and the map's track comes back at once.
  - **New types:**
    - `Battle/BattleMusicCue` (enum): what the battle screen calls for: `Theme`, `Silence`, `Field`.
    - `Battle/BattleMusic`: the music the battle screen calls for as its outcome shows. `ShowVictory()`, `ShowDefeat()`, `ShowEscape()`, `Advance(TimeSpan delta)`, `Cue`; `FanfareHold` (3.0 s). Pure.
  - **New files:** `content/audio/effects/PlaceholderBattleStart.wav`, `PlaceholderVictory.wav` (3 s), `PlaceholderDefeat.wav`, `PlaceholderEscape.wav`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Ui/UiSoundFiles.Battle`: `Start`, `Victory`, `Defeat`, `Escape`.
    - `Scenes/SceneStack.Top` (`IScene?`): the scene on top, null when empty.
    - `Battle/BattleScene` (about 264 after B1, about +12): a `BattleMusic`, advanced each update; `Finish` tells it the outcome and asks the outcome's sound; `MusicCue` (public read).
    - `Flow/MusicSelection.TrackFor(GameSession? session, BattleMusicCue? battleCue)`: `Silence` → null, `Field` → the map's track, before the pending encounter's rule; `GameFlow.FollowMusic` passes `(_scenes.Top as BattleScene)?.MusicCue`.
    - `Flow/GameFlow.Start` (about +2): `BattleRequested` plays `UiSoundFiles.Battle.Start` into `Audio.Effects` as the flash starts.
  - **Tests:**
    - `BattleMusicTests` (new): `Cue_WhileFighting_IsTheTheme`, `Advance_VictoryBeforeTheHold_IsSilence`, `Advance_VictoryPastTheHold_IsTheField`, `Cue_Defeat_IsSilence`, `Cue_Escape_IsTheField`
    - `MusicSelectionTests`: `TrackFor_BattleCueSilence_IsSilence`, `TrackFor_BattleCueField_IsTheMapsTrack`
    - `GameFlowSoundTests`: `Update_EncounterStarts_PlaysTheBattleStartOnce`, `Update_DuringTheFlash_PlaysNoEffect`
    - `BattleSceneSoundTests`: `Update_VictoryScreenOpens_AsksTheFanfare` (a battle won through `BattleScenarios`' certain-chance content, so no seed matters)
  - **Verify by hand:** a forest battle: the start sound with the flash and the battle theme; the victory screen's fanfare over silence, then the forest track fades in where it left off, during the rewards. Run from a battle: the escape sound, the forest track at once. Lose one: the defeat sound, then silence until the title's theme.
  - **Done, with deviations:** as planned; the flow's write stays one line in `Update`, as P1 made it. Scene tests cover each outcome's sound and cue, and a flow test the cue reaching the music. Not heard (unattended loop): the hand check is yours. Tests 3649 → 3665.
