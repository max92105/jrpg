# B5. Sounds on the battle's commands

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After B1 (`BattleScene.Sounds`, the playback's queue). Decision 421. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 3.

- **B5. Sounds on the battle's commands, skill and item lists and target cursor** (Game, about 140 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** while a party member chooses, Up and Down in the commands, the skill and item lists and the target cursor tick the move sound when the selection changes; Confirm that opens a list or the target cursor, or submits a command, sounds the confirm sound; Cancel back a step sounds the cancel sound. A greyed command or skill (not enough of a resource, Run against a boss once the enemy plan greys it) is silent (no buzzer, decision 421).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Battle/CommandMenu` (220, about +12): an `AskedSounds` cleared as its `Update` starts; its own cursor, `BattleSkillChoice`, `BattleItemChoice` and `BattleTargetCursor` compared around their moves; `Sounds` (internal read).
    - `Battle/BattleScene`: `AskAll(_commands.Sounds)` after `_commands.Update` (+1).
  - **Tests** (`CommandMenuSoundTests`, new: `CommandMenuTests` is 393):
    - `Update_Down_AsksTheMoveSound`
    - `Update_ConfirmSkills_AsksTheConfirmSound`
    - `Update_CancelInTheSkillList_AsksTheCancelSound`
    - `Update_ConfirmOnATarget_AsksTheConfirmSound`
    - `Update_ConfirmOnAGreyedSkill_AsksNothing`
  - **Verify by hand:** a battle: moving through commands, skills, items and targets ticks; choosing sounds; backing out sounds lower; submitting an attack confirms, then the skill's and the impact's sounds follow without doubling.
  - **Done, with deviations:** as planned, with more tests (each list's moves, Cancel at each level, a command submitted, the scene passing them up). Not heard (unattended loop): the hand check is yours. Tests 3665 → 3683.
