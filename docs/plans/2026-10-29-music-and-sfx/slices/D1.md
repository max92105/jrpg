# D1. Dialogue typing ticks

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S1. Pending Q8 (how often; silent under a voice-over?). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (typing tick), "Key decisions" 3.

- **D1. Dialogue typing ticks** (Game, about 140 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** while a dialogue page types out, `textTyping` sounds every second character typed (placeholder), never for a space; a page shown whole (Instant speed, a found item, Confirm completing the page) makes no tick. Turning a page with Confirm sounds nothing (Q7). Whether ticks stop under a voice-over is Q8; the plan keeps them.
  - **New types:**
    - `Dialogue/TypingTicks` (internal static): whether a page's typing ticks this update: every `CharactersPerTick` characters typed, never on a space. `public static bool IsDue(string page, int visibleBefore, int visibleNow)` is true when a tick boundary was crossed on a non-space character; `CharactersPerTick = 2` (placeholder). A jump over more than one boundary in an update (a long frame) ticks once.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Exploration/EventPresenter` (258, about +8): an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts; `ShowPages` reads `_reading.VisibleLength` around the playback's update and asks `textTyping` when `TypingTicks.IsDue` says so; a Confirm that reveals the rest asks nothing. `Sounds` (public read). The summary mentions the ticks.
    - `Exploration/ExplorationScene` (177, +2): `Sounds => _presenter.Sounds`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `TypingTicksTests` (new): `IsDue_SecondCharacterTyped_IsTrue`, `IsDue_FirstCharacterTyped_IsFalse`, `IsDue_BoundaryOnASpace_IsFalse`, `IsDue_JumpOverTwoBoundaries_IsTrue` (one tick, as it returns once), `IsDue_NothingNewTyped_IsFalse`
    - `EventPresenterSoundTests` (new: `EventPresenterTests` is 305): `Update_DialogueTypingAtNormalSpeed_AsksTextTyping`, `Update_DialogueAtInstantSpeed_AsksNothing`
  - **Verify by hand:** talk to the villager: the lines tick as they type, faster at Fast speed, none at Instant; Confirm mid-line shows the rest with no burst of ticks.
