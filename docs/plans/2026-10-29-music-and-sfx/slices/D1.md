# D1. Dialogue typing ticks

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S3 (the map scene's `Sounds`). Decision 422. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (typing), "Key decisions" 3, "Risks" (typing under a voice).

- **D1. Dialogue typing ticks, spaced and muted under a voice** (Game, about 170 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** while a dialogue page types out, the typing sound plays every second character typed, never for a space, and at least 60 ms after the last tick (decision 422), so a fast speed doesn't buzz. A page shown whole (Instant speed, a found item, Confirm completing the page) makes no tick, and turning a page is silent. While the event's voice-over is on (the voice channel holds a file), typing is muted.
  - **New types:**
    - `Dialogue/TypingTicks` (internal static): whether a page's typing ticks this update. `public static bool IsDue(string page, int visibleBefore, int visibleNow, TimeSpan sinceLastTick)`: true when a multiple of `CharactersPerTick` (2) was crossed on a non-space character and `sinceLastTick >= MinimumGap` (60 ms); a jump over more than one multiple in one update ticks once. Pure.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Ui/UiSoundFiles.Effects.TextTyping` + `content/audio/effects/PlaceholderTextTyping.wav` (a click, 5 ms fades).
    - `Exploration/EventPresenter` (258, about +10): an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts and a `_sinceTyped` time; `ShowPages` reads `_reading.VisibleLength` around the playback's update and asks the typing sound when `TypingTicks.IsDue` says so and `_voice.VoiceFile` is null; a Confirm that reveals the rest asks nothing. `Sounds` (public read). The summary mentions the ticks.
    - `Exploration/ExplorationScene`: `AskAll(_presenter.Sounds)` after the presenter's update (S3 gave the scene its `AskedSounds`).
  - **Tests:**
    - `TypingTicksTests` (new): `IsDue_SecondCharacterTyped_IsTrue`, `IsDue_FirstCharacterTyped_IsFalse`, `IsDue_MultipleOnASpace_IsFalse`, `IsDue_TooSoonAfterTheLastTick_IsFalse`, `IsDue_GapReachedExactly_IsTrue` (both sides of the gap), `IsDue_JumpOverTwoMultiples_IsTrue` (one tick)
    - `EventPresenterSoundTests` (new: `EventPresenterTests` is 305): `Update_DialogueTypingAtNormalSpeed_AsksTheTypingSound`, `Update_DialogueAtInstantSpeed_AsksNothing`, `Update_DialogueWhileAVoicePlays_AsksNothing`
  - **Verify by hand:** talk to the villager: the line ticks as it types, no faster than the gap at Fast speed, none at Instant; Confirm mid-line shows the rest with no burst of ticks; an intro with a voice-over types silently.
