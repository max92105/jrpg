# F1. Cues at the Forge

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S1. Pending Q7. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 3.

- **F1. Cues at the Forge** (Game, about 110 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** at the Forge, Up and Down in the pieces and in a piece's actions tick `menuMove` when the selection changes; Confirm opening the actions, an upgrade, a reroll and the salvage prompt's Yes sound `menuConfirm`; Cancel closing the actions, the prompt or the Forge sounds `menuCancel`. A refused (greyed) action sounds nothing unless Q7 adds a refused cue.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Crafting/ForgeScene` (233, about +12): an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts; `ForgeList.Cursor` and `ForgeActionChoice`'s selection compared around their moves; the action carried out, the prompt answered and the closing ask theirs; `Sounds` overrides the default. If it would pass 260, the selection comparisons move into the list and choice (each exposing its own `Sounds`).
  - **Tests** (`ForgeSceneSoundTests`, new):
    - `Update_DownInThePieces_AsksMenuMove`
    - `Update_ConfirmOnAPiece_AsksMenuConfirm`
    - `Update_CancelInTheActions_AsksMenuCancel`
    - `Update_ConfirmOnARefusedAction_AsksNothing`
  - **Verify by hand:** at the forge master: moving, opening, upgrading and salvaging each sound once; a greyed action is silent; leaving sounds once.
