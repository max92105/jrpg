# P1. The music the flow calls for, headless

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After C2 and the ADRs on how the game asks for music and effects and on music playback. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 1 and 2, "Replacement mapping".

- **P1. The music the flow calls for, headless: title, map, battle theme from the encounter's flash until the battle screen closes** (Game, about 210 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** after every update the flow writes into a music channel the track its state calls for (README, "Decisions"): the title theme before a game, the current map's track while walking (silence on a map without one), the encounter's theme or the battle theme from the encounter's flash until the battle screen is popped. Nothing is heard yet (P2); tests read the channel.
  - **New types:**
    - `Audio/MusicChannel`: the track the game wants heard now, by file in the music folder, or null for silence. `Track`, `Play(string file)`, `Stop()` (as `VoiceChannel`).
    - `Audio/AudioChannels`: the channels the flow and scenes write during an update and the host plays after it. `Voice` (the existing `VoiceChannel`), `Music`; `Effects` comes in S1.
    - `Flow/MusicSelection` (static, pure): the track the flow's state calls for. `public static string? TrackFor(GameSession? session, EncounterDefinition? fought, AudioDefinition audio)`.
    - `Flow/AudioCues(AudioChannels audio, AudioDefinition definition)`: keeps the audio channels in step with the flow after each update. `Follow(GameSession? session, SceneStack scenes)` finds the encounter fought (the battle screen's, else the pending one) and writes `MusicSelection`'s answer; S1 adds the effects.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Scenes/SceneStack.TopmostOf<T>() where T : class, IScene`: the topmost scene of that type, or null (no allocation: a backwards loop).
    - `Battle/BattleScene.Encounter` (public): the encounter it fights, kept from its constructor (which already reads `PendingEncounter` for the background); the background then reads `Encounter.BackgroundFile` (net +2 lines; 254 lines).
    - `Flow/SceneFactory.Voice` → `Audio` (`AudioChannels`); `CreateExploration` passes `Audio.Voice` to `EventPresenter` (its constructor unchanged).
    - `Flow/GameFlow` (254, about +6): `Voice` → `Audio`; builds `AudioCues` from `factory.Audio` and `catalog.Audio`; `Update` calls `_audioCues.Follow(Session, _scenes)` on every path, the flash's included (a private method, so each `return` doesn't repeat it).
    - `Program.FirstScene` returns `(IScene Scene, AudioChannels Audio)`; the error screen's is `new AudioChannels()`. `JrpgGame` takes `AudioChannels audio` and follows `audio.Voice`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `MusicSelectionTests` (new): `TrackFor_NoSession_IsTheTitleTheme`, `TrackFor_OnAMapWithATrack_IsTheMapsTrack`, `TrackFor_OnAMapWithoutATrack_IsSilence`, `TrackFor_EncounterFought_IsTheBattleTheme`, `TrackFor_EncounterWithItsOwnTheme_IsThatTheme`
    - `GameFlowMusicTests` (new file: `GameFlowTests` is 321): `Update_OnTheTitle_AsksForTheTitleTheme`, `Update_BattleOverTheMap_KeepsTheBattleThemeOnTheVictoryScreen` (a won battle with `FinishBattle` done: the channel still holds the battle theme until `BattleFinished`; seed-independent, as the flow lessons require), `Update_BackFromBattle_AsksForTheMapsTrack`
    - `SceneStackTests` (130): `TopmostOf_BattleUnderAMenu_FindsTheBattle`
    - `EventPresenterTests` and the other 15 `new VoiceChannel()` sites: unchanged (the presenter still takes the voice channel).
  - **Verify by hand:** none (nothing plays); the flow tests play it headless.
