# P2. The music plays

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After P1. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Which MonoGame types carry music", "Key decisions" 1 and 6.

- **P2. The music plays on the device: looped, at the music volume, changing tracks with a cut** (Game, about 110 lines; device code, no view type)
  - **Goal:** the host plays the music channel's track on a loop at the Music volume (a change in Options is heard at once); a new track stops the old one and starts from its beginning; null stops it. Without a device, or with a file that won't load, it is silent (ADR 0018). The crossfade and the resume come in P3.
  - **New types:**
    - `Audio/MusicPlayer(SoundLibrary sounds)` (`IDisposable`): plays on the device the track the `MusicChannel` asks for. `Follow(MusicChannel music, float gain)`: a different file stops the instance playing and starts the new one with `IsLooped = true`, then sets `Volume = gain` every call; the same file plays on.
  - **Changes:**
    - `JrpgGame.StartAudio`: builds `_musicPlayer` beside `_voicePlayer`; `Update`: `_musicPlayer?.Follow(_audio.Music, VolumeLevel.GainOf(_settings.MusicVolume))`; `Dispose` disposes it. The comment over `_sounds` names the music too.
    - `Audio/SoundLibrary`'s summary: it now opens music files as well.
  - **Tests:** none can fail headless: `MusicPlayer` only touches the device (the plan's rule: the rule it follows is `MusicSelection`, tested in P1). The slice's checks are by hand.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - The title theme loops on the title (listen past 4 s: no gap or click at the loop) and through the leader choice.
    - A new game: the town track at black; through the door to `interior_1`: it plays on, not restarted; to the forest: the forest track.
    - A random battle: the battle theme from the flash, through the victory screen; back on the map, the forest track (from its start, until P3).
    - Options, Music to 0: silent; back to 7: heard at once.
    - Start with the sound device disabled in Windows: the game runs silent, one line in the Output window.
