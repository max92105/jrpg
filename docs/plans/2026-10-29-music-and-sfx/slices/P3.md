# P3. Crossfade and resume

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After P2. Pending Q2 (how changes sound) and Q3 (resume or restart). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (crossfade), "Key decisions" 5.

- **P3. Tracks crossfade; the map's track resumes after a battle** (Game, about 200 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** when the wanted track changes, the old one fades out while the new one fades in over `FadeLength` (1.0 s placeholder); silence fades out the same way. The track faded out is kept paused, one only; wanting it again resumes it where it paused, fading in (so the map's track resumes after a battle). If Q3 answers "restart", the paused track is dropped instead and its tests change; if Q2 asks for cuts on some changes, the channel carries the fade length (design.md, "Extension points").
  - **New types:**
    - `Audio/MusicMix`: which tracks sound and at what gain while one fades into the next, and which one waits paused to resume. `Want(string? file)`, `Advance(TimeSpan delta)`; `Rising` and `Falling` (`MixedTrack?`: file and gain 0..1), `Paused` (`string?`). Pure, no MonoGame.
    - `Audio/MixedTrack` (readonly record struct `(string File, float Gain)`): one track in the mix at its gain.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Audio/MusicPlayer` (P2's cut): keeps one instance per file in the mix (at most three: rising, falling, paused); each `Follow` calls `_mix.Want(music.Track)` and `_mix.Advance(delta)`, then sets each instance's `Volume = gain × mixed gain`, pauses the one the mix pauses, resumes the one it resumes and stops and disposes the one it drops. `Follow` gains the delta: `Follow(MusicChannel music, float gain, TimeSpan delta)`.
    - `JrpgGame.Update`: passes `gameTime.ElapsedGameTime`.
  - **Tests** (`MusicMixTests`, new):
    - `Advance_HalfTheFade_BothTracksAtHalfGain`
    - `Advance_PastTheFade_OnlyTheNewTrackAtFullGain`
    - `Want_SameTrack_ChangesNothing`
    - `Want_Silence_FadesTheTrackOut`
    - `Want_TrackFadedOutBefore_ResumesIt` (`Paused` becomes the rising one)
    - `Want_ThirdTrack_DropsThePausedOne` (only one waits)
    - `Want_BackDuringTheFade_RisesFromItsCurrentGain` (no jump when the player turns back mid-fade)
  - **Verify by hand:** walk from the start map to the forest: the tracks overlap for about a second. A forest battle: the forest track fades under the flash, the battle theme rises; back on the map, the forest track resumes where it was (listen for the note it stopped on). The title after a game over: the battle theme fades into the title theme.
