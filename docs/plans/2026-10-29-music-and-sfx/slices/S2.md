# S2. Sounds on the leader choice, the defeat menu and the Forge

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S1. Decision 421. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 3.

- **S2. Sounds on the leader choice, the defeat menu and the Forge** (Game, about 200 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** three small screens get move, confirm and cancel sounds (decision 421).
    - **Leader choice:** the move sound when the highlighted character changes, the confirm sound on the choice, the cancel sound where Cancel goes back.
    - **Defeat menu** (Load, Title): move and confirm; its load list through `SaveFilesPanel`'s sounds (S1). `BattleScene.Sounds` starts here with the defeat screen's; B1 and B4 add to it.
    - **Forge:** the move sound in the pieces and in a piece's actions when the selection changes; the confirm sound on opening the actions and on an upgrade or a reroll; the cancel sound on closing the actions or the Forge. The salvage prompt is silent, its Yes included (no prompt sounds, decision 421), and a refused (greyed) action is silent (no buzzer).
  - **Changes:**
    - `NewGame/LeaderChoiceScene` (199): an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts; the selection compared around `LeaderChoiceMenu`'s update; `Sounds` overrides the default.
    - `Battle/DefeatMenu` (41): its own `AskedSounds` (move, confirm), exposed as `Sounds`; `Battle/DefeatScreen` adds its menu's and its `SaveFilesPanel`'s.
    - `Battle/BattleScene` (254, about +6): an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts, `AskAll` of the defeat screen's while it shows, `Sounds` overriding the default.
    - `Crafting/ForgeScene` (233, about +12): an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts; `ForgeList.Cursor` and `ForgeActionChoice`'s selection compared around their moves; the action carried out and the closing ask theirs; `Sounds` overrides the default. If it would pass about 260, the comparisons move into the list and the choice, each exposing its own `Sounds`.
