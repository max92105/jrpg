# S2. Cues on the leader choice and the defeat menu

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S1. Pending Q7 (which cues). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 3.

- **S2. Cues on the leader choice and the defeat menu** (Game, about 120 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** the leader choice ticks `menuMove` when the highlighted character changes and sounds `menuConfirm` on the choice (and `menuCancel` where Cancel goes back, if it does); the defeat screen's menu (Load, Title) does the same. `BattleScene.Sounds` starts here, with the defeat menu's ids only; B1 adds the impacts and victory to it.
  - **Changes:**
    - `NewGame/LeaderChoiceScene` (199): an `AskedSounds`, cleared as `Update` starts; selection compared around `LeaderChoiceMenu`'s update; `Sounds` overrides the default.
    - `Battle/DefeatMenu` (41): `Sounds` from an `AskedSounds` (move and confirm); `Battle/DefeatScreen` exposes it (with its `SaveFilesPanel`'s, S1); `Battle/BattleScene` (about 256 after P1, +6) gets an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts, adds the defeat screen's ids while it shows (`AskAll`), and overrides `Sounds`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `LeaderChoiceSceneSoundTests` (new; `LeaderChoiceSceneTests` is 177): `Update_Down_AsksMenuMove`, `Update_Confirm_AsksMenuConfirm`
    - `DefeatMenuTests`: `Update_Down_AsksMenuMove`, `Update_Confirm_AsksMenuConfirm`
    - `BattleSceneTests` (208): `Update_DefeatMenuMoved_SceneAsksMenuMove` (a battle lost at the start, decision 97, so no seed matters)
  - **Verify by hand:** a new game: moving between leaders ticks, choosing sounds. Lose a battle (let a level 1 party fall in the forest): the defeat menu ticks and confirms.
