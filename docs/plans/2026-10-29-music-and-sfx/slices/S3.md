# S3. Sounds on the game menu's opening and tabs, Options and System

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S1. Decision 421. ADR 0035 (the flow's timing). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 3.

- **S3. Sounds on the game menu's opening and tabs, the Options page and the System page** (Game, about 210 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** opening the menu from the map sounds the confirm sound (asked by the map scene in the update that returns `MenuRequested`, which the flow copies before opening the menu); in the menu, Up and Down on the tabs tick, Confirm into a page confirms, Cancel back to the tabs or out of the menu cancels. Inside a page the page's own sounds are added: Options ticks on a row change and on a value change (Left, Right), and a value at its end that doesn't change is silent; System ticks, confirms Save and Load, and its save and load lists use `SaveFilesPanel`'s sounds (S1). A written save asks the confirm sound; prompts stay silent (decision 421).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Exploration/ExplorationScene` (177, about +6): an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts; the menu's opening asks the confirm sound; `Sounds` overrides the default (D1 adds the event presenter's).
    - `Menus/IMenuPage.Sounds` (default interface member, `=> []`): the effect files the page's last update asks for.
    - `Menus/GameMenuScene` (223, about +12): an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts; tab moves, entering, leaving and closing ask theirs; after a page's update, `AskAll(page.Sounds)`; `Sounds` overrides the default.
    - `Menus/OptionsPage` (about 200 after V1, +8): its `AskedSounds`; a row move and a value change ask the move sound.
    - `Menus/SystemPage` (200, +8) and its `SavePage` and `LoadPage`: their lists' sounds through `SaveFilesPanel.Sounds`; a save written asks the confirm sound.
  - **Tests:**
    - `GameFlowSoundTests`: `Update_SceneAsksAndOpensTheMenu_PlaysItsSound` (Menu on the map: the confirm sound is in the channel in the update the menu opens, and not in the next)
    - `GameMenuSceneSoundTests` (new; `GameMenuSceneTests` is 224): `Update_DownOnTheTabs_AsksTheMoveSound`, `Update_ConfirmOnATab_AsksTheConfirmSound`, `Update_CancelInAPage_AsksTheCancelSound`, `Update_PageAsksASound_SceneAsksIt`
    - `OptionsPageTests`: `Update_RightOnMusic_AsksTheMoveSound`, `Update_RightOnVoicesAtMax_AsksNothing`
    - `SystemPageTests`: `Update_ConfirmOnSave_AsksTheConfirmSound`
  - **Verify by hand:** on the map, Menu sounds as the menu slides in; tabs tick, entering a page sounds, Cancel back sounds lower; Options: rows and values tick, an end stop is silent; set Effects to 0: the menu falls silent at once; saving sounds, its prompt doesn't.
