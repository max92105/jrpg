# S4. Sounds on the Inventory, Equipment, Party and Status pages

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S3 (`IMenuPage.Sounds`, the menu adding its page's). Decision 421. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 3.

- **S4. Sounds on the Inventory, Equipment, Party and Status pages** (Game, about 230 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** each of the four pages ticks the move sound when a selection changes (list rows, the category rail, the member column, the slot list, the item target picker, the Party cards and their action choice), sounds the confirm sound on what Confirm carries out (use an item, equip, take off, swap, open a slot's choice), and the cancel sound where Cancel closes something inside the page (a choice, the target picker). A refused Confirm is silent (a greyed row, a refusal that opens a notice), and notices and prompts are silent, their OK included (decision 421).
  - **Changes** (each page: an `AskedSounds` cleared as its `Update` starts, `Sounds` overriding the default; selections compared before and after the input that moves them):
    - `Menus/InventoryPage` (242, about +10): the list, the rail, `ItemTargetPicker`, using an item.
    - `Menus/EquipmentPage` (214, about +8): the slots, `EquipmentChoice`, equipping and taking off.
    - `Menus/PartyPage` (275, about +8): the cards, the action choice, a swap. At about 283 it stays under 300; if it would cross, its input methods move first into a `PartyPageInput` in this slice.
    - `Menus/StatusPage` (111, about +4): the member moves.
  - **Tests** (one new `...SoundTests` file per page, beside the existing ones):
    - `InventoryPageSoundTests: Update_DownInTheList_AsksTheMoveSound`, `Update_ConfirmUsesAPotion_AsksTheConfirmSound`, `Update_CancelInTheTargetPicker_AsksTheCancelSound`, `Update_ConfirmOnAGreyedRow_AsksNothing`
    - `EquipmentPageSoundTests: Update_ConfirmOnASlot_AsksTheConfirmSound`, `Update_DownInTheChoice_AsksTheMoveSound`
    - `PartyPageSoundTests: Update_RightOnTheCards_AsksTheMoveSound`, `Update_ConfirmASwap_AsksTheConfirmSound`, `Update_RefusalOpensANotice_AsksNothing`, `Update_OkOnTheNotice_AsksNothing`
    - `StatusPageSoundTests: Update_DownToTheNextMember_AsksTheMoveSound`, `Update_DownWithOneMember_AsksNothing` (a one-member party built for the test)
  - **Verify by hand:** go through each page: every move ticks, every action sounds, a greyed item and a Party refusal are silent, a single-member party's Status page is silent on Down; Cancel inside a page closes only the page's choice and sounds once.
