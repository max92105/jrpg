# S4. Cues on the Inventory, Equipment, Party and Status pages

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After S3 (`IMenuPage.Sounds`, the menu adding its page's ids). Pending Q7. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 3.

- **S4. Cues on the Inventory, Equipment, Party and Status pages** (Game, about 210 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** each of the four pages ticks `menuMove` when a selection changes (list rows, category rail, member column, slot list, the item target picker, the Party cards and their action choice), sounds `menuConfirm` on what Confirm carries out (use an item, equip, swap, open a slot's choice), and `menuCancel` where Cancel closes something inside the page (a choice, the target picker, a prompt). A refused Confirm (a greyed row, a notice) sounds nothing unless Q7 adds a refused cue.
  - **Changes** (each page: an `AskedSounds` cleared as its `Update` starts, `Sounds` overriding the default; selections compared before and after the input that moves them):
    - `Menus/InventoryPage` (242, about +10): the list, the rail, `ItemTargetPicker`, using an item.
    - `Menus/EquipmentPage` (214, about +8): the slots, `EquipmentChoice`, equipping and taking off.
    - `Menus/PartyPage` (275, about +8): the cards, the action choice, a swap; the `NoticeDialog`'s OK asks `menuConfirm`. At about 283 it stays under 300; if it would cross, the page's input methods move first into a `PartyPageInput` in the same slice (noted, not planned: the estimate is +8).
    - `Menus/StatusPage` (111, about +4): the member moves.
  - **Tests** (one new `...SoundTests` file per page, beside the existing ones):
    - `InventoryPageSoundTests: Update_DownInTheList_AsksMenuMove`, `Update_ConfirmUsesAPotion_AsksMenuConfirm`, `Update_CancelInTheTargetPicker_AsksMenuCancel`
    - `EquipmentPageSoundTests: Update_ConfirmOnASlot_AsksMenuConfirm`, `Update_DownInTheChoice_AsksMenuMove`
    - `PartyPageSoundTests: Update_RightOnTheCards_AsksMenuMove`, `Update_ConfirmASwap_AsksMenuConfirm`
    - `StatusPageSoundTests: Update_DownToTheNextMember_AsksMenuMove`, `Update_DownWithOneMember_AsksNothing` (the member doesn't change)
  - **Verify by hand:** go through each page: every move ticks, every action sounds, a single-member party's Status page is silent on Down; no doubled sounds when a page and the menu both see a press (Cancel inside a page closes only the page's choice, and sounds once).
