# V2. The volume rows as diamond pips

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After V1 and S3 (every volume acts on something, and Options sounds). Decision 424. Look at `docs/ui-reference/` (its README) before drawing. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Replacement mapping".

- **V2. The volume rows as diamond pips** (Game, raw 70, about 110 lines at 1.6x; reuses `Ui/PipRow`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** each volume row shows ten diamond pips in the value column, as many lit as its level (none at 0), with the level's number after them, in the row's text color (decision 424); the selected row keeps the diamonds either side of its value (decision 252's look). Full screen and Text speed stay text.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/OptionsPage` (about 210 after S3): volume rows draw a `PipRow` with a `PipRowLook` sized to the menu's text (pip side and gap as named constants, placeholders); the right diamond sits after the number. The `_levels` texts stay for the number. The slice writes the pixel arithmetic: ten pips plus their gaps plus the widest number fit between the value column (320 px from the label) and the column's right edge at 1920 x 1080.
    - If `PipRow` lacks a color per row (it lights pips in a resource's color), it takes the row's color: one parameter, its callers unchanged.
  - **Tests:** none can fail for a draw-only change; `OptionsPageTests` pass unchanged. Before and after captures with F12 (Debug) go in the slice's note.
  - **Verify by hand:** Options: the three volumes show their pips, the selected row's diamonds frame pips and number, Left and Right light and dim one pip at a time, 0 shows all dim.
