# E1. The event dialogs show and add the quest steps

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After QV (and QD: the catalog's quests). ADR 0012. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "What exists" (Editor). The forge plan's E2 and the gold plan's E2 are the model.

- **E1. The event dialogs show and add the three quest steps** (Editor, about 210 lines; tests count about as much as code)
  - **Goal:** the object and cell event dialogs open events holding quest steps, list them with a label, and add, move and remove them with undo; Start and Complete pick a quest from the loaded quests, Advance picks one of their stages ("placeholder_errand: return_to_giver").
  - **New types:**
    - `Editor/Events/QuestStageChoice` (internal sealed record `(string QuestId, string StageId)`): one stage of one quest, as the advance step and E2's stage box offer it, shown as `quest: stage`; equal by value, so an opened step selects its entry.
  - **Changes** (labels are C# strings, as the other steps'):
    - `EventStepKind.StartQuest`, `AdvanceQuest`, `CompleteQuest`.
    - `EventStepViewModel` (about 230 after the gold plan): `QuestId` (start, complete) and `QuestStage` (`QuestStageChoice?`, advance), `IsQuestPick`/`IsQuestStagePick` for the editor's visibility; `Label` "Start quest: x", "Advance quest: x to y", "Complete quest: x"; `Problem` "Choose the quest." and "Choose the quest's stage."; `From`, `ToStep`. About 30 lines: it must stay under 300.
    - `EventViewModel.AddStartQuest`, `AddAdvanceQuest`, `AddCompleteQuest` (`[RelayCommand]`), their buttons in the `WrapPanel`s of `ObjectEventsWindow.xaml` and `CellEventWindow.xaml`.
    - `EventStepEditor.xaml`: a quest combo (`Choices.QuestIds`) and a stage combo (`Choices.QuestStages`), shown by kind.
    - `StepChoices.QuestIds` and `QuestStages` (init, empty by default, as the gold plan's `ShopIds`); `EventEditingViewModel.Choices` fills them from `catalog.Current.Quests` (quests in id order, each quest's stages in its order).
  - **Tests** (new files: `EventStepViewModelTests` is at 260, `EventListViewModelTests` at 258):
    - `EventStepViewModelQuestTests: FromStep_StartQuestStep_ShowsStartQuestWithItsId`
    - `EventStepViewModelQuestTests: FromStep_AdvanceQuestStep_SelectsItsStageChoice`
    - `EventStepViewModelQuestTests: ToStep_AdvanceQuest_ReturnsItsQuestAndStage`
    - `EventStepViewModelQuestTests: ToStep_CompleteQuest_ReturnsCompleteQuestStep`
    - `EventStepViewModelQuestTests: Problem_StartQuestWithoutQuest_AsksForTheQuest`
    - `EventStepViewModelQuestTests: Problem_AdvanceQuestWithoutStage_AsksForTheStage`
    - `EventStepViewModelTests: StepsOfEveryKind` gains the three steps (the existing round trip)
    - `EventListViewModelQuestTests: AddAdvanceQuestCommand_NoStepChosen_AddsItLast`
    - `EventEditingViewModelTests: StartEditingAt_CatalogWithQuest_OffersItsIdAndStages`
  - **Verify by hand:** open an object's events, add Start quest, Advance quest and Complete quest with the placeholder errand: each label names what was picked; OK, save, reopen: the steps are there; Ctrl+Z removes the last. Pick no quest: OK says which step needs one. The editor loads content once: a quest written by hand in `quests.json` while it runs is offered after a restart (QE's window adds them at once).

- **Done, with deviations:** as planned; `ToStep` crossed the complexity limit with three more arms, so the quest steps are built by one `QuestStep()` helper behind one arm. The stage combo shows `QuestStageChoice.Label` ("quest: stage") through `DisplayMemberPath`. `EventListViewModelQuestTests` also round-trips the start and complete buttons. `EventStepViewModel` at 262 lines. Tests 3224 → 3236.
