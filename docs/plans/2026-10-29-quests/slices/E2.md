# E2. The event's quest condition in the dialogs' model

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After E1. Decisions 386, 389. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (the editor's condition is three boxes). The rules half of the condition's editor support; E3 puts the boxes in the dialogs.

- **E2. The event's quest condition in the dialogs' model** (Editor, about 180 lines)
  - **Goal:** an event opened in a dialog keeps its quest condition through OK (closing QV's known gap), the condition can be set as a quest, a kind and a stage, with its problem, and the event's one-line summary says it ("Press \"Talk\" when placeholder_errand reached return_to_giver: 2 step(s)").
  - **New types:**
    - `Editor/Events/QuestGateViewModel` (internal sealed, observable): an event's quest condition as edited: `QuestId` (empty for always), `Kind` (`QuestConditionKind`), `StageId`; `Stages` (the chosen quest's stages in order, from `StepChoices.QuestStages`); `NeedsStage`; changing the quest clears the stage. `Problem` ("Choose the stage the quest must be at." / "... must have reached."); `QuestCondition? ToCondition()` (throws on a problem, as `ToStep` does); `Label` for the summary (" when x is not started", " when x is active", " when x is at y", " when x reached y", " when x is completed").
  - **Changes:**
    - `EventViewModel(MapEvent mapEvent, StepChoices choices)`: holds a `QuestGateViewModel QuestGate` built from `mapEvent.OnlyWhenQuest`; `Label` appends its label; `Problem` reports the gate's before the steps'; `ToEvent` sets `OnlyWhenQuest`. Its five construction sites pass the choices (`EventListViewModel` three, `CellEventViewModel` one, one test).
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuestGateViewModelTests: New_FromCondition_ShowsQuestKindAndStage`
    - `QuestGateViewModelTests: Stages_QuestChosen_ListsItsStagesInOrder`
    - `QuestGateViewModelTests: QuestId_Changed_ClearsTheStage`
    - `QuestGateViewModelTests: Problem_ReachedStageWithoutStage_AsksForIt`
    - `QuestGateViewModelTests: ToCondition_NoQuest_ReturnsNull`
    - `QuestGateViewModelTests: ToCondition_AtStage_ReturnsKindAndStage`
    - `QuestGateViewModelTests: ToCondition_Completed_LeavesTheStageOut` (a stage left from another kind isn't written)
    - `EventQuestConditionTests: ToEvent_OpenedWithCondition_KeepsIt`
    - `EventQuestConditionTests: Label_WithCondition_SaysWhen`
  - **Verify by hand:** with N1's content not yet there, write an `onlyWhenQuest` by hand into a copy of a map, open its event in the editor, press OK, save: the condition is still in the file and the event's summary shows it. (The boxes come in E3.)
