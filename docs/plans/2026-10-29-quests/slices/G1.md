# G1. A Quests tab lists the active quests

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After J1. Q9 (the tab's place). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "What exists" (Game), "Tunables" (tab position, icon, page texts).

- **G1. A Quests tab lists the active quests** (Game + text, raw 125, about 200 lines at 1.6x)
  - **Goal:** the game menu has a Quests tab with its icon; its page lists the active quests' titles in the journal's order, or "No quests yet." Plain draw: one title per line in the page column; no cursor yet (G2).
  - **New types:**
    - `Menus/QuestsPage` (`IMenuPage`): the menu's Quests page; `Open` reads `session.Quests.Active` and formats the titles once; Cancel leaves (`LeavesOnCancel` true). Constructor `(ScreenText text, UiTextures ui, GameSession session)`, as `EquipmentPage`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Ui/TabGlyph.Quests` and its 12 x 12 mask in `Ui/TabGlyphShape` (a scroll; placeholder, decision 254).
    - `Flow/SceneFactory.CreateGameMenu`: the page after Status (Q9's pick).
    - `Ui/MessageIds`: `MenuQuests`, `MenuHintQuests`, `QuestsNone`; `content/text/ui.json`: "Quests", a footer hint worded as the other pages' (Cancel only for now; G2 adds Up/Down), "No quests yet.".
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuestsPageTests: Open_TwoActiveQuests_TitlesInJournalOrder` (a session restored from a save holding two quests; a small `internal` read accessor `ShownTitles` as the test seam)
    - `QuestsPageTests: Open_NoQuest_ShowsTheNoneLine`
    - `QuestsPageTests: Open_AfterAQuestStarted_ListsItTheNextTime`
    - `TabGlyphShapeTests` and `MessageIdsTests` cover the glyph and ids through their existing loops (check they iterate every value; add the case if not).
  - **Watch:** tests that move through the menu's tabs by count from `SceneFactory` (none found at planning; grep `CreateGameMenu` in the Game tests).
