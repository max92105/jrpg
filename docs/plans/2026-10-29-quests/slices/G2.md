# G2. The selected quest's detail

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After N1, so it is checked by hand on the placeholder quest. Q5, Q8. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Structure and folder dependencies" (Game).

- **G2. Choosing a quest shows its detail** (Game + text, about 190 lines)
  - **Goal:** Up and Down choose among the listed quests with the shared selection (decision 252, gliding through `SelectionMotion`); beside the list, plain lines show the chosen quest's title, its kind (Main or Side), its description and its current stage's text.
  - **New types:**
    - `Menus/QuestList` (internal sealed): the listed quests, their cursor and gliding highlight; `ListCursor?` null while empty, as `Crafting/ForgeList`; a fixed number of visible rows (`QuestsPage.VisibleRows` until G4's view owns it).
    - `Menus/QuestDetail` (internal sealed record): the chosen quest as the page shows it: title, kind name, description, stage text; built by `QuestDetail.Of(QuestEntry entry, UiMessages messages)` when the selection changes, never per frame.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/QuestsPage`: holds a `QuestList`; `Advance` moves its glide; Up and Down move it and rebuild the detail; draws the list with `UiTextures.DrawGlidingSelection` and the detail's lines in a second column.
    - `Ui/MessageIds` `QuestKindMain`, `QuestKindSide`; `ui.json` "Main", "Side"; the page's hint gains Up/Down.
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuestListTests: MoveDown_TwoQuests_SelectsTheSecond`
    - `QuestListTests: Show_Again_BackOnTheFirst`
    - `QuestListTests: Show_NoQuest_HasNoCursor`
    - `QuestDetailTests: Of_ActiveQuest_HasItsCurrentStageText`
    - `QuestDetailTests: Of_MainQuest_HasTheMainKindName`
    - `QuestDetailTests: Of_SideQuest_HasTheSideKindName`
    - `QuestsPageTests: Update_Down_DetailFollowsTheSelection`
  - **Verify by hand:** take the placeholder quest (N1), open Quests: its title is selected, beside it "Side", the description and "Ask the innkeeper ..."; after the innkeeper the stage text changes. With a second quest (a test save), Up and Down glide the highlight.
