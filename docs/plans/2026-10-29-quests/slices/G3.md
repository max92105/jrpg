# G3. The completed quests

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After G2. Q5 (one list with bands), Q7 (what a completed quest shows). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (page texts).

- **G3. The completed quests, under their band** (Game + text, about 160 lines)
  - **Goal:** the list shows an ACTIVE band over the active quests and a COMPLETED band over the completed ones (a band only when it has a quest); the cursor passes over the bands; a completed quest's detail shows its description and a "Completed" note instead of a stage text.
  - **New types:**
    - `Menus/QuestRow` (internal sealed record): one row of the quest list: a band's heading, or a quest's entry with whether it is completed.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/QuestList`: built from `Active` and `Completed` as rows; its `ListCursor` enables quest rows only; a row index is a list row, not a journal index.
    - `Menus/QuestDetail.Of`: a completed entry gets the completed note in place of the stage text.
    - `Ui/MessageIds` `QuestsActive`, `QuestsCompleted`, `QuestCompletedNote`; `ui.json` "Active", "Completed", "Completed".
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuestListTests: Show_ActiveAndCompleted_CompletedUnderItsBand`
    - `QuestListTests: MoveDown_FromLastActive_SkipsTheBand`
    - `QuestListTests: Show_OnlyCompleted_NoActiveBandAndCompletedSelected`
    - `QuestListTests: Show_OnlyActive_NoCompletedBand`
    - `QuestDetailTests: Of_CompletedQuest_HasTheCompletedNoteNoStageText`
