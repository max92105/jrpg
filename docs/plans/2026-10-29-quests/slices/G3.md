# G3. The Active and Completed categories

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After G2. Decisions 390 (two categories, Left and Right), 391 (completed oldest first), 392 (the completed note). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (page texts).

- **G3. The Active and Completed categories, switched with Left and Right** (Core + Game + text, about 210 lines)
  - **Goal:** the page shows one category at a time, Active first; Left and Right switch between Active and Completed (no wrap), each with its own cursor, the category's name over the list (plain; L2 gives it the tabs' look); a completed quest's detail shows its description and a "Completed" note in place of the stage text; an empty category says so.
  - **New types:**
    - `Menus/QuestCategory` (internal enum): `Active`, `Completed`, in the order shown.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Core/Quests/QuestJournal.Completed`: main before side, each oldest first, every entry at the stage it was completed at.
    - `Menus/QuestList`: one cursor and highlight per category; `Category`, `ShowNext()`, `ShowPrevious()`; built from `Active` and `Completed`.
    - `Menus/QuestDetail.Of`: a completed entry gets the completed note instead of the stage text.
    - `Menus/QuestsPage`: Left and Right switch the category and rebuild the detail; the hint names them.
    - `Ui/MessageIds` `QuestsActive`, `QuestsCompleted`, `QuestsNoneCompleted`, `QuestCompletedNote`; `ui.json` "Active", "Completed", "No completed quests.", "Completed".
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuestJournalTests: Completed_TwoQuests_OldestFirst`
    - `QuestJournalTests: Completed_MainAndSide_MainFirstAtTheirStages`
    - `QuestListTests: ShowNext_FromActive_ShowsCompleted`
    - `QuestListTests: ShowNext_OnCompleted_StaysOnCompleted`
    - `QuestListTests: ShowPrevious_BackOnActive_KeepsItsCursor`
    - `QuestListTests: Show_NothingCompleted_CompletedHasNoCursor`
    - `QuestDetailTests: Of_CompletedQuest_HasTheCompletedNoteNoStageText`
  - **Watch:** music plan: if its S3 has landed, a category switch asks `menuMove`.
  - **Verify by hand:** finish the errand: Active says "No quests yet."; Right shows Completed with the errand, its detail the description and "Completed"; Left goes back. Load a save from the middle of the errand: Active lists it, Completed is empty.
