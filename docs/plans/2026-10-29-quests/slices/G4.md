# G4. The Quests page's look

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After G3. Q5 (the main mark). `.claude/rules/game.md` "UI look"; look at `docs/ui-reference/menu-status.png` (sections under dark bands) and `menu-inventory-description.webp` (a list column with its detail) first. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (main quest mark), "Replacement mapping".

- **G4. The Quests page's look: columns, bands, kind marks, wrapped text** (Game, raw 150, about 240 lines at 1.6x)
  - **Goal:** the page looks like the menu's other pages: the list in the dark page column (`ColumnPanelView`, `UiTheme.PageColumn`) with its ACTIVE and COMPLETED bands (`SectionTitle`), each quest row with a small diamond mark (pale gold for main, dim for side) and the shared gliding selection; the detail beside it in the side column: the title in the title serif (`FontLibrary.TitleFontAt`), MAIN or SIDE in spaced caps, the description wrapped, then an OBJECTIVE band with the stage text wrapped (or the completed note).
  - **New types:**
    - `Menus/QuestListView`: draws the list column: bands, rows with their marks, the selection, scroll cues when the list is longer than its rows; owns `VisibleRows`.
    - `Menus/QuestDetailView`: draws the detail column; wraps the texts when the detail changes (`RichTextLayout` with the column's width, as `StatusSkillsView`), never per frame.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/QuestsPage`: its plain draw goes; it holds the two views and calls them.
    - `Ui/UiTheme`: `QuestMainMark`, `QuestSideMark` (placeholders, design.md "Tunables").
    - `Ui/MessageIds` `QuestsObjective`; `ui.json` "Objective".
  - **Layout check, written in the slice before drawing** (the stats review's rule): the page area's width and the body's 848 px height (`MenuLayout.BodyHeight`), the list column's width, the row pitch and two bands giving `VisibleRows`; the detail column's width, the title, kind and description heights and the band, leaving the stage text at least three lines above the footer.
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuestDetailViewTests: Wrap_PlaceholderErrandTexts_FitTheColumnAboveTheFooter` (a probe with the repository's real fonts and the real quest texts, as the affix stats retro asks)
    - `QuestListViewTests: VisibleRows_BodyHeightWithTwoBands_MatchesTheWrittenArithmetic`
    - Existing `QuestsPageTests` pass unchanged (the draw-only move's guard); before/after captures, not invented tests.
  - **Verify by hand:** with the placeholder quest active, then completed: compare the page to the Status page's bands and the reference shots; the highlight glides; a long description wraps inside its column. Capture both states (F12).
