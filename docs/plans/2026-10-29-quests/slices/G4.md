# G4. Quest notices

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After L3 (any time after R1 and N1 works). Decision 387. ADR 0006 (a step that waits), ADR 0016 (skipping stops at it). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (a step waits only for its line), "Replacement mapping" (`QuestLog` returns). (The page's look this file held before is now L1 to L3.)

- **G4. A quest step that changed the log shows a line** (Core + Game + text, about 210 lines)
  - **Goal:** a quest step that changed the log waits, and the dialogue box shows "Quest started: Placeholder errand", "Quest updated: ..." or "Quest completed: ..."; a step that changed nothing (a quest already started) applies at once and shows nothing.
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Quests/QuestChange` (public sealed record): what a quest step changed: the quest and its `QuestChangeKind`.
    - `Core/Quests/QuestChangeKind` (public enum): `Started`, `Advanced`, `Completed`.
    - `Game/Dialogue/QuestNoticeText` (internal static): a quest change as its notice line, from `ui.json` (as `FoundItemText`).
  - **Changes:**
    - `QuestLog.Start`, `AdvanceTo`, `Complete` return `QuestChange?` (null when nothing changed; an advance that starts a quest is `Started`); their summaries say so.
    - `EventRun.ApplyQuestStep` (QS's helper, so `ApplyAndCheckWait` doesn't grow): returns whether the step waits; `CurrentQuestChange` (null on any other step), cleared as `CurrentReceipt` is; the class summary names it.
    - `MapEventStarter` and `ExplorationState.CurrentQuestChange` (R1 left the room).
    - `Game/Exploration/EventPresenter`: the three steps join the steps it reads as pages, and the reading of a quest step is `QuestNoticeText.Of(...)`.
    - `Ui/MessageIds` `QuestStarted`, `QuestUpdated`, `QuestCompleted`; `ui.json` with a `{title}` placeholder.
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuestLogTests: Start_NotStarted_ReturnsStarted`
    - `QuestLogTests: Start_Active_ReturnsNull`
    - `QuestLogTests: AdvanceTo_NotStarted_ReturnsStarted`
    - `QuestLogTests: Complete_Active_ReturnsCompleted`
    - `EventRunQuestTests: Start_QuestStepThatChangedTheLog_WaitsWithItsChange`
    - `EventRunQuestTests: Start_QuestStepThatChangedNothing_DoesNotWait`
    - `EventRunQuestTests: Start_QuestStepBeforeDialogue_WaitsOnTheQuestStepFirst` (QS's `Start_QuestStepBeforeDialogue_AppliedAndWaitsOnTheDialogue`, re-pinned)
    - `EventRunQuestTests: SkipPresentation_ChangingQuestStepBetweenStills_StopsAtIt` (QS's skip test, re-pinned)
    - `QuestNoticeTextTests: Of_Started_NamesTheQuest`, `Of_Advanced_NamesTheQuest`, `Of_Completed_NamesTheQuest`
    - `EventPresenterQuestTests: Update_StartQuestStep_ShowsTheStartedLine` (new file: `EventPresenterTests` is at 305)
  - **Watch:** `EventPresenter` (after the gold plan's I2a and I2b); N1's `StartMapQuestTests` read through the new line (one more Confirm).
