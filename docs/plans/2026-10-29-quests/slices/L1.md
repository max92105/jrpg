# L1. The list column's look

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After G3. `.claude/rules/game.md` "UI look"; look at `docs/ui-reference/menu-inventory-description.webp` (a list column with its detail) and `menu-status.png` first. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Tunables" (main quest mark, page list layout), "Replacement mapping".

- **L1. The list column's look: rows, kind marks, one list layout in `Ui/`** (Game, raw 120, about 190 lines at 1.6x)
  - **Goal:** the quest list looks like the menu's other page lists: in the dark page column (`ColumnPanelView`, `UiTheme.PageColumn`), one row per quest at the shared row height, a small diamond mark before the title (pale gold for main, dim for side), the shared gliding selection; past 14 quests the window scrolls with the cursor (`ListCursor`), without scroll cues (their shared drawer is a backlog item, taken by a list that needs it).
  - **New types:**
    - `Ui/PageListLayout` (internal static): a menu page list's margins and row height, in one place: `Margin` 28, `TopMargin` 20, `RowHeight` 52 (the values the page lists use today). Only `QuestListView` reads it here; the other copies wait in the backlog (appended at approval: the Forge's, the Inventory's and the others').
    - `Menus/QuestListView`: draws the list column: rows with their marks and the selection; owns `VisibleRows`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/QuestsPage`: the plain list draw goes; it calls the view (the plain detail stays until L3).
    - `Ui/UiTheme`: `QuestMainMark`, `QuestSideMark` (placeholders, design.md "Tunables").
  - **Pixel arithmetic, written in the slice before drawing** (the stats review's rule): the body is 848 px tall (`MenuLayout.BodyHeight`, 128 to 976); the list column 640 px wide from the page area's left; L2's tabs take 48 px plus a 12 px gap under the top margin of 20; 14 rows of 52 (728) fit in the 768 left, so `VisibleRows` is 14; the mark (12 px) and an 8 px gap sit before the title, which is ellipsized past the column's width minus the margins (measured once per row when the list is built).
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuestListViewTests: VisibleRows_BodyWithTabs_IsFourteen` (the written arithmetic, from `MenuLayout.BodyHeight` and `PageListLayout`)
    - Existing `QuestsPageTests` and `QuestListTests` pass unchanged (the draw move's guard); before and after captures, not invented tests.
  - **Verify by hand:** with the errand active (and a test save holding many quests): the rows, the marks, the highlight gliding, the window following the cursor past 14 quests. Capture it (F12) beside the Inventory page.
