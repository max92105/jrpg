# L3. The detail column's look

Part of [the quests plan](../README.md). After L2. Decision 392. Look at `docs/ui-reference/menu-status.png` (sections under dark bands, values beside labels) first. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Replacement mapping".

- **L3. The detail column's look: serif title, wrapped texts, the Objective band** (Game, raw 125, about 200 lines at 1.6x)
  - **Goal:** beside the list, in the side column (`UiTheme.SideColumn`): the quest's title in the title serif (`FontLibrary.TitleFontAt`), MAIN or SIDE under it in spaced caps, the description wrapped, then an OBJECTIVE band (`SectionTitle`) with the stage text wrapped, or the completed note for a completed quest.
  - **New types:**
    - `Menus/QuestDetailView`: draws the detail column; wraps the texts when the detail changes (`RichTextLayout` at the column's width, as `StatusSkillsView`), never per frame.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/QuestsPage`: the plain detail draw goes; it calls the view.
    - `Ui/MessageIds` `QuestsObjective`; `ui.json` "Objective".
  - **Pixel arithmetic, written in the slice before drawing:** the detail column from the list column's right (L1's 640 px) plus a 40 px gap to the page area's right minus 40; its height 848 px: the top margin, the title's line height, the kind line, a gap, the description's lines, the band (`SectionTitle.Height` plus its gap), the stage text's lines; the description and stage text together keep at least three lines each above the footer.
  - **Tests:**
    - `QuestDetailViewTests: Wrap_PlaceholderErrandTexts_FitTheColumnAboveTheFooter` (a probe with the repository's real fonts and the real quest texts, as the affix stats retro asks)
    - `QuestDetailViewTests: Wrap_DetailChanged_WrapsOncePerChange` (an internal wrap count as the seam)
    - Existing `QuestsPageTests` and `QuestDetailTests` pass unchanged.
  - **Verify by hand:** the errand active, then completed: the serif title, SIDE, the description, the band and the stage text or "Completed"; a long description (a test quest) wraps inside its column. Capture both states (F12) beside the Status page.
