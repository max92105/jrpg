# B1. The battle's start and its impact sounds from freesound

Part of [the sound pass plan](../README.md). After P1. Decisions 423 (the music plan), 437. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The pipeline" ("Finding a sound") and "Key decisions" 1, 7.

- **B1. The battle's start and its impact sounds from freesound: hit, critical, miss, block, resisted, status, heal, fall** (content, about 140 lines: nine manifest entries, the credits)
  - **Goal:** the sounds of a turn's landing, and of the flash that starts a battle, are real sounds. File names unchanged, so no code or test changes.
  - **Slots (brief, longest length):** each lands on its beat, so a clean, immediate attack matters more than anything else.
    - `effects/PlaceholderBattleStart.wav`: a glass shatter or a sharp sword-draw "shing", matching the encounter flash breaking the frozen frame. 1.0 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderHit.wav`: a punchy body impact (thud, smack), mid weight; the most heard battle sound. 0.3 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderCritical.wav`: clearly bigger than the hit: a heavy impact with a crack or a metallic ring. 0.5 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderMiss.wav`: a thin, high, quick whiff of air past the ear, or a light dodging scuff; nothing like a weapon's swing (K1's slash is a blade swish, its blunt a heavy whoosh). 0.25 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderBlock.wav`: a shield's metallic clang or a wooden knock. 0.4 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderResisted.wav`: a dull, muffled thump or a dampened fizzle, "it didn't take". 0.4 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderStatusApplied.wav`: a sharp, short sting (a bright, slightly dissonant stab), not a shimmer; buffs and curses share it. 0.5 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderHeal.wav`: a warm, rising chime (restores, revives, life stolen); the only chime among the battle sounds. 0.8 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderFall.wav`: a body collapsing, or a heavy thud fading out. 0.8 s.
  - **Changes:** `content/audio/sound-sources.json` (nine entries), the nine WAVs (`-Only` each), `content/audio/CREDITS.md` (rewritten by the script).
  - **Tests:** none new; `UiSoundFilesTests` passes; the startup check finds every file.
  - **Report:** the nine chosen pages, any CC-BY flagged.
  - **Verify by hand:** heard at Phase 2's listening (P2's hand check): no separate listening here. Before committing: each printed length is within its brief and its peak at the manifest's level; the game starts with no audio problem.
