# B2. Every other battle sound from freesound

Part of [the sound pass plan](../README.md). After B1. Decisions 423 (the music plan), 437. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The pipeline" ("Finding a sound") and "Key decisions" 1, 7.

- **B2. Every other battle sound from freesound: ticks, backfire, stun, gain, status ended, move, extra turn, item, Defend, enraged, escape** (content, about 180 lines: twelve manifest entries, the credits)
  - **Goal:** every battle event kind's sound and the escape's are real sounds; only the three skill sounds are still tones after it. File names unchanged.
  - **Slots (brief, longest length):**
    - `effects/PlaceholderPoisonTick.wav`: a sickly bubble or squelch. 0.4 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderRegenTick.wav`: a single soft water droplet, quieter and shorter than the heal's chime. 0.3 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderBackfire.wav`: a spell misfiring: a crackling zap or a sputtering pop. 0.6 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderStunned.wav`: a dull bonk with a ring, or a dizzy warble. 0.6 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderResourceGain.wav`: a small rising blip or power-up; frequent (hits taken and landed give gains), so short and quiet. 0.3 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderStatusEnded.wav`: an airy release (a short breath out, a soft puff), the status letting go; no shimmer. 0.4 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderMove.wav`: a cloth shuffle or two quick steps, a place swapped. 0.4 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderExtraTurn.wav`: a quick upward whoosh or a bright accent, "again". 0.5 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderItemUse.wav`: a bottle uncorked or a pouch opened. 0.5 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderDefend.wav`: a guard raised: a leather and armor rustle with a light clank. 0.4 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderEnraged.wav`: a monster's roar or growl, the boss entering its phase. 1.2 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderEscape.wav`: running footsteps fading away. 1.0 s.
  - **Changes:** `content/audio/sound-sources.json` (twelve entries), the twelve WAVs, `content/audio/CREDITS.md`.
  - **Tests:** none new; `UiSoundFilesTests` passes; the startup check finds every file.
  - **Report:** the twelve chosen pages, any CC-BY flagged.
  - **Verify by hand:** heard at Phase 2's listening (P2's hand check). Before committing: each printed length is within its brief and its peak at the manifest's level; the game starts with no audio problem.
