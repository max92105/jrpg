# K1. Skill sounds in nine families from freesound; the tone script retired

Part of [the sound pass plan](../README.md). After B2. Decisions 431, 437. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 1, 6 and 7, "Replacement mapping".

- **K1. Skill sounds in nine families from freesound; the tone script retired** (content + Data tests, about 180 lines: nine manifest entries, 23 `soundFile` lines, the test rows, the deletions)
  - **Goal:** each skill names its family's sound (decision 431), every effect in `content/audio/effects/` is a freesound sound or a tune, and nothing generates tones any more.
  - **Slots (brief, longest length):** a skill's sound plays as its action starts, before its events' sounds land (decision 423), so each is the wind-up or the cast, never an impact or a chime that would read as the landing.
    - `effects/PlaceholderSkillFire.wav` (fire_blast, fireball, meteor): a fire whoosh or ignition. 0.8 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderSkillDark.wav` (eldritch_bolt, void_rift, chaos_lance): a low, dark magic swell. 0.8 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderSkillLight.wav` (smite): a bright, airy swell rising to the strike. 0.8 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderSkillSlash.wav` (power_strike, cleave, equilibrium): a blade's swing swish. 0.4 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderSkillBlunt.wav` (shield_bash, rampage, palm_strike, flurry, crushing_blow): a heavy swing whoosh, lower than the slash. 0.4 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderSkillMend.wav` (heal, recovery, heal_all, field_dressing, rejuvenate): a soft rising pad or breath of magic, not a chime, so the heal's chime still reads as the landing. 0.8 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderSkillShout.wav` (taunt, war_cry): a short battle cry or shout. 0.8 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderSkillWard.wav` (aura_of_protection, wide_aura, ready): a low metallic hum, a shield being raised in the air. 0.8 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderSkillHex.wav` (expose, rot): a sinister whisper or a dissonant sting held low. 0.8 s.
  - **Changes:**
