# P1. The manifest, the fetch script and the credits; the four interface sounds from freesound; the content rule amended

Part of [the sound pass plan](../README.md). After the ADR 0036 step. Decisions 426-429, 437. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The pipeline" (with "Finding a sound") and "Key decisions" 1 to 3.

- **P1. The manifest, the fetch script and the credits; the four interface sounds from freesound; the content rule amended** (script + content, about 240 lines; no C#)
  - **Goal:** the pipeline works end to end on the sounds heard most: four freesound sounds chosen, named in the manifest, converted to the game's WAV format under their current names, credited; the rule says where placeholder effects come from from now on. Phase 1's listening is this slice's hand check.
  - **First:** check `ffmpeg` is on the PATH; if not, ask the developer to run `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` (decision 428), since it's a tool installed on the PC. Measure today's tones and tunes (`perl .claude/scripts/new-placeholder-tune.pl measure content/audio/effects/*.wav`) and set the manifest's `peakDb` to the tones' typical peak (design.md, "Tunables").
  - **New files:**
    - `.claude/scripts/fetch-placeholder-sounds.ps1`: downloads each manifest entry's freesound preview and converts it into its named WAV, then rewrites the credits (design.md, "The pipeline", steps 1 to 7: the refusals of a disallowed license and of a repeated (id, start) key included). Under about 160 lines; comments say why (the bitexact flags, the trim relative to the peak, the reversed fade, the BOM-less UTF-8).
    - `content/audio/sound-sources.json`: `peakDb`, `tunes` (the two jingles), and the four entries below.
    - `content/audio/CREDITS.md`: written by the script, never by hand.
  - **Slots (brief, longest length):**
    - `effects/PlaceholderMenuMove.wav`: a soft, dry tick or blip, neutral pitch; heard on every cursor step, so quiet and unobtrusive. 0.08 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderMenuConfirm.wav`: a brighter, crisp click or two-note blip that reads as "yes". 0.25 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderMenuCancel.wav`: a lower, softer blip or a short downward swish that reads as "back". 0.2 s.
    - `effects/PlaceholderTextTyping.wav`: a tiny click (a typewriter key, a soft blip), ticked at most every 60 ms (decision 422), so it must end well before. 0.04 s.
  - **Changes:**
    - `.claude/rules/content.md`: line 15 becomes two rules: "Placeholder music and the victory and defeat jingles come from `.claude/scripts/new-placeholder-tune.pl`, never plain held tones: the developer has to live with them until the real music (decision 429)." and "Placeholder sound effects come from freesound.org (the developer's instruction of 2026-11-02; decisions 426, 427, 437; ADR 0036): CC0 first, CC-BY when nothing CC0 fits, never NC, SA or Sampling+, and never an upload that names or imitates a commercial game or film; each is an entry in `content/audio/sound-sources.json`, converted by `.claude/scripts/fetch-placeholder-sounds.ps1`, which also writes `content/audio/CREDITS.md`. Never edit a converted WAV by hand. Any later placeholder sound comes from there too."
    - `docs/map.md`: the Data `Audio` line gains "the placeholder effects' sources: `content/audio/sound-sources.json` and its generated `CREDITS.md` (ADR 0036), checked by `Audio/SoundSourcesTests` (P2)".
    - `docs/plans/backlog.md`, line "The effects other than the two outcome jingles are still plain generated tones": becomes "...are being replaced by freesound placeholders (plan 2026-11-02-sound-pass)"; K1 deletes it.
  - **Tests:** none new (the manifest's check is P2, once no effect is a tone). `UiSoundFilesTests` still passes: the four names are unchanged. `verify.ps1` passes (the script stays under 300 lines and its comments under 120 columns).
  - **Report:** the four chosen pages, each license, any CC-BY flagged.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - Run `powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .claude/scripts/fetch-placeholder-sounds.ps1` twice: the second run downloads nothing (cache) and writes the same bytes. Run it with `-Only effects/PlaceholderMenuMove.wav`: only that file and the credits are rewritten. Change a license to `CC-BY-NC-4.0` or duplicate an entry's id and start: the script refuses, naming the entry; revert. Without `ffmpeg` on the PATH (a new terminal with it removed): the script stops with the winget line.
    - Phase 1's listening (decision 437): start the game, no startup audio problem in the console. On the title, move, confirm and cancel: each sound is short, starts at the press with no lag, has no click at its start or end, and sits at about the level of the music. In a dialogue, the typing ticks are tiny and don't blur together. A vetoed sound gets a shortlist of three page links to play in the browser; the developer's pick replaces the entry before Phase 2.
    - Open `content/audio/CREDITS.md`: four rows, each linking its freesound page and its license's deed, with the modification note and the fetch date.
