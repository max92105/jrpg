# P2. The manifest's check: every effect sourced or a tune, licenses allowed, every entry credited; Phase 2's listening

Part of [the sound pass plan](../README.md). After K1 (the last tone replaced). Decisions 426, 427, 437. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The pipeline" and "Key decisions" 2, 3.

- **P2. The manifest's check; Phase 2's listening** (Data tests, about 80 lines)
  - **Goal:** the manifest, the effects folder and the credits can't drift apart: `verify.ps1` fails on an effect file with no source, a disallowed license, or credits not regenerated after a manifest edit. What the script already refuses (a repeated (id, start) key) and what freesound doesn't promise (its URL layouts, usernames) aren't tested. Then the developer hears every Phase 2 sound.
  - **New types:**
    - `tests/Jrpg.Data.Tests/Audio/SoundSourcesTests`: the repository's sound manifest checked against the effects folder and the credits. Reads `content/audio/sound-sources.json` from the test output (the project copies all of `content/`) into private records with `System.Text.Json`, unmapped members refused (`JsonUnmappedMemberHandling.Disallow`), so a misspelt field fails; names are compared with the folder's listing, case included, as `UiSoundFilesTests` does.
  - **Tests** (`SoundSourcesTests`):
    - `Manifest_EveryEffectFile_IsSourcedOrATune`: every file in `content/audio/effects/` is an entry's `file` or in `tunes`.
    - `Manifest_EveryLicense_IsAllowed`: `CC0-1.0`, `CC-BY-3.0` or `CC-BY-4.0` (decision 427); the script refuses the others too, this catches a hand edit.
    - `Credits_EveryEntry_IsListed`: `CREDITS.md` holds each entry's `file` with its `page`.
  - **Check that each can fail** before committing: locally add a WAV with no entry, set a license to `CC-BY-NC-4.0`, add an entry without rerunning the script; each matching test fails naming the file; revert.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - `verify.ps1` passes.
    - Phase 2's listening (decision 437): start a forest battle: the flash's sound fits the shatter. Attack until a miss, a critical and a fall have happened (Flurry's four hits give several impacts in one action): each sound lands with its number, never after it; the critical reads bigger than the hit; the miss doesn't sound like a swing. Rot and Expose (the sting), a heal (the chime), Rejuvenate (the droplet tick), a status running out (the release), Defend until a block, Shield Bash or Palm Strike until a stun, a Move, Ready on an ally (the hum of Ward, then the extra turn), a potion, a backfiring spell, and Run. Fight the Goblin Chief until its phase (the roar; edit a map's table locally to reach it, decisions 226, 399, and revert). Cast one skill of each family: Fire Blast, Eldritch Bolt, Smite, Power Strike, Shield Bash, Heal, Taunt, Aura of Protection, Rot; each family is recognizable before its impact, and the wind-up and the landing don't blur. The frequent sounds (gains, ticks) don't tire the ear over a long fight.
    - Each vetoed sound gets a shortlist of three page links; the developer's pick replaces the entry (`-Only`, credits rewritten) before Phase 3 starts. Note the vetoes and picks in the Done section.
