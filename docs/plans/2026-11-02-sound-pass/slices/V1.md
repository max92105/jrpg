# V1. The victory screen sounds a level-up, the drops and leaving

Part of [the sound pass plan](../README.md). After FE2 (the drops reuse `Field.ItemGot`). Decisions 430, 432, 437. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" 4, 5 and 7.

- **V1. The victory screen sounds a level-up, the drops and leaving** (Game + content, about 170 lines; adds no view type)
  - **Goal:**
    - **Level-up:** the level-up sound on the update a member's "Level Up!" first shows: when the bars' level-ups rose, by filling or by the skip that fills them at once. Two members levelling in one update ask it once.
    - **Drops:** the Confirm that shows the Items step asks `Field.ItemGot`, the field's found item sound.
    - **Leaving:** the Confirm that leaves (on the drops, or after the bars when nothing dropped) asks the confirm sound. The skip asks nothing of its own (only the level-ups it reveals); a Confirm before the entrance ends is the skip.
  - **New slot:** `effects/PlaceholderLevelUp.wav`: a single bright, non-melodic hit (a rising "whoomp" with a sparkle tail, a struck crystal), never a fanfare: it plays over the victory fanfare (decision 432). 0.8 s.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Ui/UiSoundFiles.Battle`: `LevelUp`.
    - `Battle/VictoryScreen` (96 → about 120): an `AskedSounds` cleared as `Update` starts, exposed as `Sounds`; the bars' summed `LevelUps` compared around their updates and the skip; the drops' and the leaving's asks. The class summary gains the sounds.
    - `Battle/BattleScene` (288 → about 292): `UpdateOutcomeScreen`'s victory branch keeps the request, copies `victory.Sounds` into the scene's, then returns it (as `UpdateDefeat` does). It stays under 300; the backlog's line on splitting the outcome screens stays for the next change that grows it (README, "Out of scope").
    - Content: one manifest entry, the WAV, the credits.
  - **Tests:**
    - `VictoryScreenSoundTests` (new; `VictoryScreenTests` is 148; reuse its spoils builders):
      - `Update_BarFillsALevel_AsksTheLevelUpSound`
      - `Update_UpdateAfterTheLevelUp_AsksNothing` (the per-update clear)
      - `Update_BarFillsWithoutALevel_AsksNothing`
      - `Update_SkipLandsALevel_AsksTheLevelUpSound`
      - `Update_SkipWithoutALevel_AsksNothing`
      - `Update_ConfirmShowsTheDrops_AsksTheItemSound`
      - `Update_ConfirmLeavesWithoutDrops_AsksTheConfirmSound`
      - `Update_ConfirmLeavesFromTheDrops_AsksTheConfirmSound`
    - `BattleSceneSoundTests` (146): `Update_VictoryConfirmLeaves_SceneAsksTheConfirmSound` (the pass-up to what the flow reads: a won battle's leaving Confirm needs no level-up, so it holds whatever the fixture's experience).
  - **Report:** the chosen page, CC-BY flagged if so.
  - **Verify by hand:** Phase 5's listening (decision 437): win a forest battle with a party close to a level (the Status page shows the experience left): the level-up hit sounds as "Level Up!" pops, over or after the fanfare, and doesn't fight it; skipping with Confirm still sounds it. A battle with drops: Confirm sounds the pluck as the Items step shows, then the confirm on leaving. Nothing sounds twice. A vetoed sound gets a shortlist of three page links; the pick replaces it before L1.
