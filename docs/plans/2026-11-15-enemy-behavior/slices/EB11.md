# EB11. Triggers: a foe's HP is below, every Nth turn of its own

Core + Data + Editor, about 290 lines (Core about 45 and 12 tests at about 12 lines; Data about 15 and 5 tests;
Editor about 15 and 2 tests). Needs EB9 (the memory). Design: design.md, sections 1 (the bounds), 2.5, 3.1; decisions
835, 838, 843, 847 (trimmable: striking a row removes its half).

## Goal
Two states read at the enemy's turn: "a foe's HP is below N %" (that one: the living foes below, so "finish off the
weakest" is one rule) and "every Nth turn of its own" ("every 3rd turn: Crushing Blow", the boss that charges a big
move on a beat; a skipped turn doesn't count; that one: itself). The number box reads a percent or turns, from 2 to
10.

## Changes
- Core `Battle/TacticConditionKind`: `FoeHpBelow`, `EveryNthTurn`, each with its summary.
- Core `Battle/TacticCondition`: `int Turns = 0` after `Percent` ("the N of every Nth turn, 2..10; unused by the other
  kinds"); `Battle/TacticConditionNumber.Turns`; shape rows `FoeHpBelow` (Foes, Percent), `EveryNthTurn` (Itself,
  Turns).
- Core `Battle/TacticMemory`: `public int TurnsActed { get; private set; }`, `public void NoteTurnActed()`;
  `TacticMemoryNotes.Note`: an enemy actor's turn counts once per `Execute` it acted in (a skipped turn has no
  `Execute` of its own).
- Core `Battle/TacticConditions.CombatantsMeeting`: `FoeHpBelow` → the living foes under the share (`battle.Combatants`
  on the other side); `EveryNthTurn` → the enemy when `(TurnsActed + 1) % Turns == 0` (the turn it is choosing for is
  its `TurnsActed + 1`th; its comment says so). The switch reaches 6 arms and a default (the reactions share one).
- Core `Battle/EnemyTargeting.HealCandidates`: nothing to change (decision 835 already gives both kinds the legal
  allies: `FoeHpBelow` names foes, `EveryNthTurn` names itself and isn't `OwnHpBelow`).
- Data `Battle/EnemyTacticsValidator.FindConditionProblem`: by number, `Turns` → `Within("...when.turns", turns, 2,
  MostTurnsBetween)` and the percent left out; the others refuse a `turns`: "turns must be left out of allyHpBelow (was
  3)." `private const int MostTurnsBetween = 10;` with the reason (decision 847).
- Data `Content/AbsentWhenZeroFilter`: `(typeof(TacticCondition), "turns")`.
- Editor `TacticConditionChoice.All`: "a foe's HP is below", "every Nth turn of its own"; `TacticRuleRow`: `NumberUnit`
  "turns" for `Turns`, `ToAction` writes the number to `Turns`, the problem names "Turns"; `From` reads `Turns` into the
  box.
- `docs/map.md`, Core `Battle` row.
