# IU10. The equipment form: kind, damage and damage type

Editor, about 170 lines (view model about 60, XAML about 25, tests about 85). Needs IU8. Design: design.md, sections
2.3 and 5.1; decision 880.

## Goal
An equipment item or a unique shows its form under the general fields: its kind (the eight `EquipmentKind`s) and, for a weapon
only, its damage and damage type. A kind changed away from weapon drops the damage and the type from the record it
makes (Data refuses them on anything else); a kind changed to weapon starts the boxes empty, so the problem line asks
for them. The bonuses are carried untouched until IU11.

## New types
- `Editor/Items/ItemEquipmentViewModel` (internal sealed partial, `ObservableObject`): an equipment's kind, damage, type
  and (from IU11) bonus rows as typed.
  - `From(EquipmentStats equipment)`; `Kind`, `IsWeapon`, `DamageText`, `DamageType` (`DamageType?`).
  - `KindChoices` (every `EquipmentKind`) and `DamageTypeChoices` (every `DamageType`), generated from the enums (ADR
    0045's rows over a Core enum).
  - `Problem`: "Damage must be a whole number (was 'x')." for a weapon; Data's checks (at least 1, a type required) come
    through the list.
  - `Changed` (an event): a typed field, never a `Problem` notice.
  - `ToEquipment()`: `source with { Kind, Damage, DamageType }`, damage 0 and type null off a weapon; throws on a
    problem.

## Changes
- `Editor/Items/ItemViewModel`: `Equipment` (`ItemEquipmentViewModel?`, set for an equipment item or a unique), its `Problem` after
  the general ones, `ToItem` taking `Equipment.ToEquipment()`; `Changed` relayed to the list.
- `Editor/Items/ItemCatalogViewModel`: re-checks on an item's `Changed` (typed fields only, `.claude/rules/editor.md`).
- `Editor/Items/ItemCatalogPage.xaml`: the Equipment section under the general fields, visible when `Equipment` is
  set (equipment and uniques): Kind, then Damage and Damage type visible through `IsWeapon`; boxes on
  `PropertyChanged`. Local styles `BasedOn` the theme's.
