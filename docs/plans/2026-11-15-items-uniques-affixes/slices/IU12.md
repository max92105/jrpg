# IU12. The consumable form: effect kind, targeting, power, damage type, own strip

Editor, about 210 lines (view model about 70, the strip box about 25, XAML about 30, tests about 85). Needs IU8.
Design: design.md, sections 2.3, 2.4 and 5.1; decisions 873, 881.

## Goal
A consumable shows its effect under the general fields: the kind (damage, restoreHp, revive, rest), the targeting
(those Data allows an item), its power (HP for a damage, a heal or a revive; hidden for a rest, which writes 0), a
damage's damage type (hidden otherwise, which writes null), and the item's own strip (an editable box over the effects
images, empty plays its user's strip; a note when the file is missing). Items keep a flat power (decision 873): no
tiered record is offered. The effect it makes is its source's `with` the edited fields, so statuses, hits and range are
carried; row 7's `CatalogReferences.Rebound` keeps any status grant bound. If the developer later runs IU5, this form's
power box becomes a record box (IU5's file).

## New types
- `Editor/Items/ItemEffectViewModel` (internal sealed partial, `ObservableObject`): a consumable's effect as typed:
  kind, targeting, power, damage type.
  - `From(Effect effect)`; `Kind`, `Targeting`, `PowerText`, `DamageType` (`DamageType?`), `TakesPower` (not rest),
    `TakesDamageType` (damage).
  - `KindChoices`: damage, restoreHp, revive, rest (decision 881); `TargetingChoices`: every `Targeting` but
    `OtherCombatant` and `AlliesInRange` (Data's item rules); `DamageTypeChoices` (every `DamageType`).
  - `Problem`: "Power must be a whole number (was 'x')."; Data's checks (a damage needs a type, the range of a power,
    a rest's targeting) come through the list.
  - `Changed` (an event): typed fields only.
  - `ToEffect()`: `source with { Kind, Targeting, Power, DamageType }`, power 0 for a rest and type null off a damage;
    throws on a problem.

## Changes
- `Editor/Items/ItemViewModel`: `Effect` (`ItemEffectViewModel?`, a consumable's), its `Problem` after the general
  ones, `ToItem` taking `Effect.ToEffect()`; `EffectFile` typed (empty is null) and `MissingStripNote`, as the icon's
  (IU8).
- `Editor/Items/ItemCatalogViewModel` and `ItemEditingViewModel`: take the effects images (`Func<IReadOnlyList<string>>`,
  the readable PNGs of `ArtFolders.Effects`, as `CreateEffectStripEditing` reads them), handed over by
  `Shell/MainViewModel.Database`.
- `Editor/Items/ItemCatalogPage.xaml`: the Effect section, visible when `Effect` is set: Effect, Target, Power, Damage
  type, Own strip and its note; boxes on `PropertyChanged`.
