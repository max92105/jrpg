# IU14. One affix as the Affixes page types it

Editor, about 190 lines (about 100 of them tests). Needs IU1. Design: design.md, sections 2.3 and 5.2; decisions 884,
885.

## Goal
`AffixViewModel` holds one affix as typed: its id (while new), stat (every `StatKind`), min, max, weight and gold value
as typed numbers, and one box per equipment kind it rolls on. Its problem names the typed id or a box that isn't a
number; Data's rules (nonzero values, the sign rule, at least one kind, the bound) come through the list (IU15). The
record it makes is its source `with` the typed fields. A new affix starts from decision 885's values; Duplicate copies
every field but the id.

## New types
- `Editor/Loot/AffixViewModel` (internal sealed partial, `ObservableObject`): one affix as typed and the record it makes.
  - `From(AffixDefinition affix)`, `New()`, `Duplicate()`.
  - `Id` (typed while `IsNew`), `Stat`, `MinText`, `MaxText`, `WeightText`, `GoldValueText`, `Kinds`
    (`IReadOnlyList<EquipmentKindToggle>`, one per `EquipmentKind` in enum order), `StatChoices` (every `StatKind`).
  - `Label`: "power: Power 2..5", "(new affix)" without an id.
  - `Problem`: the id rule while new (`Objects/ObjectIds.ProblemOf`), then "Min must be a whole number (was 'x')." and
    the same for Max, Weight and Gold value (`Catalogs/TypedNumber`).
  - `Changed` (an event): a typed field or a kind box, never a `Label` or `Problem` notice.
  - `ToAffix()`: throws on a problem; the kinds in enum order.
- `Editor/Loot/EquipmentKindToggle` (internal sealed partial, `ObservableObject`): one equipment kind's box on an affix:
  `Kind`, `Label`, `IsChosen`.
