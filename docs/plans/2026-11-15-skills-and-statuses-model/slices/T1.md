# T1. A tiered skill deals its user's Power times its record's `perPower`

Core, about 160 lines (about 100 of them tests). Needs ADR 0056. Design: design.md, sections 2.2, 2.5 and 5.

## Goal
An effect may name a `TieredEffect` record; used by a member, its damage or heal is the member's Power (gear included)
times the record's `perPower`, ignoring Power's damage percent, held at `DamageFormula.MaxPower`; the crit, resistance,
Exposed, block and dominance chain after it is unchanged. The formula takes the modifiers' sum from the start, so the
developer's two sheet examples are its tests; until WK gives a source, the battle passes 0. Nothing loads a record yet
(T2); tests build them.

## New types
- `Effects/TieredEffect` (public sealed record): a named damage or heal whose amount is its user's Power times its
  `perPower` (decision 782; `effects.json` from T2). `TieredEffect(string Id, EffectKind Kind, int PerPower)` with
  `DamageType? DamageType { get; init; }` (a damage record's type; null for a heal).

## Changes
- `Effects/Effect`: init `TieredEffect? Tier { get; init; }`: "the tiered effect a skill's damage or heal names (ADR
  0056); null for a flat power". The record's summary for `Power` gains "0, and unread, when `Tier` is set".
- `Stats/CombatStats`: init `int Power`: "the combatant's Power, what a tiered effect multiplies (decision 788): a
  member's sheet Power, gear included; an enemy's content power (T3); 0 by default".
- `Battle/MemberBattleStats.Of`: `Power = sheet.Primaries.Power`; its summary lists Power.
- `Effects/DamageFormula`:
  - `public const int MaxPower = 1_000_000`: "the most a tiered amount reaches, so `Calculate`'s product (up to about
    1.8 x 10^12 times the power) stays within a `long` (decision 818)".
  - `public static int TieredAmount(int power, int perPower, int modifierPermille)`: `power x perPower x (1000 +
    modifierPermille) / 1000` in a `long`, rounded down once, held within 0 and `MaxPower`; the comment cites the
    developer's Damage Test sheet (decision 704) and its example.
- `Battle/EffectScaling.ScaledFor`: for Damage, RestoreHp or Revive, a `Tier` gives
  `Power = DamageFormula.TieredAmount(user.Power, tier.PerPower, modifierPermille: 0)` (WK passes the damage modifiers;
  the comment says so); no tier keeps today's `StatFormula.Scaled(power, DamagePermille)`. The summary says Power's
  percent raises flat values only (decision 784). Grants unchanged.

## Tests
- `DamageFormulaTests` (179 lines, about 220 after):
  - `TieredAmount_SheetLowAtPowerOneWithFourHundredSeventyThreePercent_IsEleven` (`TieredAmount(1, 2, 4730)`: 11.46
    rounded down).
  - `TieredAmount_SheetExtremeAtPowerFiveHundredWithFourHundredSeventyThreePercent_Is166170`.
  - `TieredAmount_NoModifiers_IsPowerTimesPerPower` (`TieredAmount(15, 2, 0)`: 30).
  - `TieredAmount_ModifiersAtMinusOneHundredPercent_IsZero` (the floor; `Calculate` then gives its minimum 1).
  - `TieredAmount_HugeProduct_HeldAtMaxPower` (`TieredAmount(int.MaxValue, 1000, 0)`).
- `EffectScalingTests` (51 lines): stats built in the test with `Power = 20` and `DamagePermille = 2000`:
  - `ScaledFor_TieredDamage_IsPowerTimesPerPower` (a record of `perPower` 4: 80, not 160).
  - `ScaledFor_TieredHeal_IsPowerTimesPerPower`.
  - `ScaledFor_FlatDamage_StillRaisedByDamagePercent` (no tier: power 10 at 200 % gives 20; skip if an existing test
    already covers it).
- `BattleTieredSkillTests` (new; a class and a member built in the test with a Power differing from the default 0, the
  enemy with no resistances, certain hits and no crit through the test rules):
  - `Execute_TieredSkill_DealsTheMembersPowerTimesPerPower` (Power 20 from the class, `perPower` 2: 40; covers the
    sheet-to-stats copy, decision 788).
  - `Execute_TieredSkillWithPowerFromGear_CountsTheGear` (an item adding 5 Power: 50).
  - `Execute_TieredSkillWithDominantForce_RaisesTheTieredAmount` (a dominance rule built in the test: 40 x 1.5 = 60;
    decision 783's order).
