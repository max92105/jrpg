# T1. A tiered skill deals its user's Power times its tier

Core, about 140 lines (about 80 of them tests). Needs ADR 0056. Design: design.md, sections 2.2, 2.5 and 5.

## Goal
An effect may name a `DamageTier`; used by a member, its damage or heal is the member's Power (gear included) times the
tier's `perPower`, ignoring Power's damage percent, and the crit, resistance, Exposed, block and dominance chain after
it is unchanged. Nothing loads a tier yet (T2); tests build them.

## New types
- `Effects/DamageTier` (public sealed record): a named step of damage or healing per point of its user's Power
  (decision 704). `DamageTier(string Id, int PerPower)`.

## Changes
- `Effects/Effect`: init `DamageTier? Tier { get; init; }`: "the tier a skill's damage or heal names (ADR 0056); null
  for a flat power". Update the record's summary for `Power` ("ignored, and 0 in content, when `Tier` is set").
- `Stats/CombatStats`: init `int Power`: "the combatant's Power, what a tiered effect multiplies (decision 704): a
  member's sheet Power, gear included; an enemy's content power (T3); 0 by default".
- `Battle/MemberBattleStats.Of`: `Power = sheet.Primaries.Power`; its summary lists Power.
- `Effects/DamageFormula`: `public static int TieredAmount(int power, int perPower)`: `power x perPower` in a `long`,
  held within `int.MaxValue`; the comment cites the developer's Damage Test sheet (decision 704) and that the
  modifiers join in WK.
- `Battle/EffectScaling.ScaledFor`: for Damage, RestoreHp or Revive, a `Tier` gives
  `Power = DamageFormula.TieredAmount(user.Power, tier.PerPower)`; no tier keeps today's `StatFormula.Scaled(power,
  DamagePermille)`. The summary says Power's percent raises flat values only (decision 784). Grants unchanged.

## Tests
- `DamageFormulaTests` (179 lines, about 205 after):
  - `TieredAmount_LowAtPowerFifteen_IsThirty`.
  - `TieredAmount_ExtremeAtPowerFiveHundred_Is29000` (the sheet's Power 500 row without modifiers).
- `EffectScalingTests` (51 lines): stats built in the test with `Power = 20` and `DamagePermille = 2000`:
  - `ScaledFor_TieredDamage_IsPowerTimesPerPower` (tier `perPower` 4: 80, not 160).
  - `ScaledFor_TieredHeal_IsPowerTimesPerPower`.
  - `ScaledFor_FlatDamage_StillRaisedByDamagePercent` (no tier: power 10 at 200 % gives 20; the existing tests may
    already cover it: check and skip if so).
- `BattleTieredSkillTests` (new; a class and a member built in the test with a Power differing from the default 0, the
  enemy with no resistances, certain hits and no crit through the test rules):
  - `Execute_TieredSkill_DealsTheMembersPowerTimesTheTier` (Power 20 from the class, a tier of 2: 40 damage; covers
    the sheet-to-stats copy, decision 788).
  - `Execute_TieredSkillWithPowerFromGear_CountsTheGear` (an item adding 5 Power: 50).
  - `Execute_TieredSkillWithDominantForce_RaisesTheTieredAmount` (a dominance rule built in the test: 40 x 1.5 = 60;
    decision 783's order).
