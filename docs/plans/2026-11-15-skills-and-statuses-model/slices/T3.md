# T3. Enemies have a Power for their tiered skills

Core + Data, about 170 lines (about 110 of them tests). Needs T2. Design: design.md, section 1 (the Chief's power) and
2.2.

## Goal
An enemy record may carry `power` (left out while 0); a combatant made from it has that Power, which its tiered skills
multiply. Data refuses a power outside 0..10 000, and a power of 0 on an enemy whose tactics or phases name a skill
with a tiered effect.
`enemies.json` doesn't change yet (no skill has a tier; T4 sets the Chief's).

## Changes
- `Core/Battle/EnemyDefinition`: `public int Power { get; init; }`, declared right after `DamageType` so the writer
  writes it after `damageType`: "what its tiered skills multiply (decision 787); 0, left out of the file, for an enemy
  with none".
- `Core/Battle/Combatant.FromEnemy`: `Power = enemy.Power` in its `CombatStats`.
- `Data/Content/AbsentWhenZeroFilter`: `(typeof(EnemyDefinition), "power")` with the comment "Decision 787: only an
  enemy with tiered skills has a Power."
- `Data/Battle/EnemyCatalogValidator`: power within 0..10 000 (decision 818: `"power must be within 0..10000 (was
  10001)."`); and, through `EnemyReferences.Skills`, at least 1 when an action of its tactics or phases names a skill
  whose effect has a tiered effect (`"power must be at least 1: its skill 'cleave' names a tiered effect (was 0)."`).
  A private `FindPowerProblem`, so `FindProblem` stays under CA1502.
- `tests/Jrpg.Core.Tests/Builders/EnemyDefinitionBuilder` (117 lines): `WithPower(int power)`.
