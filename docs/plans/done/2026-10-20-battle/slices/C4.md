# C4. A battle with nobody standing is lost at once

Part of [the battle plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md).

- **C4. A battle with nobody standing is lost at once** (Core, about 90 lines; decision 97)
  - **Goal:** if every active member has fallen when a battle starts, the battle begins already lost, instead of throwing. This closes the B1b known crash path.
  - **Changes:**
    - `BattleState` no longer requires a living party member. With none, `Phase` is `Defeat` from the start, and `Execute` throws as for any finished battle. Enemies still need a living member.
    - `GameSession.StartPendingBattle` documents it.
  - **Tests:** `BattleState_Start_NobodyStanding_IsDefeatAtOnce`, `BattleState_Start_NoLivingEnemy_Throws` (unchanged rule), `GameSession_StartPendingBattle_NobodyStanding_StartsLostBattle`.
- **Done, with deviations:** about 60 lines. The planned `BattleState_Start_NoLivingEnemy_Throws` and `..._NobodyStanding_IsDefeatAtOnce` are constructor tests (`Constructor_NoLivingEnemy_Throws`, `Constructor_NobodyStanding_IsDefeatAtOnce`), replacing the old two-sided `Constructor_SideWithNobodyAlive_Throws` theory whose party case this rule reverses. A battle lost at the start has no `BattleEnded` event, since nothing was executed. Until B8b, the Game's turn sequence still lets an enemy act in such a battle and Core throws; B8b1's `BattleScene_Update_BattleLostAtStart_ShowsDefeat` covers it.
