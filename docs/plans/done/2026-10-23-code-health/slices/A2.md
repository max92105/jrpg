# A2. The rail's look: pointer on the focused side, its own view

Part of [the code health plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The inventory rail", tunables "Menu pointer" and "Dimmed selection"; the examples `Menu4`, `Menu7`, `Menu9` (a pointing hand at the rail's category when the rail has the cursor, at the list's row otherwise). Needs A1.

- **A2. The rail's look** (Game, about 140 lines, about 50 of them moved drawing)
  - **Goal:**
    - The player sees where the cursor is. A small pointer (`Ui/MenuPointer`) sits left of the selected tab when the rail has the cursor, and left of the selected row when the list has it.
    - The side without the cursor keeps its selection but dims it: the tab's glow and the row's highlight at half opacity.
    - The rail's drawing leaves `InventoryPage`.
  - **New types:**
    - `Ui/MenuPointer` (static): the menu's "you are here" mark, drawn left of a chosen line.
      - `static void Draw(SpriteBatch batch, Texture2D pixel, Vector2 tip, float opacity)`: a small diamond, like the defeat menu's pointer.
      - The size and gap are placeholder constants.
      - P2 reuses it for the sidebar.
    - `Menus/CategoryRailView`: draws the Inventory page's category rail, one icon tab per category, the selected one lit, with the pointer when the rail has the cursor.
      - `CategoryRailView(UiTextures ui)`.
      - `void Draw(SpriteBatch batch, TextureLibrary textures, Place place, float opacity)`.
      - `readonly record struct Place(CategoryRail Rail, Rectangle Area)`.
      - `const int Width = 72`.
      - `DrawRail` and `DrawRailTab` move here from `InventoryPage`, with their constants.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/InventoryPage`:
      - the rail drawing and its constants go;
      - `DrawRow` draws the pointer on the selected row when the list has the cursor, and the highlight at half opacity when the rail has it.
    - `Ui/UiTheme`: the dimmed-selection opacity (placeholder 0.5) as a named value.
    - `docs/map.md`: the Ui row lists `MenuPointer`; the Menus row lists `CategoryRail`/`CategoryRailView`.
  - **Tests:** none (draw-only). `InventoryPageTests` and `CategoryRailTests` pass unchanged.
  - **Verify by hand** (before/after screenshots with `.claude/scripts/play-game.ps1`, Esc then the Inventory tab):
    - [ ] On the list: the pointer is at the selected item, and the rail's tab is lit but dimmer.
    - [ ] After Left: the pointer is at the rail's tab, and the row highlight is dimmer.
    - [ ] Compare with `docs/ui_ux_exemples/Menu7.webp` and `Menu9.webp`: the same reading of where the cursor is, in our style.
- **Done, with deviations:** `MenuPointer.Draw` takes the pointed line's left end at its middle (`lineStart`) and keeps its own 4 px gap, so callers don't repeat it. `CategoryRailView` uses a private `TabPlace` record so its tab drawing stays at 4 parameters. The list's 12 px gap after the rail stays in `InventoryPage` (`RailGap`), the rail's own sizes move with it. `InventoryPage` is 334 lines. Screenshots: on the list the pointer is at the row and the rail's glow is dimmed; after Left the pointer is at the tab and the row is dimmed. Review: `DrawRow` decides the selected row once (dimmed, or lit with the pointer); the two hand-drawn row pointers elsewhere are a backlog line.
