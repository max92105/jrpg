# P2. The party sidebar's look: shown member lit, reserve set apart, scroll cues

Part of [the code health plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The party sidebar"; examples `Menu2`, `Menu5`, `Menu6`, `Menu11` (a pointing hand at the edited member) and `Menu5`/`Menu9` (the card panel); questions 153, 154 (placeholders). Needs P1 and A2's `Ui/MenuPointer`.

- **P2. Sidebar look** (Game + content, about 150 lines)
  - **Goal:**
    - The shown member reads at a glance: their card is lit (the existing selected look) and `Ui/MenuPointer` sits at its left edge.
    - The reserve is set apart: a "Reserve" caption over a hairline before the first reserve card, and reserve cards at 60 % opacity (question 154 placeholder). The shown card is always at full opacity, even in the reserve.
    - When cards are hidden above or below the window, a small cue (a fading edge or a chevron, placeholder) says so at that edge.
    - Nothing changes in what is listed or marked.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/PartySidebarView`:
      - the pointer;
      - the reserve caption, measured once, and its hairline (`UiTextures.SectionLine`);
      - the reserve opacity;
      - the scroll cues;
      - the layout leaves room for the caption: the window still holds `VisibleCards` cards, and the caption takes a card gap plus its line.
    - `Ui/UiTheme`: the reserve opacity (0.6) as a named value.
    - `content/text/ui.json`, `Ui/MessageIds`: `menu_sidebar_reserve` "Reserve".
  - **Tests:** none (draw-only). P1's tests pass unchanged.
  - **Verify by hand** (screenshots before and after):
    - [ ] Status, first member: pointer and lit card on the first card. The reserve caption and the dimmed reserve cards follow the active members.
    - [ ] Right to a reserve member: that card is full opacity, lit and pointed at. The window scrolls and a cue shows hidden cards above.
    - [ ] Compare with the examples: the marked member reads as clearly as the pointing hand does there.
- **Done, with deviations:** the caption reuses the Party page's `party_reserve` ("Reserve") through `Ui/SectionTitle` instead of a new `menu_sidebar_reserve` message, so `ui.json` and `MessageIds` don't change. `PartySidebarView` takes `ScreenText` (4 parameters); a private `CardSlot` record keeps `DrawCard` at 4.
