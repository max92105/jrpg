# ADR 0016: Intro cinematics are map cutscenes

Part of [the new-game flow plan](../README.md). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The intro as a map cutscene".

- **ADR 0016: Intro cinematics** (checkbox step before K1)
  - **Question:** is the character intro the cutscene system the roadmap expects, or something simpler?
  - **Options:** A, a slideshow content type played by its own scene (the draft's recommendation); B, map cutscenes on `EventRun`; C, video files.
  - **Done when:** `docs/decisions/0016-intro-cinematics.md` is accepted by the developer and indexed in `docs/decisions/README.md`.
  - **Done, with deviations:**
    - The developer chose **B**, map cutscenes, over the draft's recommendation of A (2026-10-23). They also chose one `intro` map with an arrival event per leader, each gated on that leader (decision 185), over one map per leader.
    - The intro phase was re-sliced accordingly: K1 (arrival activation and leader condition), K2a (the still step and skipping), K2b (the editor), K2c (the Game shows stills), K3 (the intro map), K4 (the look).
    - A1a's voice became an event step, and A1b plays it through `EventPresenter`.
    - `cinematics.json`, `CinematicScene` and `LeaderChoice.IntroCinematicId` are gone from the plan.
