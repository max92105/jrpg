# F1. Walk-through findings: description, section titles, Status, gliding pointers

Part of [the escape menu third pass plan](../README.md). Decisions 333-336. Asked for by the developer on 2026-10-27 after a first look at the finished pages, before the walk-through (Z), and done in one commit at their request rather than as separate slices.

- **F1. The item description readable and hideable, larger section titles, the Status page redone, every menu pointer gliding** (Game)
  - **Goal:** the five findings: (1) the Inventory's description strip is opaque and Info shows or hides it; (2, 3) Party's and Equipment's section titles are easy to read; (4) the Status page follows `menu-status.png`: clear sections, values close to their labels, "next" at the experience bar's end; (5) the pointer glides on every menu list, the save files included.
  - **Changes (Game):**
    - `Input/GameButton.Info`, bound to X and to a controller's Y; `Menus/ItemDetailLine` hides the description (`ToggleDescription`), a use's notice showing either way; `InventoryPage` toggles it on Info, not while aiming; `UiTheme.ItemDetailFill` (opaque) for the strip; the inventory hint names the button.
    - `Ui/SectionTitle`: a dark band (`UiTheme.SectionBand`, `Height` 36) with a 22 px white label, in place of the 18 px grey label over a hairline; it no longer needs `UiTextures`. Its users place their content from `Height`; `LeaderProfileView` keeps its own 26 px class line.
    - `Menus/StatusSheetView` and `StatusSkillsView` redone (decision 334); the Experience section title goes (`status_experience` with it): the experience sits in the profile.
    - `Ui/SelectionMotion.Follow(int, int)` and `JumpTo(int)` for lists without a `ListCursor`; `SystemPage` glides its entries and passes the time to the open save files (`ISaveFilesPage.Advance`); `CategoryRail.Selection`, `MemberColumn.Selection` (advanced by `GameMenuScene`, jumping to the first member when a page opens), `PartyCursor.Pointer`, `PartySidebar.Pointer`, and the Party page's actions glide.
  - **Tests:** `SystemPageMotionTests` (2), `ItemDetailLineTests` (+3), `InventoryPageDescriptionTests` (3), `MenuListMotionTests` (7), `GameMenuSceneMemberColumnTests` (2), `PartyPageMotionTests` (2).
  - **Verify by hand:** Inventory: the strip hides the rows under it; X hides and shows the description, a potion's notice still shows. Party, Equipment, Status: the section bands read clearly. Status against `menu-status.png`: profile, experience with "next" at the bar's end, parameters, equipment beside resources, skills. Every list: System, Save, Load, the category rail, the member column, the Party cards and actions, the sidebar while aiming: the diamond glides and bobs.
- **Done, with deviations:** Info's choice lasts while the menu is open, not the whole session as proposed: the menu is rebuilt each time it opens, and keeping it longer would take a fifth constructor parameter on `InventoryPage`. On the card lists (Party, sidebar) the diamond glides and bobs but each card keeps its own band, since a card draws its band under its text. Tests: 2262 in the solution.
