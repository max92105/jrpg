# E1. The Equipment page compares sheets

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Design: "The pages" (Equipment page), "Replacement mapping" (previews). After S2. Look at `docs/ui-reference/menu-equipment.webp` (the parameters with arrows and new values) first.

- **E1. The Equipment page compares sheets: what the highlighted item changes, or what the worn one gives** (Core + Game, about 240 lines)
  - **Goal:** the Equipment page's parameters section (HP, attack, defense with a preview) becomes a **Changes** section: with a choice open, every stat the highlighted item (or "Remove") would change, as "label, now ▸ new" colored up or down; with no choice open, what the highlighted slot's item gives (the sheet compared with that slot emptied). The Game no longer reads attack or defense after this slice and S1 (so M1 and M2b remove them without touching screens). Plain draw in today's two-column spot; E2 does the look.
  - **Decision 337 (taken while slicing):** the comparison also keeps Attack and Defense lines, read from `TotalStats` with the item on or the slot emptied, listed first; `StatsWith` and `StatsWithout` stay for them until M2b.
  - **New types:**
    - Game `Menus/StatComparison` (internal sealed record): the stats that differ between two sheets, in `StatKind` order, each with its value before and after; built by `StatComparison.Between(StatSheet before, StatSheet after)`, walking `StatKind` and reading `StatSheet.ValueOf` (P4), so it has no switch of its own.
    - Game `Menus/StatComparisonText` (internal sealed record): a comparison's lines as text, each new value colored by its direction (replaces `StatPreviewText`'s two fixed lines).
  - **Changes:**
    - Core `Party/PartyMember`: `SheetWith(ItemDefinition item)` and `SheetWithout(EquipmentSlot slot)` replace `StatsWith` and `StatsWithout` (and `StatsWearing`); `TotalStats` stays for the battle until M2b.
    - Game: `Menus/StatPreview` and `StatPreviewText` are deleted; `Menus/EquipmentPage.ShowPreview` builds the comparison; the slot cursor's moves rebuild the "gives" comparison (built when the row changes, never per frame); `Menus/EquipmentSheetView` draws up to eight lines in two columns ("+N more" past eight, which no item in this plan reaches); `EquipmentSheet.Stats` goes.
  - **Tests:**
    - `StatComparisonTests: Between_PowerBonusOn_ListsPowerAndCritDamage` (a primary's change shows with what it moves among the twenty kinds)
    - `StatComparisonTests: Between_SameSheet_IsEmpty`
    - `StatComparisonTests: Between_LowerFireResistance_IsDown`
    - `EquipmentPageTests: Update_ChoiceHighlightsArmor_ComparesItsResistances`
    - `EquipmentPageTests: Update_SlotHighlighted_ShowsWhatTheWornItemGives`
    - `PartyMemberGearTests: SheetWithout_WornArmor_LosesItsBonuses` (replaces the `StatsWithout` tests)
  - **Existing tests that change:** `StatPreviewTextTests` (deleted with its type, 2 tests), the `PartyMemberStatTests` on `StatsWith`/`StatsWithout` (about 10 `CombatStats` sites become sheet assertions), `EquipmentSheetTests` on `Stats`.
  - **Size:** Core about 20 net (two methods in, three out); presenters raw about 60 (`StatComparison` 30, `StatComparisonText` 20, page wiring 10, non-view); the view's Changes lines raw 40, at 1.6x about 65; 6 tests about 80; changed tests about 20: about 245.
  - **Watch:** `EquipmentPage.cs` (255) and `EquipmentSheetView.cs` (197); if the page passes 280, the preview state (`_preview`, `_previewedRow`, `ShowPreview`) moves to `Menus/EquipmentComparison` first.
  - **Verify by hand:** open a choice on the chest slot with chain mail and the wizard robe: the lines change as the highlight moves; "Remove" shows the losses in red; with no choice, moving between slots shows what each worn item gives; an empty slot shows nothing. F12.
- **Done, with deviations:** decision 337 (taken while slicing): the comparison keeps Attack and Defense first, from `TotalStats` with the item on or the slot emptied, so `StatsWith` and `StatsWithout` stay until M2b beside the new `SheetWith` and `SheetWithout` (all four share one `WornWith` helper; `PartyMember.cs` 274 lines). The comparison state lives in a new `Menus/EquipmentComparison`, so `EquipmentPage` stays at 275 lines. `StatComparison.Between` takes two `Numbers` (sheet and battle stats). The section is titled "Changes" (`equipment_changes`) in both modes; past eight lines, "+N more" (`equipment_more_changes`). The three `StatPreview` tests in `EquipmentChoiceTests` became `Comparison_*` tests of the same behavior; `EquipmentSheetTests.Of_WearingSword_AttackText…` went with `EquipmentSheet.Stats`. Extra tests: `StatComparisonTextTests` (4), `EquipmentPageComparisonTests.Open_EmptyWeaponSlot…`, `Update_RemoveAllHighlighted_ShowsNothing`, `PartyMemberGearTests.SheetWith_NotEquipment_Throws`. From the review: a percent stat is compared as the screen writes it (a whole percent, ADR 0026), so a change under 1 % (the wizard robe's +3 Will on Light: 2.0 % to 2.7 %) isn't listed as "2 % ▸ 2 %"; a page test removes the worn sword through the choice and sees Attack fall; `PartyMember` builds its own sheet through `SheetWearing` too, so the comparison and the sheet can't differ; the view's lines per column come from `StatComparisonText.MostLines`. Look not checked by me (machine busy): the developer's hand check. Tests: 2407 → 2420.
