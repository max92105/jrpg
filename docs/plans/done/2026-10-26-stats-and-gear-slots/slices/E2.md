# E2. Equipment page look

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decision 252 (one selection look). Backlog: "`Menus/EquipmentSheetView` draws its parameter rows the old way … Give the Equipment page the same row look when it is next touched." Design: "The pages". After G2. Look at `docs/ui-reference/menu-equipment.webp` and G1c's captures first.

- **E2. Equipment page look: the Status row look, the comparison in two columns** (Game, about 210 lines with the 1.6x)
  - **Goal:** the Equipment page reads like the Status page and the reference: slot names as small spaced-caps labels with an icon column, item names at body size, a hairline per row; the Changes section's lines as label, current value, the diamond arrow and the new value in the up or down color, right-aligned in two columns; a section caption saying which it shows ("CHANGES" with a choice open, "GIVES" with none). Behavior unchanged.
  - **New types:**
    - Game `Menus/EquipmentChangesView` (internal sealed): draws a `StatComparisonText` as the two-column Changes section, out of `EquipmentSheetView` so it stays under 250.
    - Game `Menus/SheetRowLook` (internal static): the row look S2 gave the Status page (spaced-caps label, right-aligned value, hairline), moved out of `StatusParametersView` so both pages draw rows one way.
  - **Changes:** `Menus/EquipmentSheetView`: rows through `SheetRowLook`; `Menus/StatusParametersView`: calls it instead of its own helpers (same pixels: S2's captures are the check); `content/text/ui.json`: `equipment_changes`, `equipment_gives`.
  - **Tests:** none for drawing; `EquipmentPageTests: Update_ChoiceOpens_CaptionSaysChanges` and `Update_ChoiceCloses_CaptionSaysGives` (through a read accessor on the page).
  - **Size:** raw about 120 (`EquipmentChangesView` 50, `SheetRowLook` 25 moved, sheet view 35, messages 10), at 1.6x about 185, plus 2 tests: about 210.
  - **Verify by hand:** before/after captures (F12) of the page with and without a choice; compare with `menu-equipment.webp`; the selection look is the shared one (decision 252) on the slot rows and the choice list; nothing overlaps at nine slots.
- **Done, with deviations:**
  - **`SheetRowLook` is a small class, not a static one**: it holds the two fonts (18 px spaced-caps labels, 26 px values), so each call stays under four parameters (`Draw(batch, row, Cell, opacity)`, `DrawLabel`, `DrawValue` with a `Value(text, color, right edge)`), and `StatusParametersView` draws through it with the same pixels as before (its fonts, `DrawCell`, `CellText` and `Measure` went).
  - **`EquipmentChangesView`** owns the section's height (`Height`: band, four lines, the "+N more" line) and measures a comparison once per `StatComparisonText` instance; each line is the label at the left and, right-aligned, the value before, a diamond and the value after in its color. The caption comes from the page: `EquipmentPage.ChangesCaptionId` ("Changes" with a choice open, "Gives" otherwise), the read accessor the tests use.
  - **Slot rows:** the slot label is the small spaced-caps one at the row's left, the item's icon and name stay at body size, and a hairline runs under every slot row; the separate hairline above "Remove all" went, since every row now has one and the 10 px gap keeps the action apart.
  - **Caps:** the review found the labels spaced but not uppercased (`SheetRowLook` spaces, the Status page caps its labels beforehand); the slot labels are uppercased once in `EquipmentLabels.From` and the comparison's once per comparison in `EquipmentChangesView.Measure`.
  - **Tests:** `EquipmentPageCaptionTests` 3 (a new file: `EquipmentPageTests` is at 286). Count: 2,573 → 2,576. `EquipmentSheetView` 208 → 168 lines, `StatusParametersView` 157 → 141.
  - **Hand check (yours):** before/after captures (F12) of the page with and without a choice, against `menu-equipment.webp`; the selection look is the shared one on the slot rows and the choice list; nothing overlaps at nine slots; the Status page's middle band looks exactly as before.
