# M2b-2. Defense leaves members, items and the pages

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). ADR 0027, decision 337. Design: "Replacement mapping". After M2b-1. Split from M2b on 2026-10-27.

- **M2b-2. Defense leaves members, items and the pages** (Core + Data + content + Game + tests, about 230 lines)
  - **Goal:** after this slice nothing in the game says defense, and `Attack` is `AttackDamage`.
  - **Decision 337:** the Equipment comparison's Defense line goes with defense, with `PartyMember.StatsWith` and `StatsWithout`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/CombatStats`: loses `Defense`; `Attack` is renamed `AttackDamage`. `PartyMember.BaseStats` and `TotalStats` go: `Battle/MemberBattleStats` (D1) builds the whole line from the sheet and the worn weapon.
    - `Party/ClassStats`: loses `Defense` (`ClassGrowth`, validator, `classes.json`).
    - `Items/EquipmentStats`: loses `DefenseBonus` (validator, `items.json`; the armors keep their P4 bonuses).
    - Game: `Menus/StatComparison` loses the Defense line and reads the weapon damage without `TotalStats`; `Menus/StatusSheet` and `StatusLabels` lose Defense (Combat 7 rows; `StatusParametersView`'s rows follow).
    - Text: `stat_defense`, `slot_armor` stay only if still used (grep; the Equipment page's armor label goes in G1a-1).
  - **Tests:**
    - `CombatantTests: FromPartyMember_SwordWorn_AttackDamageIsSword`
    - The `WithDefense` stat tests (`PartyMemberStatTests`, `CombatantTests`) rewritten or removed; the builders' `WithDefense` goes.
    - Game: the comparison and Status page tests without Defense.
  - **Call sites:** `new ClassStats(` about 20 (the `Defense` argument goes); `CombatStats(` constructions in tests (count); `DefenseBonus` (builder).
  - **Verify by hand:** the Status page's Combat column has seven rows; the Equipment page compares no Defense. F12.
- **Done, with deviations:**
  - **Attack damage on the sheet:** the raw attack damage lives on the sheet as `StatSheet.AttackDamage`. `MemberSheet.Of` takes the worn weapon's `int? weaponDamage`, and null means the rules' unarmed damage.
    - So `SheetWith` and `SheetWithout` preview it.
    - `StatComparison.Between` compares two sheets; its `Numbers` type is gone.
    - `StatFormula.WeaponDamage(sheet)` is the one page query.
    - `MemberBattleStats` builds the line from the sheet.
    - `SaveGameRepair` and `LeaderProfile` pass their weapon's damage (none for a new leader).
  - **Status band:** with 7 Combat rows, Resistances with Resources are the taller column (312 against 284 px). So `StatusParametersView.Height` is now the taller of the two.
  - **Tests:**
    - The stat-line tests in `PartyMemberStatTests` became sheet tests on `AttackDamage`, and the defense-only ones (weapon defense bonus, armor defense) are gone.
    - `PartyMemberTests` and `GameSessionSaveTests` keep their max HP growth checks.
    - The fixture `missing-defense-bonus.json` became `missing-slot.json`.
    - New: `MemberSheetTests.Of_WeaponWorn_AttackDamageIsTheWeapons`.
    - Count: 2,477 → 2,473.
  - **Battle:** unchanged by this slice, since M2b-1 already stopped reading defense. The goblin trio table is M2b-1's.
