# P2. Stat rules in content; Constitution raises max HP

Part of [the stats and gear slots plan](../README.md). Decisions 293, 296, 328. Design: "Tunables" (`rules.json` `stats`), "Formulas", "Core types" (members built with the rules). After P1.

- **P2. Stat rules in content; Constitution raises max HP; members are built with the rules; `Vitals.ResizeMax`** (Core + Data + content, about 250 lines)
  - **Goal:** `rules.json` gains a `stats` section; a member's max HP is their class's HP at their level raised by Constitution's percent (+1 % a point), at recruiting, restoring and every level-up.
  - **New types:**
    - Core `Stats/StatRules` (public sealed record): the conversion rates and bases `rules.json` gives; this slice has `HpPercentPer100Constitution`, P3a and P3b add theirs.
    - Core `Stats/StatFormula` (public static): the primaries-to-derived conversions, one pure place; this slice: `MaxHp(int classMaxHp, int constitution, StatRules rules)` = `classMaxHp × (100 + constitution × rate ÷ 100) ÷ 100`, in a `long`, rounded down once, with the reasoning ("a percent of a base that grows, so Constitution on gear keeps its worth", ADR 0026).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Content/GameRules`: gains `Stats` (`StatRules`); `content/rules.json` its `stats` object; `Data/Content/GameRulesValidator`: the rate at least 0, named.
    - `Stats/Vitals.ResizeMax(int maxHp)` (internal; moved here from P4 by the review, since `RestoreProgress` needs it): sets the max; HP is kept, clamped to the new max, and a living holder keeps at least 1 (decision 328). P4 calls it on gear changes too.
    - `Party/PartyMember`:
      - `Recruit(definition, characterClass, skillCatalog, StatRules rules)`; the rules are kept for level-ups (and P4's gear).
      - `Restore` (internal) goes: `GameSession.RestoreMember` recruits, then calls a new internal `RestoreProgress(progress)` (level, experience, lasting amounts, then `Vitals.ResizeMax` for the level's max and the saved HP), which keeps both methods under four parameters (design.md, "Core types").
      - `Primaries` (the class's at the level); max HP through `StatFormula.MaxHp`; `LevelUp` raises vitals by what the max gained, as now.
    - `Session/GameSession` and `Exploration/EventRun`: pass `catalog.Rules.Stats`.
    - `Data/Saving/SaveGameRepair.RepairMember`: clamps HP against `StatFormula.MaxHp` at the repaired level (P4 adds gear).
    - `Game/NewGame/LeaderProfile`: HP from `StatFormula.MaxHp` at level 1 (one line; L1 redoes the profile).
    - Test support: `tests/Jrpg.Core.Tests/Builders/TestStatRules.Neutral` (every rate 0: Constitution adds nothing, so existing HP expectations hold; P3a adds base accuracy 100 and base crit 0); `PartyMemberBuilder` recruits then restores progress.
  - **Tests** (new ones in `PartyMemberStatTests`, `StatFormulaTests`, `VitalsTests`):
    - `StatFormulaTests: MaxHp_TwentyConstitution_AddsTwentyPercent`
    - `StatFormulaTests: MaxHp_ZeroConstitution_IsClassHp`
    - `VitalsTests: ResizeMax_BelowHp_ClampsHp`
    - `VitalsTests: ResizeMax_LivingAtOneHpToOne_KeepsOne` (and the other side: `ResizeMax_Fallen_StaysAtZero`)
    - `PartyMemberStatTests: Recruit_Constitution_RaisesMaxHp`
    - `PartyMemberStatTests: GainExperience_LevelUp_MaxHpGrowsWithConstitution`
    - `GameRulesValidatorTests: Validate_NegativeHpPercentPerConstitution_NamesTheField`
  - **Call sites:** `PartyMember.Recruit(` about 40 (Core tests 25 through `TestStatRules.Neutral`; Game tests' `BattleScenarios`, `CommandMenuTests`, `MemberColumnTests`, `StartMapScenarios`, Data's `ContentCatalogLoaderTests` and the two simulations through `catalog.Rules.Stats`); `PartyMember.Restore(` 5. Count them in the Done note.
  - **Size:** about 60 Core, 30 Data and content, 8 tests (about 105), 45 call-site lines: about 250. If it grows past 260, `Vitals.ResizeMax` and its three tests go first as their own slice.
  - **Watch:** `PartyMember.cs` (241) after this slice; P3a and P4 add to it.
