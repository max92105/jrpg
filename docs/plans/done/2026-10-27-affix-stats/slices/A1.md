# A1. Lifesteal

Part of [the affix stats plan](../README.md). Decisions 294, 321, 346, 365; ADR 0030. First slice of the plan. Builds on design.md's "Core changes" and "Key decisions".

- **A1. Lifesteal** (Core + Data + content + Game, about 250 lines: about 90 of code, 160 of tests)
  - **Goal:** a member with lifesteal heals by its share of the HP each landed hit of their attacks and damaging skills took, rounded down, within max HP; never from an item (decision 321), never while fallen. The battle shows a heal number on them. Gear gets it only as the `life_steal` affix; a base item listing it is refused at load.
  - **New types:**
    - `Battle/LifeStolen(Combatant Actor, int Amount)`: the event of an actor healed by their hit; only when the amount is above 0.
    - `EffectLanding.HitSource(Combatant Actor, int LifeStealPermille)`: private nested record, who lands the hits and what their hits carry (A2 adds `PenetrationPermille`).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Stats/StatKind.LifeSteal`, appended after `DarkResistance`. The summary drops its count: "The stats a sheet shows and gear can add (ADR 0026): the five primaries, the derived numbers, one resistance per damage type, then the stats only affixes give (`StatKinds.AffixOnly`, ADR 0030)".
    - `Stats/StatKinds.AffixOnly` (`ImmutableArray<StatKind>`, `[LifeSteal]`; each later slice appends its kind): the stats only affixes give, in the order screens list them.
    - `Stats/StatSheet.LifeStealPermille` (init, 0) and a private `AffixStatOf(StatKind kind)` switch (`LifeSteal` arm; default `Primaries.ValueOf(kind)`), called from `DerivedOrPrimary`'s default arm, which stays at 8 arms. Without it the Equipment comparison throws (`StatComparison.Between` reads every kind).
    - `Stats/CombatStats.LifeStealPermille` (init, 0); `Party/MemberSheet.Of` sets the sheet's from the worn bonus (`PermilleOf`); `Battle/MemberBattleStats.Of` copies it.
    - `Effects/DamageFormula.LifeStolen(int hpTaken, int permille)`: `hpTaken × permille ÷ 1000` in a `long`, rounded down, with the reasoning comment (a share of what the hit really took, so overkill steals nothing; per hit, so a small hit can steal 0, decision 365).
    - `Battle/EffectLanding`: `Land` builds a `HitSource` from the actor's `CurrentStats`; a new `LandFromItem(Combatant actor, Effect effect, IReadOnlyList<Combatant> targets, List<BattleEvent> events)` builds one with 0 (decision 321). Both share the target and hit loops, which take the `HitSource` instead of the actor, so they stay at four parameters with no flag argument. After a landed damaging hit, a living actor heals `LifeStolen` through `Vitals.RestoreHp` (which returns what it restored) and `LifeStolen` is reported when that is above 0. The class summary gains the lifesteal sentence.
    - `Battle/BattleState.UseItem` calls `LandFromItem` (one line).
    - `Game/Battle/ActionPlayback.Read`: `LifeStolen` adds a heal number (`FloatingNumberTone.Heal`) on the actor in the impact beat.
    - `Data/Items/ItemCatalogValidator.FindBonusProblem`: a kind in `StatKinds.AffixOnly` is refused: "equipment.bonuses.lifeSteal is a stat only affixes give (ADR 0030)."
    - Text: `stat_life_steal` ("Lifesteal") in `content/text/ui.json`, `MessageIds.StatLifeSteal`, a row in `Ui/StatNames` (its summary: "an arm per stat would pass the build's complexity limit", no count).
    - Content: `affixes.json` `life_steal` (`lifeSteal`, weapon and ring, 8-15, weight 10; decision 365).
    - Docs: `docs/map.md`'s Stats line drops "the twenty stats" for "the stats a sheet shows and gear adds, the affix-only ones last (`StatKinds.AffixOnly`)"; its Battle line names `LifeStolen` and `LandFromItem`.
