# ADR 0029: Gear pieces

Part of [the loot plan](../README.md). Before G1a. Decisions 339, 344, 345. Extends ADR 0003 (equipping moves the copy) and ADR 0028 (slots); bumps the save format under ADR 0022.

- [x] **Write ADR 0029** from the README's "ADRs" entry, option A (the developer chose it when approving the plan, so there is nothing left to ask), with B, C and D as the alternatives.
  - **Records (A):**
    - `Items/GearPiece` is one piece of gear, owned or worn: a base item (equipment), a rarity and its rolled affixes in order; equal when all three are. `GearPiece.Plain(item)` is a Common piece without affixes.
    - `Items/Rarity`: Common, Magic, Rare, Unique (reserved: never rolled in the loot plan). Material is the materials' tier for color only, not a gear rarity (decision 339).
    - The inventory keeps stacks for consumables and materials (`Entries`) and a list of pieces for gear (`Gear`), one row per piece, no cap and no room rule (decision 345). `Inventory.Add(item, quantity)` turns equipment into plain pieces. `QuantityOf` and `TryRemove` stay stacks-only; `GearCountOf(itemId)` counts a base's pieces.
    - Members wear pieces; `PartyEquipment` moves pieces (ADR 0003's move model: a worn piece is not in the inventory, and keeps its rolls wherever it goes).
    - A rolled affix carries its stat at runtime (`RolledAffix`); a save keeps only its id and value (`SavedAffix`), the stat resolved from content on load (ADR 0011).
    - Saves keep each piece as `SavedGear` (base id, rarity, affixes), worn by slot and owned in a `gear` list: format 5, older formats refused (ADR 0022).
  - **Consequences:** fixed-stat items, starting items and map gifts keep working as plain pieces; two plain swords are interchangeable; an item level can join the piece later as one more field (decision 341).
