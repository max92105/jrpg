# D3. The victory screen lists the drops

Part of [the loot plan](../README.md). Decision 355. Design: "Pages" (Victory). After D2. Reference: `docs/ui-reference/battle-victory.jpg` (the rewards; the reference shows items on the step after "Next").

- **D3. The victory screen lists the drops** (Game, raw 140, about 220 lines at 1.6x: a new view type)
  - **Goal:** after the experience bars fill, Confirm shows an "Items" step listing what dropped (name, ×quantity for a stack); Confirm there leaves the battle. A victory without drops leaves on the first Confirm after the bars, as today.
  - **New types:**
    - `Battle/VictoryDropRow(string Name, string IconFile, MeasuredText? Quantity)`: one dropped thing as the Items step shows it, formatted once (the quantity null for a piece).
    - `Battle/VictoryDropsView`: draws the Items step: the serif "Items" title and the rows, in the victory screen's band look (plain here; P1b tints rarity).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Battle/VictoryScreen(BattleSpoils spoils)`: `Rewards` reads `spoils.Rewards`; `IsShowingDrops`; Confirm after the bars shows the drops when `spoils.Drops` isn't empty, otherwise returns `BattleFinished`; Confirm on the drops returns `BattleFinished` once. `BattleScene` passes the spoils.
    - `Battle/VictoryScreenView`: draws `VictoryDropsView` instead of the cards while `IsShowingDrops` (a call, about 10 lines; 249 now).
    - Text: `victory_items` ("Items"); `MessageIds.VictoryItems`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `VictoryScreenTests: Update_ConfirmAfterBarsWithDrops_ShowsDrops`
    - `VictoryScreenTests: Update_ConfirmOnDrops_LeavesBattle`
    - `VictoryScreenTests: Update_ConfirmAfterBarsWithoutDrops_LeavesBattle`
    - `VictoryScreenTests: Update_ConfirmWhileBarsFill_DoesNotShowDrops` (the guard: Confirm skips the filling only)
    - `VictoryDropsViewTests: RowsOf_StackAndPiece_StackHasQuantityPieceNone`
  - **Call sites:** `new VictoryScreen(` in `VictoryScreenTests` (3) wrap their rewards in `new BattleSpoils(rewards, Drops.None)`; `BattleScene` 1.
  - **Watch:** `VictoryScreenView.cs` 249: if wiring passes 270, move the cards' drawing into a `VictoryCardsView` first (note it in the Done section).
  - **Verify by hand:** win against the goblin trio until something drops: after the bars, Confirm shows "Items" with the drops; Confirm returns to the map. A victory with no drop goes straight back.
- **Note from D1:** `Drops.None` was left out of D1 (no user there); add it here if the victory screen's tests need an empty `Drops`.
- **Done, with deviations:**
  - **Look:** "Items" in the title serif (56 px) where the cards start, then each drop on the cards' pointed name tab (icon, name, "×n" for a stack) in the cards' two columns, 7 rows each down to the footer's hairline.
