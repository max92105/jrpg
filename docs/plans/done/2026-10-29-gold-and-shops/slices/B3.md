# B3. Category tabs on both lists, and the column titles

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 384 (the Inventory's categories in the Shop, the tab buttons with Q and R on the keyboard), 51 (the category rail's icons). References: `docs/ui-reference/shop-sell.webp` (the icon rail beside the list, the column titles), `menu-inventory-description.webp`. After B2. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Structure and folder dependencies" (`Trade` → `Menus`), "Replacement mapping" (`InventoryFilter.Shows`).

- **B3. Category tabs on both lists, and the column titles** (Game + text, about 190 lines; reuses `CategoryRailView`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** both lists show the Inventory's category rail at their left (All, Consumables, Weapons, Armor, Materials, with their icons) and only the chosen category's rows; the tab buttons (`PreviousTab`/`NextTab`: the shoulders, and the new keyboard keys Q and R) change it from the list, the cursor going to the first row. Each tab (Buy, Sell) keeps its own category. Column titles over the list: Item, Owned, Price.
  - **New types:** none.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Menus/InventoryFilter.Shows(InventoryCategory category, ItemDefinition item)` (the private `Keeps`, made internal for its second user; `SelectStacks`/`SelectPieces` call it).
    - `Trade/StockList` and `Trade/SaleList`: `Refresh(InventoryCategory category)` keeps the rows `InventoryFilter.Shows`.
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: one `Menus/CategoryRail` per tab; the tab buttons move it in the list.
    - `Trade/ShopListView`: the rail through `Menus/CategoryRailView` at the column's left (56 px; the rows shift right), the `Ui/ColumnTitles` row (B1's arithmetic holds the 36 px).
    - `Input/InputBindings`: `Keys.Q` → `PreviousTab`, `Keys.R` → `NextTab` (the keyboard had none; the Inventory, Equipment and Status pages gain them too, which do the same as their controller shoulders).
    - Text `shop_column_item`, `shop_column_owned`, `shop_column_price`, the footer hint names the categories' buttons; `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `StockListTests: Refresh_WeaponsCategory_ShowsOnlyWeapons`
    - `SaleListTests: Refresh_MaterialsCategory_ShowsOnlyMaterials`
    - `ShopSceneCategoryTests: NextTab_InTheList_ChangesTheCategoryAndGoesToTheFirstRow` (new file)
    - `ShopSceneCategoryTests: NextTab_OnSell_LeavesTheBuyCategory`
    - `InputBindingsTests: KeyBindings_Q_IsPreviousTab` and `KeyBindings_R_IsNextTab` (new file: no bindings test exists)
    - Existing: `InventoryFilterTests` pass unchanged.
  - **Watch:** an empty category shows the empty line, with no cursor. `ButtonLabels` reads the new keys for the footer.
  - **Verify by hand:** at the shopkeeper: R moves the rail to Consumables, then Weapons (the rolled sword), Armor; Q back; on Sell, Materials shows Scrap greyed; Buy kept its category.
- **Done, with deviations:** the rail is the Inventory's full `CategoryRailView` (72 px, not 56), so the rows' right-anchored positions (the owned count 200 px before the right edge, the price at it) replaced the fixed 760 and 960. The rail never takes the cursor (the tab buttons move it from the list), so its chosen band shows dimmed as the Inventory draws a rail without the cursor. `Refresh(category)` puts the cursor on the first row; `Refresh()` keeps it. `ButtonLabelsTests.Of_TabButtonOnKeyboard_ThrowsNamingIt` became two facts for Q and R (the keyboard has the keys now, question 92). Added `PreviousTab_OnAll_WrapsToMaterials` and `NextTab_OnTheTabs_ChangesNothing`. The review turned the category change into `TryChangeCategory(input)` (no flag argument), made the first-row tests able to fail (a moved cursor before the refresh; the first consumable named), shared one `Rebuild(selected)` between the two refreshes, and the footer now names the tab buttons for the device in use (`ShopView.ShowDevice`, `ButtonLabels`).
