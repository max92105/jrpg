# H1. A Shop screen in the menu's frame

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 353 (a screen an NPC opens over the map), 370 (it fades itself in), 380 (gold with its coin). References: `docs/ui-reference/shop-sell.webp` (header with the shop's name top left and the gold top right, the map dimmed behind, the footer hint). ADR 0008. After L1. The forge plan's F1a and F1d are the model. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Structure and folder dependencies" (Game: `Trade` → `Menus` for the frame), "Risks" (CA1812).

- **H1. A Shop screen in the menu's frame that fades in over the map and Cancel closes** (Game + text, raw 120, about 190 lines at 1.6x: adds a view type)
  - **Goal:** a Shop screen in the look it keeps: the map dimmed behind it (`UiTheme.Scrim`), a `HeaderBar` with the shop's name at top left and the party's gold (`GoldAmountView`) at top right, the empty page and detail columns (`ColumnPanelView`, at the Forge's places), a `FooterBar` with its hint. It fades in through `Ui/OverlayFade`; Cancel starts the fade out and `EventScreenClosed` comes once it has run, once. Nothing lists yet (B1), and nothing opens it in play until E1 (known gap: tests only).
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Trade/ShopScene`: the Shop screen's input and its close request (an `IScene`). `ShopScene(ScreenText text, UiTextures ui, GameSession session, Shop shop)`.
    - `Game/Trade/ShopView`: draws the Shop's frame: scrim, header (name, gold measured when it changes), the two columns, footer; the opacity from the scene.
  - **Changes:**
    - Text `shop_hint` (the footer's line, placeholder; B2, B3 and L2b extend it as their buttons arrive); `MessageIds`.
  - **Pixel arithmetic** (1920 x 1080, the menu's frame as the Forge reads it: header 88 (`MenuHeader.Height`), body from 128 to 976 (`MenuLayout.BodyHeight`, 848), footer 72 from 1008): the list column x 40 to 1000, the detail column x 1040 to 1880 (the Forge's); the gold right-aligned 48 px from the right edge, centered in the header as `MenuHeader` centers its text.
  - **Tests** (`ShopSceneTests`, new file; a session from the repository catalog and `ShopFor("general_store")`):
    - `Update_Cancel_RequestsCloseOnceTheFadeHasRun` (Cancel, then updates through 0.12 s; one `EventScreenClosed`, then null)
    - `Update_Confirm_RequestsNothing`
    - Draw-only: before and after captures (F12) once N1 reaches it.
  - **Watch:** CA1812: `SceneFactory.CreateShop` lands in E1, its first caller; if the build flags `ShopScene` here, it lands in this slice. The look must be the Forge's and the menu's (one frame): ask before departing from both.
  - **Verify by hand:** none until N1 (no way in yet); then capture with F12 and compare with `shop-sell.webp`.
- **Done, with deviations:** CA1812 didn't flag `ShopScene`, so `SceneFactory.CreateShop` stays in E1. `ShopView.ShowGold(gold)` is fed by the scene each update and formats only on a change, as `MenuHeader` does. The frame is `ForgeView`'s a second time; sharing it waits for `MenuLayout`'s numbers to live in `Ui/` (backlog). Not captured (nothing opens the Shop yet). Tests 3030 → 3032.
