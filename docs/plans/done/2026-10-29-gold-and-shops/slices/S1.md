# S1. Buying stacks in Core

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 377 (the picker's limit is Core's), 385 (stacks are never rolled). After G4. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (quotes; `Shop` buys, `ItemSales` sells; refusal order), "Structure and folder dependencies".

- **S1. Buying stacks in Core: prices, `Shop`, quotes with a quantity** (Core, about 220 lines)
  - **Goal:** an item may have a price; a shop's stock holds the stacks it sells. Each purchase of a quantity is quoted first: its total, and why it's refused (the stack can't take that many, or the purse is short); `MostBuyable` says how many the gold and the stack's room allow (the picker's limit, decision 377). Buying takes the total and adds the copies. A refused purchase, or an item the stock doesn't hold, changes nothing. Pieces come in S2, content in S4.
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Trade/ShopDefinition(string Id, string Name, IReadOnlyList<string> ItemIds)`: public sealed record; one shop as content describes it: its name and what it sells, in order.
    - `Core/Trade/ShopStock`: public sealed class; one shop's goods as the party finds them. Here `ShopStock(ShopDefinition definition, IReadOnlyList<ItemDefinition> stacks)`; `Definition`, `Stacks` (S2 adds the rolled pieces).
    - `Core/Trade/TradeRefusal`: public enum; why a trade is refused: `StackFull`, `ShortOfGold` (S3 adds `NotForSale`).
    - `Core/Trade/TradeQuote(int Gold, TradeRefusal? Refusal)`: public sealed record; one trade as offered: the gold it costs or gives, and its refusal (null when it can be done); `bool IsRefused`.
    - `Core/Trade/Shop`: public sealed class, internal constructor; buying from one shop's stock. `Shop(ShopStock stock, Inventory inventory, Purse purse)` (S2 adds `Pricing`); `string Name`; `ShopStock Stock`; `TradeQuote QuoteBuy(ItemDefinition item, int quantity)`; `int MostBuyable(ItemDefinition item)` (0 when not even one can be bought); `bool TryBuy(ItemDefinition item, int quantity)`.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Items/ItemDefinition.Price` (`int?` init): what it costs in a shop; null is never bought or sold.
  - **Tests** (`ShopBuyTests`, new file; a stock of two stacks from `ItemDefinitionBuilder`):

  - **Call sites:** none.
  - **Watch:** `Shop`'s constructor reaches four parameters in S2; a fifth means it does too much. Core trusts validated content for a stocked item's price (`item.Price ?? throw` naming the item: a content bug, S4 checks it).
  - **Verify by hand:** none (Core only).
