# S3. Selling in Core

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 375 (a sale pays the share of the whole price), 377 (the picker's limit), 378 (anything with a price sells), 369 (owned pieces only). After S2. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions", README "Planner's picks" (one overflow rule).

- **S3. Selling in Core: the sell share, `ItemSales`** (Core, about 235 lines)
  - **Goal:** an owned priced item sells for the sell share (`rules.json` `trade.sellPercent`, S4) of its price, rounded down: a quantity of a stack, or one owned piece (worn ones aren't owned) at the share of its base price plus its affixes' values. Each sale is quoted first; not for sale refuses (no price, or a share worth 0). `MostSellable` is what is owned (0 when not for sale). Gold past the purse's cap is lost (G1's rule; no refusal). Selling is the same at every shop, so it lives on the session.
  - **New types:**
    - `Core/Trade/TradeRules`: public sealed record; `rules.json` `trade`: `int SellPercent { get; init; }` (0 by default: every sale refused, as empty crafting rules refuse every action).
    - `Core/Trade/ItemSales`: public sealed class, internal constructor; selling owned items for gold. `ItemSales(Inventory inventory, Purse purse, Pricing pricing)`; `TradeQuote QuoteSale(ItemDefinition item, int quantity)`, `int MostSellable(ItemDefinition item)`, `bool TrySell(ItemDefinition item, int quantity)`; `TradeQuote QuoteSale(GearPiece piece)`, `bool TrySell(GearPiece piece)` (the first equal owned piece).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/TradeRefusal.NotForSale`.
    - `Trade/PriceFormula.SalePrice(int price, int sellPercent)`: `price × sellPercent / 100` in a `long`, rounded down once (comment: the shop keeps the rest, the classic JRPG half).
    - `Trade/Pricing(IReadOnlyDictionary<string, int> affixValues, TradeRules rules)`: `int SalePriceOf(ItemDefinition item)`, `int SalePriceOf(GearPiece piece)` (0 for an unpriced item).
    - `Content/GameRules.Trade { get; init; } = new()` (init, as `Crafting`, so no constructor site changes); its summary names it.
    - `Session/GameSession`: builds one `Pricing` (affix values from the catalog's affixes, the rules' trade); `Sales`.
