# S6. The shops' stocks saved, restored and repaired

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 385 (a saved stock piece with an unknown base or affix is dropped and reported), 360 (an affix out of range is clamped). ADR 0007 (repair against changed content). After S5 (G2 landed the shape). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Risks" (save format).

- **S6. The shops' stocks saved, restored and repaired** (Core + Data, about 180 lines)
  - **Goal:** a save keeps every opened shop's stock (its pieces and the leader level they were rolled at); a load restores them, so the shop shows the same pieces and doesn't reroll until the leader gains a level. Against changed content, a stock of a shop that no longer exists is dropped, a piece whose base is gone or no longer equipment, or that has an affix the pool no longer has, is dropped, and an affix value out of range is clamped; each change is reported.
  - **New types:**
    - `Data/Saving/ShopStockRepair`: the shops' half of `SaveGameRepair`: drops what no longer fits, clamps the rest, reports each change.
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/ShopStock.ToSaved()`; `Session/ShopStocks.Capture()` and `Restore(IReadOnlyList<SavedShopStock> saved)` (pieces through `ContentCatalog.PieceFrom`, stacks resolved from the shop's list).
    - `Session/GameSession.Capture` writes `ShopStocks = _stocks.Capture()`; `Restore` restores them.
    - `Data/Saving/SaveGameRepair`: `ShopStocks = ShopStockRepair…`; `GearRepair`'s affix clamping becomes internal and shared (one rule for owned and stock pieces).
  - **Tests:**
    - `GameSessionShopSaveTests: Capture_OpenedShop_SavesItsPiecesAndLevel` (new file)
    - `GameSessionShopSaveTests: Restore_SavedStock_ShopShowsTheSamePiecesWithoutDrawing`
    - `GameSessionShopSaveTests: Restore_LeaderAboveTheSavedLevel_RollsAgainOnOpening`
    - `ShopStockRepairTests: Repair_UnknownShop_DropsTheStock` (new file)
    - `ShopStockRepairTests: Repair_UnknownBase_DropsThePiece`
    - `ShopStockRepairTests: Repair_BaseNoLongerEquipment_DropsThePiece`
    - `ShopStockRepairTests: Repair_UnknownAffix_DropsThePiece`
    - `ShopStockRepairTests: Repair_AffixOutOfRange_ClampsIt`
  - **Call sites:** none.
  - **Watch:** `SaveGameRepair` 221 (+3), `GameSession` about 271 (+3).
  - **Verify by hand:** after H1 and N1 (the shopkeeper): open the shop, save, load, open it again: the same pieces.
- **Done, with deviations:** a stock piece with an unknown affix is dropped whole (decision 385), unlike an owned piece, which loses only the affix; `GearRepair.RepairAffixes` became internal and clamps the kept stock pieces, so the clamp has one rule. `ShopStocks` restores through `ContentCatalog.PieceFrom` and shares `StacksOf` between a roll and a restore. G2's stored-save fixture named a shop "general" that the repair now drops: it names `general_store`. Added `Capture_NoShopOpened_SavesNoStock` and `Repair_KnownStock_KeepsIt`. Tests 3015 → 3025.
