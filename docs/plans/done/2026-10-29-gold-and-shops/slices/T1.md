# T1. Buying a stock piece

Part of [the gold and shops plan](../README.md). Decisions 370 (a refused action greyed with its reason), 379 (buying goes at once, with a notice), 380 (notices name the item, the header shows the gold), 385 (a bought piece leaves the stock). After L2b. The forge plan's F2a and F2c are the model. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Key decisions" (quotes, refusal order).

- **T1. Buying a stock piece; a refused row's reason; the notice** (Game + text, about 190 lines; drawn by `ShopDetailView`, adds no view type)
  - **Goal:** on the Buy list, the highlighted refused row's reason shows in the detail column ("Not enough gold.", "You can't carry more."). Confirm on an available piece buys it at once through `Shop.TryBuy(piece)`: the gold in the header drops, the piece leaves the Buy list and joins the Sell list, every row's quote is asked again (a purchase can grey others), the cursor stays on the row (or the last), and a notice says what was bought ("Bought: Magic Bronze Sword.") until the cursor moves or the tab changes. Confirm on a greyed row does nothing. Stacks are bought through the picker (T2; Confirm on a stack does nothing until then).
  - **New types:**
    - `Game/Trade/TradeRefusalNames`: each `TradeRefusal`'s message id, as `Crafting/ForgeRefusalNames` (`static string IdOf(TradeRefusal refusal)`).
  - **Changes:**
    - `Trade/ShopScene`: Confirm on a Buy piece; `Notice` (string?), cleared on a move, a category or a tab change; after a purchase `StockList.Refresh`, `SaleList.Refresh` and the header's gold.
    - `Trade/ShopDetailView`: the reason line for a refused row, or the notice.
    - Text `shop_refused_short_of_gold`, `shop_refused_stack_full`, `shop_refused_not_for_sale` (all three, so the names test covers every refusal), `shop_bought` ("Bought: {name}."); `MessageIds`.
  - **Tests:**
    - `ShopSceneBuyTests: Confirm_AffordablePiece_BuysItAndTakesItsPrice` (new file)
    - `ShopSceneBuyTests: Confirm_PieceShortOfGold_ChangesNothing` (the guard: Core refuses, no notice)
    - `ShopSceneBuyTests: Selected_ShortOfGold_ShowsTheReason`
    - `ShopSceneBuyTests: Confirm_BoughtPiece_LeavesTheBuyListAndJoinsTheSellList`
    - `ShopSceneBuyTests: Down_AfterBuying_ClearsTheNotice`
    - `TradeRefusalNamesTests: IdOf_EveryRefusal_HasItsOwnLine` (new file)
  - **Watch:** `ShopScene` size (B2's watch).
  - **Verify by hand:** at the shopkeeper with 100 G: buy the rolled cap: the header's gold drops by its price, the cap leaves the Buy list and shows on Sell, "Bought: …"; buy until short: the dear rows grey, and highlighting one says "Not enough gold."; Confirm does nothing. Leave and talk again: the cap is still gone (the stock is kept).

- **Done, with deviations:** `ShopDetailView` already drew one line under the row, so it only changed its doc: `ShopScene.Notice` picks the highlighted row's refusal line over the trade's notice (`_tradeNotice`, cleared on a move, a category or a tab change). The scene's tests set the purse before the Shop opens, since rows quote their refusal when built, and check the piece by reference: the bag can hold a plain copy equal to it by value.
