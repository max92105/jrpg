# P2. The music plays

Part of [the music and sound effects plan](../README.md). After P1. ADR 0035 (preloading). Design: [design.md](../design.md), "Which MonoGame types carry music", "Key decisions" 1 and 8.

- **P2. The music plays on the device: looped, at the music volume, cut on a change; the themes preloaded** (Game, about 110 lines; device code, no view type)
  - **Goal:** the host plays the music channel's track on a loop at the Music volume (a change in Options is heard at once); a new track stops the old one and starts from its beginning (a cut, decision 416); null stops it. The title and battle themes are loaded at startup, so the flash doesn't stall on a load. Without a device, or with a file that won't load, it is silent (ADR 0018). The pause and resume around a battle come in P3.
  - **New types:**
    - `Audio/MusicPlayer(SoundLibrary sounds)` (`IDisposable`): plays on the device the track the `MusicChannel` asks for. `Follow(MusicChannel music, float gain)`: a different file stops and disposes the instance playing and starts the new one with `IsLooped = true`; `Volume = gain` on every call; the same file plays on.
  - **Changes:**
    - `JrpgGame.StartAudio`: builds `_musicPlayer` beside `_voicePlayer`, and loads `UiSoundFiles.Music.Title` and `.Battle` through `_sounds.Get(AudioFolders.Music, ...)` (a comment says why, as `LoadContent`'s menu background has); `Update`: `_musicPlayer?.Follow(_audio.Music, VolumeLevel.GainOf(_settings.MusicVolume))`; `Dispose` disposes it. The comment over `_sounds` names the music too.
    - `Audio/SoundLibrary`'s summary: it opens music files as well.
  - **Tests:** none can fail headless: `MusicPlayer` only touches the device, and the rule it follows (`MusicSelection`) is P1's. The checks are by hand.
  - **Verify by hand:**
    - The title theme loops on the title (listen past 4 s: no gap or click at the loop) and through the leader choice.
    - A new game: the town track at black; through the door to `interior_1`, it plays on, not restarted; to the forest: the forest track.
    - A random battle: the battle theme cuts in at the flash with no stall; the forest track comes back when the victory screen opens (from its start, until P3; B4 puts the fanfare first).
    - Options, Music to 0: silent; back to 7: heard at once.
    - Start with the sound device disabled in Windows: the game runs silent, one line in the Output window.
  - **Done, with deviations:** as planned; the host's audio lines move to `PlayWhatTheChannelsAsk`, since `Update` crossed the complexity limit. Not heard (unattended loop): the hand check is yours, and nothing in this slice can fail headless.
