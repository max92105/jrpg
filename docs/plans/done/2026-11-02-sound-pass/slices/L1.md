# L1. Levels evened out across every sound; the sounds still vetoed replaced

Part of [the sound pass plan](../README.md). After V1. Decision 437. Design: [design.md](../design.md), "The pipeline" ("Finding a sound"), "Risks" (loudness).

- **L1. Levels evened out across every sound; the sounds still vetoed replaced** (content, hand check only; a manifest line or two per sound changed)
  - **Goal:** each phase's listening already settled its sounds' choice; L1 makes them sit together: the levels across screens, and any veto left open at a phase's end.
  - **How:**
    1. The developer plays the route below and lists, per file: too loud or quiet (a `gainDb`), too long (a `maxSeconds` or `fadeOutSeconds`), late (a `startSeconds`), or still vetoed.
    2. Level and length changes: the manifest line, then `fetch-placeholder-sounds.ps1 -Only <file>`, `CREDITS.md` rewritten with it. A still vetoed sound: a shortlist of three page links to play in the browser; the developer's pick replaces the entry (with today's `fetched`).
    3. The developer listens again to the changed ones; repeat until the list is empty.
  - **Route:** title and menus (move, confirm, cancel, typing); the field (a chest, the door and the forest edge, a recruit, `placeholder_errand` started and completed, the inn); the Shop (tabs, a list, the picker, a purchase, a sale) and the Forge's salvage; a battle with its flash, hits, a critical, a miss, a status, a heal, a fall, a skill of each family, a victory with a level-up and drops; a run; a defeat.
  - **Tests:** none new; `verify.ps1` passes after each rerun (`SoundSourcesTests` catches a manifest edit without its credits).
  - **Verify by hand:** the route with nothing left on the list. The Done section names the files changed and why, so the next plan that adds sounds knows the developer's taste.
- **Done:** the developer played the route on 2026-11-02 and kept every sound as it is: no veto, no level or length change, so no manifest line or file changed. The freesound picks of P1 to V1, with their `gainDb`, `maxSeconds` and `startSeconds`, are the developer's accepted taste for the next sounds.
